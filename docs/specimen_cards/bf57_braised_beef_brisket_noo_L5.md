PREP
Weigh 350 g braised beef brisket noodles, separating 220 g solids (beef + noodles) from 130 g broth. Place solids in consumer blender; pulse 6-8 s until coarsely minced. Gauge the mince against the 4 mm sieve instead of screening it (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the particles fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap of the spoon back. Do not tip the batch onto the sieve and press it through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Discard retained chunks that will not pass the gauge. Return mince to broth, stir to combine. If the mixture pools thin liquid on standing after 30 s, stir in thickener at 0.5 g per 100 ml of mixture incrementally until the per-level note is satisfied. Final target: ~350 g specimen, particles ≤4 mm, moist, no separate thin liquid, mashable with a fork. Per-level note from the food list is achievable.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into 15 mm of specimen on a flat plate; specimen must squash and NOT recover its shape when fork is lifted — pass if it stays deformed. Spoon tilt: place 5 ml on a spoon and tilt to 45 degrees; specimen must hold shape and slide off slowly as a cohesive mass, not pour — pass if it does not run. Fork drip: not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: coarse mince with visible noodle and beef flecks, all pieces ≤4 mm, glossy with absorbed broth, no standing liquid in the well. On the spoon: one cohesive mound holding its shape, dull-moist sheen, no separate thin broth pooling underneath. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): lift forkful and hold 5 s above plate; must not drip continuous flow — pass if ≤2 drops fall.

FAIL MODES
Visible chunks >4 mm after sieve — re-pulse 3 s and re-sieve. Thin broth separates and pools on the plate within 30 s — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, re-test. Specimen recovers shape under fork — too dry or over-thickened; stir in 10 ml warm broth and re-test.
