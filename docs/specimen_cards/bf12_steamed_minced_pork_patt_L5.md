PREP
Weigh 150 g seasoned minced pork onto the scale. Steam until just cooked through, ~8-10 min; cool 2 min. Empty into a bowl, juices included. Chop with the fork against the bowl wall until no piece visibly exceeds 4 mm. Press the mass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard what stays on mesh and note the retained mass. If dry to the eye, fold in 5-10 ml of the reserved steaming liquid. Do not blend. No thickener for L5.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate; it must squash, the tines leave a clear dent, and the surface does not rebound when the fork lifts. Spoon tilt: a heaped teaspoon tilts to 45°; specimen holds shape, no flow, no drip. Fork drip: lift fork with a loaded scoop and hold 5 s above the plate; nothing drops. L5 needs fork-drip and fork-pressure pass; spoon-tilt is supportive. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: cohesive clump that sits as one lump with no crumbs falling off. On the spoon: mound holds its peak, surface slightly glossy from juices, no separate liquid pooling on the plate. Color is uniform pale grey-brown with no pink pork visible, confirming the chop reached every particle.

FAIL MODES
Visible particle >4 mm after sieving: re-chop that portion and re-sieve. Specimen rebounds under fork pressure (under-steamed or too firm): re-steam 3 min and retest. Dry, crumbly mass: fold in another 5 ml cooking liquid; if still dry, the batch is too lean, re-form with 10% added liquid and re-shoot.
