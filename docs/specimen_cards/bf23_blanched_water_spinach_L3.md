1. PREP
Weigh 200 g blanched water spinach (drained). Blend on high 45-60 s until smooth. Add 60 ml water; blend 15 s. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard fibrous residue. Weigh the sieved purée; for every 100 ml, add 2.0 g thickener (this is the L3 calibration starting point — record the actual grams used). Stir 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 10 s. If still too runny, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml and re-mix; if too stiff, add 5 ml water per 100 ml. Record final thickener g/100 ml and total volume.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork from cup; purée flows in a continuous slow ribbon and drips through the tines within 5-10 s — passes if it drips, fails if it pours in a stream or sits.
Fork pressure: press fork tines into 1 cm of purée; tines leave a clear imprint that does not level out; surface holds the mark.
Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon to about 30°; purée slides off slowly in one dollop, not a pour, not a stack.
Syringe flow (L3 confirmation): fill the 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release for 10 s; pass = 8-10 ml remains in the barrel.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: glossy green, even colour, no leaf flecks or fibres, smooth ribbon between tines. On the spoon: one cohesive mound that slides as a single lump. No watery halo around the specimen.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin — drips in a steady stream or <8 ml remains in syringe: add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest, re-test.
Too thick — sits on fork or ≥10 ml stays in syringe with no movement: whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml, re-test.
Visible fibres or specks on the sieve residue: re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if fibres persist, L3 is not achievable for this batch — record and stop.
