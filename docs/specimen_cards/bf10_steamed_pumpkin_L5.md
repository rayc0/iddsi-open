1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed pumpkin flesh onto the plate. Mash thoroughly with a fork until uniform; press and drag to break every lump. Sieve one fork-load at a time through the 4 mm sieve, discarding or re-mashing any residue that sits on top of the mesh — repeat until the whole portion passes. Do not add water, milk, or thickener; the per-level note states the food is moist as-is. The blender is not used. Target specimen: ~250 g of moist mash with all particles ≤ 4 mm and no free liquid pooling on the plate.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch white, hold 1 s, then lift. Pass: the squash mark remains, the food does not spring back or recover its shape, and the tines do not slide through loose liquid. Spoon-tilt test: load a heaped teaspoon, invert it over the plate until the spoon is near-vertical. Pass: the specimen sits in a cohesive clump, holds together for several seconds, may slide slowly, and does not pour or drip. Fork-drip test: lift a fork-load 10 cm above the plate. Pass: very little to no drip falls; any that does is drops, not a stream.

3. VISUAL QC
Mash sits on the spoon as a soft, damp mound with no sheen of free liquid around the base. Colour is uniform orange with no pale chunks. Fork-tine marks are visible on the surface, confirming softness.

5. FAIL MODES
Lumps ≥ 4 mm sitting on the sieve — re-mash, re-sieve, retest. Pooling of thin liquid on the plate — drain briefly; do not add thickener, re-mash and retest. Springy recovery after fork press — specimen too firm or fibrous, re-mash finer. If any fail persists after one re-make, record the failure and stop.
