PREP
Weigh 100 g crust-off white bread. Tear into ~2 cm pieces, add 120 ml room-temperature water, blend 45 s on high, stopping to scrape the bowl once. Press through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard what will not pass and note the loss. Weigh the sieved puree. Add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 ml of puree (calibration starting point — record actual used). Whisk 30 s, rest 2 min, whisk 10 s. If the syringe shows outside 8-10 ml at 10 s, add 0.1 g thickener increments, rest, retest. Note the final gram value.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork horizontal 1 cm over bowl; liquid drips in slow, continuous dollops, not a stream. Fork pressure: 4 mm white groove holds clean shape after thumb press; no crumbs. Spoon tilt: pooled dollop slides slowly down the spoon when tilted, not runny. Syringe flow: fill 10 ml, release stopwatch; after 10 s the syringe retains 8-10 ml.

VISUAL QC
Glossy, smooth, off-white puree with no visible lumps or bread crust flecks. Coats a spoon evenly with a thin even film. Holds a soft mound briefly before flattening under its own weight.

FAIL MODES
Too thin if puree drips in a steady stream or syringe reads under 8 ml at 10 s; add 0.1 g thickener, rest, retest. Too thick if fork cannot carve a groove or reads above 10 ml; add 5 ml water, reblend 10 s, rest, retest. Lumpy or streaky after sieving; reblend 15 s and re-sieve — do not proceed.
