1. PREP

Weigh 200 g sweetcorn kernels on the digital scale. Drain canned kernels; if frozen, thaw and pat dry with paper towel. L6 is a bite-sized-piece level: every kernel must measure 8–15 mm along its longest axis against the 100 mm ruler. Halve any kernel exceeding 15 mm and re-measure the halves; discard any piece under 8 mm and top the batch back up to 200 g with 8–15 mm kernels from the same batch. No sieve is used at any mesh size — a sieve cannot enforce a 15 mm upper limit and would cut the specimen below L6. No blender. No thickener. No water added.

Firmness gate — this is the step that decides whether the batch can be L6 at all. Press one kernel from the batch with the thumbnail until the thumbnail blanches white: it must squash flat and not rebound. Canned kernels normally pass as-is. If the kernel stays firm or rebounds the batch is not yet L6: simmer at a bare simmer 5 min, drain, pat dry, cool 2 min, re-test. Add a further 3 min only if still firm, to a 10 min total maximum — past that the kernels pulp and the batch has fallen to L5. If a kernel still rebounds after the full 10 min, sweetcorn from this batch cannot be brought to L6 by a reliable bench method; reject it and do not shoot.

Re-weigh 200 g onto the A4 capture kit surface. The per-level note states "serve as-is, kernels ≤ 15 mm" — do not blend; the simmer above only softens the kernel, it adds nothing and changes no level.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork-pressure test (mandatory at L6): press the fork tines firmly onto a single kernel with the thumbnail until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: the kernel squashes flat and does not return to its original shape. Repeat on 3 kernels from different areas of the plate; all 3 must pass. Spoon-tilt and fork-drip are not L6 tests in this protocol and are not run.

3. VISUAL QC

Kernels appear intact, glossy yellow, individual and countable on the fork. No slurry, no puree, no pooling liquid on the plate (visual check only — free liquid is not a scored test at L6). Each kernel measures 8–15 mm against the 100 mm ruler in the frame.

5. FAIL MODES

Kernel > 15 mm (fails L6 size rule): halve oversized pieces, re-measure the halves, keep only pieces still 8–15 mm, re-shoot.
Kernel < 8 mm (too small for L6): discard the small pieces and top the batch up to 200 g with 8–15 mm kernels; do not shoot a mixed-size plate.
Kernel remains firm and rebounds under the thumbnail (fails fork-pressure — too firm, not yet L6): the batch is under-softened or the wrong variety. Simmer 3–5 min longer (10 min total maximum), drain, pat dry, cool 2 min, re-test. If it still rebounds after the full 10 min, sweetcorn from this batch cannot reach L6 by a reliable bench method — reject the batch and source softer kernels; do not shoot and do not substitute a finer level.
Kernels pulp, split or lose countability (over-simmered — the batch has dropped to L5): discard, fresh batch, shorten the simmer to 5 min and re-test.
Free liquid pools on the plate: drain longer or pat dry, re-shoot.
