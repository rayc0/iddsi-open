1. PREP

Place 200 g sweetcorn kernels on the digital scale. Drain canned kernels; if frozen, thaw and pat dry with paper towel. The per-level note states "serve as-is, kernels ≤ 15 mm" — do not blend. No sieve needed. No thickener added. No water added. Confirm each kernel visually measures ≤ 15 mm against the 100 mm ruler; if any exceed 15 mm, halve or discard those pieces. Re-weigh 200 g onto the A4 capture kit surface.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork-pressure test (L6 mandatory): press the fork tines firmly onto a single kernel with the thumbnail until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: kernel squashes flat and does not return to original shape. Repeat on 3 kernels from different areas.

Spoon tilt (supporting): place 5 kernels on a spoon and tilt to 90°. Pass: pieces sit on the spoon, do not flow or drip off.

Fork drip (supporting): lift 5 kernels on fork tines and hold level for 5 seconds. Pass: no liquid drips through tines. Kernels are low-moisture; drip test confirms absence of free liquid.

3. VISUAL QC

Kernels appear intact, glossy yellow, individual and countable on the fork. No slurry, no puree, no pooling liquid. Each kernel measures ≤ 15 mm against the 100 mm ruler in the frame.

5. FAIL MODES

Kernel > 15 mm (fails L6 size rule): halve oversized pieces, re-measure, re-shoot.
Kernel remains firm and rebounds under thumbnail (fails fork-pressure): this batch is undercooked or wrong variety — re-prep with longer heating, re-test.
Free liquid pools on the spoon (fails drip test): drain longer or pat dry, re-shoot.
