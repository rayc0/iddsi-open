PREP
Weigh 300 g stewed hairy gourd (skin and seeds removed). Place in blender; blend 8-10 s until coarse. Transfer to tray and mash firmly with fork until no lumps exceed 4 mm. Press all material through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue that will not pass. Return sieved mash to bowl and fold gently to redistribute moisture; if the mash sits in a loose pool, add thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml of mash and stir to combine. Record final grams and any thickener added on the capture sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat spoon until the thumbnail blanches white; the mash must squash, hold the depressed shape, and not spring back. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoonful 45 degrees; specimen stays as a cohesive mass with no run-off or drip. Fork drip: lift fork clear of the bowl and hold 5 s; nothing drips through or falls from the tines. Particle check: lift a 5 g scoop and confirm no piece exceeds 4 mm against the ruler.

VISUAL QC
Surface looks uniformly wet and glossy with fine specks only. No skin threads, no free liquid pooling at the bowl edge. Particles are rice-sized or smaller, evenly distributed.

FAIL MODES
Stringy fibres or skin remain and refuse to pass the sieve; re-blend 3 s and re-mash. Mash weeps thin liquid on standing; add thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml and rest 60 s before re-testing. Mash holds shape on tilt but springs back under fork; it is L4 or L6, not L5 — thin with 5-10 ml warm water and re-sieve to <=4 mm, restart.
