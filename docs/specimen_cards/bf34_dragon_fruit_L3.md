1. PREP
Weigh 200 g ripe dragon fruit flesh (no skin). Add 80 ml water. Blend 25 s on high until smooth. Sieve through 4 mm once; discard fibrous residue. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of sieved volume as the calibration starting point; record actual grams used. Stir 20 s, rest 2 min, stir 5 s. If the mix runs off the spoon, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml and re-stir; record final value.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork over bowl for 10 s; drips slowly and continuously through tines — passes if it does not pour in a single stream and does not sit as a lump.
Fork pressure: press fork tines into specimen on a plate for 5 s; surface m dents clearly, no full separation, specimen does not spring back.
Spoon tilt: tilt a loaded spoon; specimen slides off slowly in one cohesive dollop, leaves a thin even coating.
Syringe flow: fill 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release, wait 10 s; leaves 8-10 ml in the syringe. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Even magenta-pink, no skin flecks or fibre. Smooth glossy surface that mounds then slowly levels on the spoon. Coats the spoon back without running off the rim.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: drips in a steady stream off fork, syringe reads under 8 ml — add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 2 min, re-test.
Too thick: piles and holds shape, leaves over 10 ml — whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml, re-test.
Fibre lumps: visible specks, sieve catches residue — re-blend 10 s and re-sieve. Max one re-make; second fail = discard and note cause.
