1. PREP
Select a firm, ripe pear. Rinse, dry, and place on a clean board. Cut into 8 wedges; core each wedge by removing the seeds and central stem fibres with a paring knife. Cut wedges lengthwise into slices approximately 8–10 mm thick. Weigh out 80–120 g of slices as the reference specimen. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. Serve slices whole on a white plate as normally presented. Record the actual mass in grams on the capture sheet. The per-level note states fresh slices as regular, so processing is not required and would invalidate the L7 specimen.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: Press the tines of a fork flat onto a slice with normal thumb pressure. The slice must not compress, squish, or change shape; it must retain its full original form. Spoon tilt: Slide a slice onto a metal spoon and tilt the spoon to a 45-degree angle. The slice must remain on the spoon with no sliding or flow. Fork drip: Lift a pierced slice above the plate; no liquid, puree, or particles must drip from the fork. All three tests must pass for L7 confirmation.

3. VISUAL QC
Slices hold their shape on the fork with sharp, intact edges. Flesh surface looks matte and fibrous, never glossy or wet. No pooling liquid visible on the plate beneath the slices.

4. SHOTS
Capture three stills and two clips. Still 1: 45-degree hero of the slice arrangement with the 100 mm ruler and 15 mm checkerboard in frame. Still 2: top-down plate shot with the checkerboard visible. Still 3: fork-press close-up at mid-compression. Clip 1: 8–12 seconds of fork-press action and spoon-tilt. Clip 2: full IDDSI test film showing fork pressure, spoon tilt, and fork drip. 5. FAIL MODES
Slices are too ripe or over-steamed and compress under fork pressure: remake with a firmer pear. Cut pieces are too thin and bend under fork pressure: remake at 8–10 mm thickness. Juice pools on the plate from bruising or warming: pat slices dry before capture and re-shoot.
