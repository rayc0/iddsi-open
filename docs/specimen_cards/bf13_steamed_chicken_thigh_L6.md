PREP — Weigh 200 g steamed chicken thigh, skin and gristle trimmed off. Dice to ≤15 mm pieces on the A4 board against the 15 mm checkerboard. Pulse in the blender 5–10 s only if a piece still exceeds 15 mm after dicing; re-check and discard the pulse if pieces turn paste-like. No sieve, no thickener, no added liquid.

IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure: press a piece flat with the fork tines until thumbnail blanches white; piece squashes, does not spring back. Spoon tilt: lift a small portion on a spoon; piece holds shape, falls off cleanly when tilted or shaken, no runny liquid. Fork drip: lift a forkful from a small pile; no drip or stream through the tines. L6 passes all three.

VISUAL QC — On the fork: matte surface, no glistening skin or jelly sheen. On the spoon: pieces sit as distinct cubes, not a smear, no pooled liquid at the spoon well. Pieces read clearly against the 15 mm checkerboard squares.

FAIL MODES — Pieces remain firm or rubbery after fork press: re-steam 2 min, retest. Pieces mash to paste from over-blending: re-dice from fresh thigh, no blender. Pieces exceed 15 mm or include gristle: discard batch, re-trim and re-dice.
