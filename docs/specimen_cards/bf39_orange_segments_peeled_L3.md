PREP
Weigh 200 g fresh peeled orange segments on the digital scale. Add 80 ml water. Blend in the consumer blender for 45 s until smooth. Press through the 4 mm sieve; discard pulp and seeds retained. Weigh the strained purée back on the scale. Add thickener at 4.5 g per 100 ml of purée as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Stir with a fork for 30 s, rest 2 min, re-stir 15 s. If the drip through fork tines is faster than the L3 window, add thickener 0.5 g at a time, rest, and re-stir until the flow window is hit. Record final purée mass, thickener mass, and total volume used.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a forkful 5 cm over a plate; the purée should drip slowly through the tines, not run in a continuous stream. Fork pressure: pressing the fork tines into 1.5 cm of purée on a spoon leaves a clear pattern that does not flow back together. Spoon tilt: tilting a spoonful 45 degrees shows the purée moves as a single bolus, not liquid. Syringe flow test: fill the 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release, wait 10 s; 8-10 ml should remain in the syringe. All four must pass.

VISUAL QC
On the spoon: a soft, glossy mound with no free liquid pooling at the edge. On the fork: a slow, thick drip once every 1-2 seconds, no splatter. Homogeneous orange colour with no visible pulp specks or seeds.

FAIL MODES
Too runny: drip is continuous or syringe reads under 8 ml — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-test. Too thick: no drip or syringe reads over 10 ml — thin with 5 ml water, rest, re-test. Visible pulp or seeds in final purée — re-sieve before adding more thickener; do not re-blend after thickener is added, as blending breaks the thickened matrix.
