1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed fish fillet. Flake against the grain into pieces no larger than 15 mm using fork and knife; do not blend — blending turns fish to paste and is incompatible with L6. Inspect every flake for bones; discard any piece containing bone. Hold pieces on a plate, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. Record starting mass on the scale log. Forks and spoon must be dry.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure: press fork tines onto a piece on a flat plate until thumbnail blanches white. Pass when the piece squashes, does not spring back, and breaks apart rather than recovering shape. Spoon-tilt: lift a heaped spoonful and tilt until vertical; pass when pieces sit in a pile on the spoon and slide off together, not as a single cohesive bolus that pours continuously. Fork-drip: no liquid should drip through the tines; pass is zero drip. Skip the syringe flow test — that is L3 only. L6 requires size plus fork-pressure evidence only.

3. VISUAL QC
Flake surface shows clear muscle layers, not a uniform paste. Pieces each measure under 15 mm against the 100 mm ruler. Pile holds shape on the spoon with visible gaps between pieces; no pooling liquid on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm — re-flake and re-measure. Fillet is over-steamed to a wet mash, so fork leaves liquid on plate — re-make with a shorter steam and pat pieces dry. A bone is found during QC — discard that piece and any from same fillet section; re-flake remaining muscle.
