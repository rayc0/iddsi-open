1. PREP
Weigh 200 g takeaway mapo tofu (sauce + curds). Decant into consumer blender; blend 10 s to break tofu curds into smaller pieces while keeping visible solids. Pour through 4 mm sieve over a bowl; press solids through with the back of a spoon so only soft curds and sauce pass; discard any firm skin or coarse chili solids caught on the mesh. Return sieved mass to the blender, add 60 ml warm water, blend 5 s to loosen to a spoonable consistency. Spoon-cut any lumps that remain larger than 15 mm with the edge of a spoon until all pieces measure ≤ 15 mm against the 100 mm ruler. Do not add thickener — this is L6, not L3. Record final mass on the scale for the log.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press fork tines onto a piece until thumbnail blanches white. Piece must squash, change shape, and not spring back when fork is removed. Spoon-tilt test: a spoonful must hold its shape on the spoon and only fall off when the spoon is tilted fully sideways, not on a slight tilt. Fork-drip test: a piece resting on the fork must not drip or run through the tines continuously. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a soft, irregular cube roughly 10–15 mm, surface glossy with chili oil, edges rounded not ragged. On the spoon: a cohesive mound that slides slowly when tilted, sauce pooling slightly at the base. No runny liquid pooling separately on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm after cutting → re-cut smaller. Mixture is too fluid and runs through fork tines or drips on slight spoon tilt → re-blend with less water and re-test. Mixture is too stiff to squash under fork → add 10 ml water, re-blend, re-test.
