1. PREP
Weigh 200 g takeaway mapo tofu (sauce + curds). Drain in a fine colander for 10 min; retain the sauce and record its mass. Do not blend at any point and do not press the curds through a 4 mm sieve — a 4 mm mesh cannot enforce a 15 mm limit, and passing everything through it leaves a loose ≤4 mm saucy mass that is L5 at best. Cut the drained curds to 10–15 mm cubes, target 12 mm ± 2 mm, against the 100 mm ruler; discard firm tofu skin, whole chili solids, Sichuan peppercorn, and any bone or gristle. Gauge for oversize only: pass the cubes over a 15 mm sieve and re-cut anything retained until nothing is held back. Simmer the retained sauce for 60 s until no free oil separates and it coats the back of a spoon; discard the separated oil. Toss the curds with no more than 15 ml of the reduced sauce per 100 g of curds — the plate must show no separate runny liquid pooling. Do not add thickener — this is L6, not L3. Do not add water. Record the final plate mass (target 150–170 g) on the scale for the log.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test — the only IDDSI test that applies at L6: press the fork tines onto a single piece until your thumbnail blanches white. The piece must squash, change shape, and not spring back when the fork is removed. Size confirmation on the 100 mm ruler: every piece must measure 8–15 mm on its longest edge. Spoon-tilt and fork-drip tests are not used at L6 and must not be run or recorded as level evidence.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a soft, irregular cube roughly 10–15 mm, surface glossy with chili oil, edges rounded not ragged. On the plate: separate pieces sitting in a light sauce film, no runny liquid pooling separately and no free oil. No crumbling to mash when the pieces are lifted.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm after cutting → re-cut smaller and re-gauge on the 15 mm sieve. Pieces below 8 mm, or the batch crumbles to mash when lifted → the curd is too fragile for L6; this batch cannot be produced at L6 by bench method, and the closest achievable is L5 (fork-mashable, ~4 mm) — cut to 4 mm, re-test, and file as L5 rather than filing an L6 card. Free liquid pools on the plate → drain longer, reduce the sauce a further 30 s, and re-toss with less sauce; do NOT re-blend and do NOT add water. Too stiff to squash under fork pressure → the curd is overcooked or fridge-firm; rest 5 min at room temperature, and if it still will not squash, add 10 ml of the reduced sauce and toss — do not blend, and do not add water.
