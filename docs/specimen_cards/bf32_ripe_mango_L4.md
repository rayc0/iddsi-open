1. PREP
Peel ripe mango, remove pit. Weigh 250 g flesh, add 40 ml water. Blend 45 s on high until smooth. Pass through 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fiber. Return puree to blender, blend 10 s to re-homogenize after sieving. Add thickener at 0 g per 100 ml starting point (calibration baseline); record actual grams added to reach L4 hold. No further dilution.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the specimen; puree must mound and not drip between tines under gravity; a clear tine outline should hold.
Spoon tilt test: load a heaped spoon; surface stays mounded at a 20–30 degree tilt; no thin tail runs off.
Fork drip test: lift fork; specimen sits in a mound above the tines and does not flow through gaps; no separate thin liquid pools on the plate.
(Syringe flow test is for L3; not required here.)

3. VISUAL QC
Specimen on fork: uniform bright yellow, no visible fiber or seed flecks, matte sheen, clean tine outline visible beneath the mound. Specimen on spoon: smooth, glossy, mounded surface that holds its peak. No free liquid ring around the mound.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen drips through fork tines or flows on tilt — too thin; re-make adding thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml increments and re-test. Visible fiber streaks or seed specks after sieving — sieve was rushed; re-pass and re-blend. White starch haze or gummy mouthfeel — over-thickened; re-make from baseline and step up in 0.25 g per 100 ml increments. If any re-make is required, re-shoot all five media items before logging the specimen as L4.
