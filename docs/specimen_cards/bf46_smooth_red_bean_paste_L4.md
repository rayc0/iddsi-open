1. PREP
Weigh 200 g ready-to-eat red bean paste into blender jar. Blend 20 s on high to remove lumps. Pass through 4 mm sieve using spatula, pressing gently; discard retained skins/seeds. Weigh back; record actual yield. Do not add water, milk, or thin liquid — the per-level note specifies serve-as-is if shape holds with no thin liquid. Do not add thickener; L4 for this food is calibrated from the as-packaged paste, and the operator records the actual gram yield only. If a thin serum separates on standing, decant and discard it before capture.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: scoop a heaped teaspoon, hold fork tines down over a white plate for 10 s. PASS — paste sits as a mound above the tines; nothing drips. Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the mound on the spoon until thumbnail blanches white. PASS — mound deforms but does not separate, no thin liquid weeps at the edges, no residue left in the tine gaps. Spoon tilt test: draw spoon across the paste then tilt to 90°. PASS — paste holds a soft peak and does not flow off. Skip the syringe flow test; it applies to L3 only.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, uniform deep-red-brown surface with zero visible bean skins or grits. Paste mounds cleanly on the spoon with straight edges and no slumping. Tines of the fork remain clean after the drip test; no serum pools on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
A. Thin serum pools on the plate during drip test → reject; decant again, re-test; if it recurs, reject the batch. B. Visible skins, grits, or bean fragments in the mound → reject; re-sieve or re-blend 10 s and sieve again. C. Paste flows through fork tines or slumps off the tilted spoon (L5/Drink territory) → reject; do not rescue by adding thickener, re-make with a different pack.
