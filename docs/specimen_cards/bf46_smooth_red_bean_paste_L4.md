1. PREP
Pre-screen the pack before anything else: draw a dessert spoon through the paste and lift it out — a pack that levels out within 10 s is thinner than L4; set it aside and open another, and record which pack was used. Weigh 200 g ready-to-eat red bean paste into the blender jar. Blend 20 s on high, scraping the jar down once at 10 s, to remove lumps. Pass through the 4 mm sieve with a spatula, pressing gently — at L4 the sieve is the lump-free gauge and this is the level where it is deliberately used; discard everything retained (skins, seeds, grits). Weigh back and record the actual yield (expect 180–200 g). Do not add water, milk or any thin liquid to a paste that already passes, and do not add thickener to a paste that already passes: L4 for this food is calibrated from the as-packaged paste, and the operator records the actual gram yield only. If a thin serum separates on standing, decant and discard it before capture. Bring the sieved paste to 20–25 °C before testing and capture it at 20–25 °C — warm paste flows more readily than cold, so a warm-only test will pass a paste that fails at room temperature.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-drip test (5.3): scoop a heaped 5 ml teaspoon, hold the fork tines down over a white plate for 10 s. PASS — paste sits as a mound above the tines; nothing drips or streams through. Fork-pressure test (5.1): press the fork tines into the mound on the spoon until your thumbnail blanches white. PASS — the mound deforms but does not separate, no thin liquid weeps at the edges, and no residue is left in the tine gaps. Spoon-tilt test (5.2): place one heaped 5 ml teaspoon (about 10 g) on a dessert spoon and tilt to 90°. PASS — the paste holds a soft peak and does not flow, slump or drip off the spoon. Skip the syringe flow test (5.4): it applies to L3 only.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, uniform deep-red-brown surface with zero visible bean skins or grits; no air bubbles from blending (tap the spoon twice on the bench before shooting). Paste mounds cleanly on the spoon with straight edges and no slumping. Tines of the fork remain clean after the drip test; no serum pools on the plate. All checks made at 20–25 °C.

5. FAIL MODES
A. Thin serum pools on the plate during the drip test → decant again and re-test; if it recurs within 2 min, reject the pack — do not blend the serum back in. B. Visible skins, grits or bean fragments in the mound → re-sieve through the same 4 mm sieve once more, or re-blend 10 s and sieve again; if a third pass is needed, reject the pack. C. Too thin — paste flows through the fork tines, slumps off the tilted spoon, or will not hold a peak: this is L3 behaviour (lump-free, flows freely), not L5, which requires ~4 mm lumps and is not reachable from a smooth paste. Correct by THICKENING, in this order: (1) gentle evaporation — warm the 200 g in a small heavy pan over low heat, stirring constantly, until the fork-drip test holds a mound for 10 s and the spoon holds a peak; re-weigh and record the mass lost (expect 10–30 g). (2) If evaporation alone will not hold a peak, blend in a measured thickener — max 5 g cornflour or 3 g xanthan gum per 200 g — then re-run all three tests. Never add water, milk or any thin liquid at this step, and never rescue a thin paste by mixing in paste from a different pack — there is no criterion for that and it produces a mislabelled specimen. If neither step works, reject the pack. D. Too thick — paste will not pass the 4 mm sieve, or the mound stays rigid with no deformation under the thumbnail press: correct by THINNING with a measured amount of water, 5 ml at a time, blending 5 s after each addition and re-testing, to a maximum of 20 ml per 200 g. Record the total ml added; a paste needing more than 20 ml is not this pack's L4 calibration — reject it. E. Specimen tested above 25 °C (just-warm paste) → the flow result is not valid; cool to 20–25 °C and repeat all three tests before capture.
