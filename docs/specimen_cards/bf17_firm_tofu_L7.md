PREP
1. Remove tofu from pack, drain packing liquid, pat block dry with paper towel.
2. Starting amount: 200 g whole block, kept intact. Do NOT blend, do NOT sieve, do NOT add thickener or water. Reference specimen for L7 must be the food as normally served.
3. Per the per-level note ("block/slice as served"), cut a single 30 mm × 30 mm × 15 mm slice on the capture board with the 100 mm ruler for scale. Record actual cut dimensions on the data sheet.
4. Keep remaining block chilled for re-takes if needed.

IDDSI TEST
- Fork pressure: press the square tines of a standard fork fully into the slice until the tines bend. The slice must NOT break apart, mash, crumble, or pass through the tine gaps. Pass = specimen remains as one cohesive piece under full thumb pressure.
- Spoon tilt: tilt a standard spoon 45°. The slice must NOT slide, drip liquid, or fall off. Pass = specimen stays on the spoon.
- Fork drip: lift fork with slice clear of the plate for 10 s. Pass = no drips, no liquid runs.
- Syringe flow test: NOT applicable (L7 only). State "N/A" on sheet.

VISUAL QC
- Slice holds a clean, sharp rectangular edge with no crumbling at corners.
- Surface shows smooth, intact skin with no split layers or weeping liquid pooling on the plate.
- Block retains original pale ivory colour; no translucent mushy zones at cut faces.

FAIL MODES
- Slice smears, splits, or crumbs under fork thumb pressure → under-cooked/soft batch; re-make with a firmer chilled pack and re-record actual supplier code.
- Visible liquid pools on plate from cut faces → excess syneresis; drain longer, pat drier, re-cut slice.
- Slice bends without breaking and tines sink in fully → L6 not L7; re-make from a denser block, document on sheet.
