1. PREP
Weigh 150 g hard-boiled egg (white + yolk, no shell). Add 100 ml cool water. Blend in consumer blender 45-60 s on high until fully smooth. Press through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard any residue retained on mesh (L3 is lump-free — a clean sieve pass is required). Transfer sieved purée to a cup on the digital scale. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of sieved purée (≈250 ml, i.e. ≈5.0 g) as the calibration starting point; whisk briskly 30 s, rest 2 min, re-whisk 10 s. Record actual grams used, final weight (g), and total volume (ml, by graduated cylinder). Target: specimen pours in a continuous slow ribbon and drips slowly through the fork tines; 8-10 ml remains in the syringe at t=10 s.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork; sample flows through the tines as a slow, continuous drip, not as separate drops and not as a stream.
Spoon tilt: tilt spoon 45°; specimen flows off in a steady ribbon in 3-5 s.
Syringe flow (mandatory at L3): fill 10 ml syringe, release; after 10 s, 8-10 ml remains in the barrel.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, uniform pale-yellow liquid with no yolk granules, white flecks, or specks visible against white cup. Coats spoon back as an even thin film; leaves the spoon in one continuous ribbon, not in clumps. No free water separation at the cup wall.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin (drips through fork as a stream, <8 ml left in syringe): whisk in extra thickener 0.3 g/100 ml increments, re-test.
Too thick (spoon tilt holds a mound and gives no ribbon; syringe still holds 10 ml at t=10 s with no flow): stir in 5-10 ml water, re-whisk, rest 2 min, re-test. Do not add thickener on this branch.
Gritty or speckled (visible yolk lumps, sieve residue): re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if still gritty, re-make — specimen must pass sieve cleanly.
