1. PREP
Drain liquid from the chilled box. Place 400 g silken tofu on a board and cut with a knife and ruler into cubes measuring 8–15 mm in every dimension (nominal 12 mm). Do NOT blend; the per-level note requires intact pieces. Do NOT sieve — a 4 mm sieve cannot size an L6 piece and would reduce the batch to L5 or finer. Do NOT add thickener; L6 is unthickened. Do NOT add water. Check every piece against the 100 mm ruler: no dimension below 8 mm, none above 15 mm.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test (the only IDDSI test that applies at L6): press the flat of a fork down on one cube until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass if the cube squashes, does not spring back, and breaks apart easily.
Piece-size check: with the 100 mm ruler and 15 mm checkerboard in frame, verify every piece in the batch measures 8–15 mm in all three dimensions. A piece under 8 mm is an L5 lump and must be re-cut, not served as L6. Spoon tilt and fork drip are L3–L5 and L3–L4 tests respectively and do not apply at L6 — do not run them and do not record them as pass/fail. Skip the syringe flow test; it is L3 only.

3. VISUAL QC
Cubes hold a clean, sharp cut edge with rounded corners. Surface is glossy and wet, not torn or smeared. Pieces separate when lifted with a fork; no paste or smear between them.

5. FAIL MODES
Cubes cut above 15 mm: re-trim and reshoot. Cubes cut below 8 mm: the batch is L5, not L6 — re-cut from a fresh block to 8–15 mm. Pieces collapse into a paste or smear rather than deforming as a discrete cube under the fork: the block is too fragile for L6 — pat cubes dry with paper towel and reshoot; if they still paste, replace with a firmer block from a fresh box. Pieces spring back when pressed: tofu too firm — set aside and replace with a softer block from a fresh box.
