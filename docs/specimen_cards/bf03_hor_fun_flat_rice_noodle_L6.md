PREP
Weigh 200 g blanched hor fun sheets on the scale. Stack sheets, cut across the width into strips, then cross-cut into pieces no larger than 15 mm. Confirm each piece measures ≤15 mm against the 100 mm ruler. Do not blend; do not sieve; do not add thickener. No liquid is added. The L3 calibration thickener row does not apply to this L6 specimen — record "N/A" in that column. Pieces must remain visually separate, not mashed into a paste.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test — press the fork tines into a piece until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: the piece squashes, does not spring back, and can be mashed with easy pressure between fork and bowl. Spoon-tilt test — place a heaped teaspoon on the plate and tilt to 90 degrees. Pass: the piece sits in a pile and slides/plops off with little or no flow; it does not pour. Fork-drip test — lift a piece on the fork and hold over a white plate for 10 seconds. Pass: minimal drip, no continuous flow, no tail. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
Pieces on the fork should appear as distinct flat soft strips, not clumped or sticky. Surface should look moist and pliable, not dry-cracked or gummy. On the spoon-tilt, the mound should hold shape briefly before sliding; it must not level out to a smooth liquid layer.

FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm — re-cut smaller. Pieces feel rubbery or spring back under fork pressure — discard, source is too firm, re-blanch or remake. Pieces clump into a sticky mass with no separation — overworked, discard and re-cut fresh. Any single failure → full re-make.
