1. PREP
Dice braised winter melon into cubes ≤15 mm per side. Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add thickener; this is L6, not L3, so no liquid calibration is required. Weigh 200 g of diced pieces on the digital scale as the working specimen. Confirm no skin, fibrous core, or seed clusters remain — winter melon flesh must be uniformly soft through to the centre of each cube.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the tines of a fork against a single cube until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass criterion: the cube squashes fully and does not return to its original shape; it breaks apart easily. Spoon-tilt test: not applicable at L6 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence. Fork-drip test: not applicable at L6 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence.

3. VISUAL QC
Cubes on the fork appear translucent-ivory with a slight gloss, no opaque white core. Edges look soft-rounded rather than sharp-cut. Surface yields visibly under the fork tines before pressure is even applied. By-eye check only (not a section 5.2 test at L6): place a cube on a spoon and tilt to 45°; pass criterion: the piece slides/rolls off readily — it must not sit as a solid lump. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L6): lift a fork-load of cubes; pass criterion: pieces sit proud on the tines, nothing pours through the gaps as liquid.

5. FAIL MODES
Cubes exceed 15 mm — re-dice smaller. Centre of cube is firm or opaque — winter melon was under-braised; re-braise until fully soft. Pieces hold shape on spoon-tilt with no squash on fork-press — texture is closer to L7; re-cook at lower temperature longer.
