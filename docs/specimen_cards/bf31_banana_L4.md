1. PREP
Weigh 150 g peeled ripe banana on the scale. Cut into 2 cm coins. Add to blender with 60 ml water. Blend 25 s on high, stop, scrape down, blend 20 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Weigh the sieved puree and record the actual mass — target 190–210 g; do not work to a guessed volume. Return puree to blender, add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 g sieved puree (about 3 g for a 200 g batch) as the L4 calibration starting point, blend 10 s to homogenize, rest 2 min, stir, and re-test. 0 g is not a valid L4 starting point for banana: ripe banana puree is thin and separates on standing and will not hold L4 without thickener. The operator records the actual thickener mass used on the capture sheet. The 60 ml of water above is the only water this specimen ever gets: adding water to a runny L4 is the route to L3, which pours off the spoon and drips slowly through the fork tines, and the FAIL MODES below forbid it outright.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines fully into the specimen. The specimen must hold a clean indentation with no surface recovery for several seconds and must not flow through the slots between the tines.
Spoon tilt test (protocol 5.2, the L4 wording): scoop a full spoon, hold it level, then tilt it sideways. Pass = the specimen holds its shape on the level spoon and then slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue left behind, with no separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon. A specimen that pours off in a slow continuous stream or drips in threads is too thin — that is the L3 criterion, not L4. A specimen that will not slide off at all and has to be scraped away is over-thickened and is past L4. "Zero flow off the spoon" is NOT the L4 pass: an L4 puree does slide off, and chasing a zero-flow endpoint over-thickens the specimen out of L4.
Fork drip test: lift a forkful 5 cm above the surface. The mass must sit in a mound above the tines with no drip or thread.
No separate thin liquid may pool on the surface of the specimen at any time.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface shows a soft satin sheen, not glossy or wet. Color is uniform pale cream, no brown flecks, no seed specks, no fiber strings. The mound on the spoon holds clean edges with no slumping shoulders.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny: puree flows through the fork tines or drips in threads — add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 g of puree (1.0 g on a 200 g batch), re-blend 5 s to disperse, rest 2 min after each addition, until fork drip holds a mound above the tines and spoon tilt slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue. Never thin a runny batch with water: water is the L3 route, and a watered batch that passes fork-drip by holding a mound is a different level from the one on the label.
Over-thickened: the specimen will not slide off the tilted spoon and has to be scraped away, or holds a sharp cut edge and cracks — the titration has overshot L4. Do not add water to rescue it; re-make from the reserve aliquot at the last dose that passed and re-test. If no dose between 1.5 and 4.0 g per 100 g gives a puree that both holds a mound above the fork tines and slides off the spoon as one mass, banana L4 is not reachable for this pack — record the event as unresolved and do not label it L4.
Lumpy: visible fiber or seed specks remain after sieving — re-pass through the 4 mm sieve, do not push through with force. L4 is lump-free, so a lumpy specimen is too coarse; it is never corrected by leaving it. If a second pass does not clear it, the specimen is coarser than L4 (the L5 direction): mark the event FAIL with tests.pass = false, do not shoot it as L4, and do not re-label the media L5 — protocol section 7 QC rule 1 forbids renaming a specimen's media into another level.
Thin liquid pooling on top after standing 30 s — the puree is separating and is under-thickened. Do NOT drain it off and re-blend, which mixes the free liquid straight back in; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g puree, rest 2 min, re-test. If pooling recurs at the ceiling dose of 4.0 g per 100 g puree, the banana was over-ripe (soft and watery) — swap for a just-ripe batch and restart.
Ceiling: banana L4 is reachable only with thickener. If the specimen still fails the spoon-tilt criterion above — either still pouring in a stream at 4.0 g thickener per 100 g puree, or refusing to slide off the spoon at all — record the event as unresolved and do not label it L4.
