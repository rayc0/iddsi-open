1. PREP
Weigh 150 g peeled ripe banana on the scale. Cut into 2 cm coins. Add to blender with 60 ml water. Blend 25 s on high, stop, scrape down, blend 20 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Weigh the sieved puree and record the actual mass — target 190–210 g; do not work to a guessed volume. Return puree to blender, add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 g sieved puree (about 3 g for a 200 g batch) as the L4 calibration starting point, blend 10 s to homogenize, rest 2 min, stir, and re-test. 0 g is not a valid L4 starting point for banana: ripe banana puree is thin and separates on standing and will not hold L4 without thickener. The operator records the actual thickener mass used and any extra water on the capture sheet; extra water is added only in 5 ml increments, re-testing after each.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines fully into the specimen. The specimen must hold a clean indentation with no surface recovery for several seconds and must not flow through the slots between the tines.
Spoon tilt test: load a heaped teaspoon, tilt to 90 degrees. The specimen must sit in a mound with zero flow off the spoon.
Fork drip test: lift a forkful 5 cm above the surface. The mass must sit in a mound above the tines with no drip or thread.
No separate thin liquid may pool on the surface of the specimen at any time.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface shows a soft satin sheen, not glossy or wet. Color is uniform pale cream, no brown flecks, no seed specks, no fiber strings. The mound on the spoon holds clean edges with no slumping shoulders.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny: puree flows through the fork tines or drips in threads — add thickener in 0.5 g increments per 100 g puree, re-blend 5 s to disperse, rest 2 min after each addition, until fork drip holds a mound and spoon tilt shows zero flow. Never thin a runny batch with water.
Lumpy: visible fiber or seed specks remain after sieving — re-pass through the 4 mm sieve, do not push through with force. L4 is lump-free, so a lumpy specimen is too coarse; it is never corrected by leaving it.
Thin liquid pooling on top after standing 30 s — the puree is separating and is under-thickened. Do NOT drain it off and re-blend, which mixes the free liquid straight back in; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g puree, rest 2 min, re-test. If pooling recurs at the ceiling dose of 4.0 g per 100 g puree, the banana was over-ripe (soft and watery) — swap for a just-ripe batch and restart.
Ceiling: banana L4 is reachable only with thickener. If the specimen still fails spoon tilt at 4.0 g thickener per 100 g puree, record the event as unresolved and do not label it L4.
