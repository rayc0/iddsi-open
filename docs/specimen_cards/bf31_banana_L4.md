1. PREP
Weigh 150 g peeled ripe banana on the scale. Cut into 2 cm coins. Add to blender with 60 ml water. Blend 25 s on high, stop, scrape down, blend 20 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Return puree to blender, add 0.0 g thickener for this L4 calibration starting point, blend 10 s to homogenize. The operator records the actual thickener mass used and any extra water on the capture sheet. Total volume target: roughly 200 ml.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch white. The puree must hold the indentation clearly with no surface recovery for several seconds — a clean dent, not a slow slump.
Spoon tilt test: load a heaped teaspoon, tilt to 90 degrees. The specimen must sit in a mound with zero flow off the spoon.
Fork drip test: lift a forkful 5 cm above the surface. The mass must sit in a mound above the tines with no drip or thread.
No separate thin liquid may pool on the surface of the specimen at any time.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface shows a soft satin sheen, not glossy or wet. Color is uniform pale cream, no brown flecks, no seed specks, no fiber strings. The mound on the spoon holds clean edges with no slumping shoulders.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny: puree flows through fork tines or drips in threads — re-blend 5 s and add thickener in 0.5 g increments until the fork-drip test holds a mound.
Lumpy: visible fiber or seed specks remain after sieving — re-pass through the 4 mm sieve, do not push through with force.
Thin liquid pooling on top after standing 30 s — drain off, re-blend briefly; if it recurs, the banana was under-ripe, swap batch and restart.
