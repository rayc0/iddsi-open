PREP
Take 150 g braised lotus root. Cut into 2 cm pieces, add 40 ml hot water. Blend 45 s, rest 15 s, blend 30 s until a coarse slurry forms. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any fibrous residue that will not pass. Return sieved pulp to the blender, blend 20 s to a uniform smooth paste. Calibrate from L3 starting point: 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml paste, stirred in with a fork and rested 60 s. If the fork-drip mound collapses below the tines, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml and re-test. Record the final grams per 100 ml on the data sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: a 5–7 mm mound sits cleanly on the tines and does not flow through or drip continuously. Fork pressure: tines pressed into the mound leave a clear indentation; the mound does not split into separate liquid and solid, and pieces do not fall below. Spoon tilt: a spoon holding the specimen tilted 45° holds the full mound with no slide or run-off. No separate thin liquid pools on the plate surface.

VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy-smooth with no visible fibre or speckle. Colour is uniform deep beige-pink across the mound. Mound edges hold a rounded dome with no weeping liquid ring at the base.

FAIL MODES
Mound flows through fork tines → re-sieve and add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml. Visible fibres or speckle remain → re-blend 20 s and re-sieve. Liquid pools around the mound on the plate → discard, re-blend with 10 ml less water and recalibrate thickener.
