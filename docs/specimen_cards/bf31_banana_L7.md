1. PREP
Peel one firm ripe banana — fully yellow, no brown spotting, no oxidised patches; the cut face must feel firm to thumb pressure. Per-level requirement, stated on this card: L7 = pieces 15 mm or more, regular, requiring real chewing. Cut the peeled banana crosswise into halves with a single knife stroke. Keep one half as the reserve for a re-shoot; place the other half cut face up on the plate. Weigh the plated half on the digital scale: it must be 90–110 g and at least 15 mm in every dimension (a half is, by construction); record the actual mass to 1 g on the capture sheet — that recorded mass is the reference value for the set. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. This specimen is a whole food as normally served, so there is no calibration to perform and no grams-per-100 ml to record.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure (the deciding test at L7): hold the half steady on the plate and press the fork tines fully into the flesh with the same thumbnail-blanching pressure used at L6. Pass: the piece holds its shape — it may dent, split along the fibres, or cleave into two pieces, but it must NOT squash flat to a paste, smear, or collapse to something that needs no chewing. Pass = clear shape retained and real chewing required. A piece that flattens with little effort is L5/L6, not L7 — fail the specimen, do not record it as L7.
Spoon tilt: not required at L7; skip.
Fork drip: not required at L7; skip.
Syringe flow: not required at L7; skip.

3. VISUAL QC
Whole peeled banana half on plate, pale cream to pale yellow flesh, no brown oxidation streaks. Skin-on is wrong; blended paste is wrong; sliced coins are wrong; a half that has squashed flat is wrong.

5. FAIL MODES
Served unpeeled with skin on. Cut into coins or mashed beyond the L7 piece. Left out long enough to brown — cut fresh faces or re-make.
Flesh over-ripe and squashing flat to a paste under the fork with little effort — reads as L5/L6, not L7 — re-make from a firm just-ripe banana. Resisting the fork is the required L7 behaviour and is NOT a failure.
Any failure: re-make from a fresh banana, re-shoot the full set, do not patch a single frame.
