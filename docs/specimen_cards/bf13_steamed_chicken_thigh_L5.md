1. PREP
Steam 180 g fresh chicken thigh until just cooked through. Strip and discard skin, bone, and visible fat. Place 120 g cooked meat in the blender; add 40 ml warm broth from the steaming pot. Pulse 6–8 s, scrape down, pulse 4 s more. Stop and check: meat should be coarse mince, not purée. Rub a tablespoon of the mince across the 4 mm sieve — it should pass with thumb pressure; any stringy bits remain in the sieve and are discarded. Transfer to a bowl, fold through 2 ml more warm broth with the fork to bring to moist, cohesive mince (not wet, not dry). No thickener required. Record final mass; final portion served for test: ~80 g.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch, hold 5 s. PASS — specimen squashes, does not spring back, thumb leaves a clear print; particles stay ≤4 mm.
Fork drip: hold loaded fork clear of the cup for 10 s. PASS — no drip falls; mince holds together on tines.
Spoon tilt: scoop a teaspoonful, tilt spoon to ~45°. PASS — mince sits in a cohesive lump; it slides only if pushed, does not flow. No separate thin liquid pools.

3. VISUAL QC
Mince on fork shows distinct granules, not a paste, all visibly under 4 mm. Surface glistens but no free broth pools on the spoon. A light thumb-print stays indented.

5. FAIL MODES
Mince still strings or shreds when sieved — re-pulse 2–3 s and re-sieve; if still fibrous, downgrade to L6 or re-cook longer before mincing. Specimen weeps free liquid into the cup — drain on sieve 30 s, re-fold with less broth. Specimen balls up dry on the fork — fold in 2–3 ml warm broth and retest. Any re-make requires a fresh take number.
