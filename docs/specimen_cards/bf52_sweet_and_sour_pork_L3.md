1. PREP
Weigh 200 g sweet and sour pork onto the digital scale. Add 60 ml warm water (40–50 °C) directly into the takeaway container. Blend in the consumer blender on high for 45 s, stop, scrape down with a fork, blend 30 s more. Pass the entire blend through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a fork; discard any coarse residue that will not pass — L3 is lump-free, so the 4 mm sieve is the production gauge here and a fully smooth puree is the target. Measure the sieved volume. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of sieved liquid as the calibration starting point; whisk in with a fork for 20 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Record the actual grams used on the data sheet. If the pour is too fast or too slow, adjust in 0.2 g/100 ml steps and re-record.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: scoop a forkful and hold the fork level, 10 cm above the plate, for 10 s; the specimen must drip through the tines as a slow continuous strand — not pour in a stream, and not sit on the fork without moving — pass. Spoon tilt test: tilt a spoonful to 30° and hold 5 s; the specimen must flow off slowly in a ribbon, not run, and must not fall off in a single blob — pass. Flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, hold it vertical, release and time 10 s; between 8 and 10 ml must remain in the syringe — pass. No fork pressure test at L3: that method covers L4, L6 and L7, and its "a clear pattern remains pressed in" criterion describes an L4 — do not run it here, and do not thicken the specimen to satisfy it.

3. VISUAL QC
Coating on the fork tines is even, glossy, and fully opaque with no visible pork fibre or batter specks. The specimen pours off a spoon in a slow, continuous ribbon that holds together for 1-2 cm before merging. Colour is uniform pale orange-red, no separated oil pooling on the surface.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen pours too fast from the spoon, runs through the fork, or leaves under 8 ml in the syringe at 10 s — too thin; add 0.2 g/100 ml thickener, remix 20 s, rest 60 s, retest. More than 10 ml remains in the syringe at 10 s, or the specimen holds shape on the fork instead of dripping — too thick, drifting toward L4; thin with 20 ml warm water, reblend 30 s, re-sieve, then retest and only re-thicken if it now flows too fast. Specimen is lumpy — reblend 20 s and re-sieve through the 4 mm sieve, then re-calibrate against the flow test. Oil separates and pools on top — reblend with an extra 20 ml water, re-sieve, re-thicken to the flow window.
