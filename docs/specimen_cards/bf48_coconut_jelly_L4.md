1. PREP
Weigh 150 g chilled coconut jelly straight from the pack into the blender cup, on the 0.1 g scale. Add 30 ml of the pack syrup that ships with it (shake the pack first so the syrup is uniform). Blend 25 s on high, stop, scrape the wall, blend 20 s more. The result should read as a smooth, opaque white puree. Pour through the 4 mm sieve using a spoon back; press and discard any fibrous bits that will not pass. Target solids caught in the sieve: under 5 g. Re-weigh the sieved puree on the 0.1 g scale and record that mass — it is the single dose base for every corrective step below, in grams per 100 g of weighed puree; never dose per ml, and never per the original 150 g batch. If the section-2 tests fail on the thin side (any drip in the fork-drip test, or the puree slides off the spoon in the spoon-tilt test), add 0.5 g of the kit thickener per 100 g of weighed puree (e.g. 160 g puree → 0.8 g thickener), hand-whisk 10 s, rest 60 s, and re-run all three tests. Record the actual thickener dose in grams on the card.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: lift a fork loaded with puree 15 cm above the plate; the mound must sit on top of the tines with no drips. Pass = zero drip in 10 s.
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat into the puree on a plate; the surface must blanch white around the tines but the puree must not squeeze through to leave separate liquid. Pass = no thin liquid weeps out.
Spoon tilt test: load a heaped teaspoon, tilt to 45 degrees; the puree must hold its shape on the spoon without sliding off. Pass = holds for 5 s.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: a soft, glossy mound that drops cleanly when tipped, no pooled liquid at the base. On the fork tines: a coherent cap that sits proud, not a glossy coat. On the plate after fork press: a clean dent, no watery ring around it. L4 is lump-free: the 4 mm sieve is the gauge for that — the catch must stay under 5 g and the puree must show no visible grain on the back of the spoon.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: puree drips through the fork or flows off the spoon in the tilt test → thicken — add 0.5 g kit thickener per 100 g of the re-weighed puree, hand-whisk 10 s, rest 60 s, re-run all three tests.
Too thick: puree cracks on the spoon or resists the fork press → thin — add 10 g pack syrup per 100 g of the re-weighed puree, re-blend 15 s, re-run all three tests.
Grainy: sieve catches over 5 g fibre → re-blend 15 s and re-sieve; if the catch is still over 5 g, discard the batch — a grainy puree is not L4.
