Applicable tests: fork pressure only (100 mm ruler in frame for the sizing check). Spoon tilt and fork drip are L3/L4/L5 tests and must not be recorded as pass/fail at L6. No syringe flow test above L3.

1. PREP
Weigh 200 g trimmed raw bok choy on the scale. Blanch in 1 L rapidly boiling unsalted water for 60 s, drain, pat dry between paper towels. Record actual cooked weight on the capture sheet. Slice stems lengthwise into halves, then cross-cut every 10-12 mm. Leaves: stack, roll to a log no thicker than 15 mm, and cut the log into 8-15 mm lengths. Target final size: 8-15 mm in EVERY dimension — no piece under 8 mm, none over 15 mm. Sizing check: lay the cut pieces on the 15 mm checkerboard; every piece must be shorter than one 15 mm square and longer than half of one. Pieces over 15 mm: re-trim. Pieces under 8 mm: set aside and re-cut from a thicker stem section. Do NOT blend; the fork-pressure criterion requires intact soft pieces, and blending destroys the test geometry. Do NOT sieve; sieving is for purées and a sieve can never enforce an 8-15 mm window. Do NOT add thickener; L6 pieces carry their own structure. Record final piece size range (mm, min-max) on the capture sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test (the only test at L6): press thumbnail into a stem piece until nail blanches white. Pass = piece squashes, does not spring back, and the nail blanches white. Sizing is part of the pass: measured on the 100 mm ruler, every piece is 8-15 mm in its longest dimension and ≥8 mm in its narrowest. No spoon-tilt test, no fork-drip test, no syringe flow test at L6.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: pieces hold together as discrete chunks, not mash; visible moisture sheen but no pooling liquid. On the spoon: pieces sit as a small mound, not a slurry — presentation only, spoon tilt is not an L6 test. Colour should read pale to mid-green, glossy, not dull or fibrous-looking.

4. SHOTS
Shoot three stills and two clips, handheld or tripod, 45 cm distance, smartphone, natural desk light + lamp. 1) 45° hero: pile of pieces on white plate with 100 mm ruler and 15 mm checkerboard in frame. 2) Top-down with checkerboard centred under the pile. 3) Fork-press close-up mid-press, thumbnail blanching white. Clip A: 8-12 s fork-press, then a continuous sizing pan across the pile against the ruler. Clip B: IDDSI test film, full sequence. 5. FAIL MODES
(1) Pieces >15 mm → re-cut, reshoot. (2) Pieces <8 mm → too small for L6, discard them and re-cut from thicker stem sections; if most of the batch is under 8 mm it is an L4 specimen and must be reshoot on the L4 card, not this one. (3) Pieces too firm, nail does not blanch white → re-blanch 30 s longer in boiling water, re-test, reshoot. (4) Pieces collapse to purée under the thumbnail, or turn slippery and translucent → over-blanched; start again with a 40 s blanch. (5) Excess water pooling → drain, pat dry, reshoot.
