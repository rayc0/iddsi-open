1. PREP
Peel ripe banana; weigh 120 g. Add 60 ml water. Blend 25-30 s on high until smooth and pourable (per-level note: blend-thin). Sieve through the 4 mm sieve into a bowl; press with the back of a spoon; discard fibre. Weigh the sieved puree and the bowl to get volume by difference (1 g ≈ 1 ml). Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 3.0 g per 100 ml of puree. Stir with a fork for 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 15 s. Operator records the actual grams used on the data sheet. If it will not pour off the spoon, add 5 ml water and re-stir; if it drips freely through the fork, add 0.2 g thickener and re-stir. Total target: about 180 g finished specimen.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: tilt a fork loaded with specimen; liquid drips in a slow, continuous stream, not a steady pour and not a single dollop. Pass = slow continuous drip.
Fork pressure test: press fork tines flat against the specimen surface; surface yields easily, tines leave a clear print, specimen does not hold the tine shape when the fork is lifted. Pass = clear print, no standing shape.
Spoon tilt test: a spoon held at 45° releases most of its load in a slow ribbon; a thin coating remains. Pass = slow ribbon, thin residual.
IDDSI flow test: fill the syringe to 10 ml; after 10 s, 8-10 ml remains. Pass = 8-10 ml retained.

3. VISUAL QC
Specimen on the fork: glossy, smooth, no visible fibre lumps; drips off the lower tines before the upper ones clear. On the spoon: fluid surface levels flat when the spoon stops moving, with no peaks or splits. Colour uniform pale cream, no brown specks.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: drip test runs as a steady pour, syringe leaves under 8 ml; add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, stir, rest 2 min, re-test.
Too thick: fork drips as a single dollop or not at all, syringe leaves over 10 ml; whisk in 5 ml water, re-test.
Visible fibre on the fork after drip: sieve was skipped or rushed; re-sieve and re-thicken, do not patch the existing batch.
