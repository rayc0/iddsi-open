1. PREP — Weigh 250 g steamed sweet potato flesh on the scale. Hand-mash with a fork to coarse crumb. Pulse in the consumer blender for 5-8 s only, to break down any lump larger than 4 mm. Do not blend to a smooth purée; the per-level note requires visible lumps up to 4 mm, and a smooth purée is L4. Sieving at L5 is a GAUGE, not a production step: take a 20 g sample of the mash and press it against the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon — the sample must pass through with light pressure, which confirms no lump exceeds 4 mm. Any material retained on the mesh is an oversized lump: lift it off, hand-mash it with the fork, and re-gauge. Never blend the batch to force it through the mesh, and never discard the whole mash to remove fibre. Weigh the sieved... [gauge only] mash; sprinkle thickener at 0.5 g per 100 g mash, folding in — this is a binder for free moisture only and must not be increased to fill gaps, which would smooth the specimen toward L4. Add 10-15 ml hot water per 100 g until the mash coheres when pressed but still looks moist. Hold for 60 s before testing. Record actual grams of thickener and water used on the data sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST — Spoon-tilt test (the required test at L5): load a heaped teaspoon and tilt to 45°; the specimen must sit in a cohesive pile and not slide, drip, or fall off the spoon. Fork-pressure test (supporting check only at L5, not a required test): press the fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate; the sample should squash, the tines should leave a clear imprint, and the material should not spring back — a specimen that fails this while passing spoon-tilt is still L5 and must not be rejected on fork pressure alone. No fork-drip test at L5: fork-drip applies at L3 and L4 only. No syringe flow test at L5: syringe flow applies to L3 only.

3. VISUAL QC — Coarse crumbs up to 4 mm clearly visible in the mound, sized against the 4 mm sieve holes and the 15 mm checkerboard; surface glistens with moisture but no free liquid pools around the mound on the plate; the pile holds its shape when the fork is withdrawn, leaving distinct tine marks.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Over-blended to a smooth purée — fails spoon-tilt by spreading and sliding off the tilted spoon, i.e. it reads L4; remake with a fresh 250 g portion, hand-mash to coarse crumb and pulse 5-8 s only, then gauge with the 4 mm sieve. (b) Too dry, crumbs do not cohere, fall off the tilted spoon — add 5 ml water per 100 g and rest 60 s before re-testing. (c) Excess liquid pools on the plate, indicating the specimen is too thin (L4 or lower) — fold in 0.2 g thickener per 100 g, rest 60 s, re-test.
