1. PREP
Steam fresh pear until fork-tender. Weigh 200 g cooked flesh. Add 40 ml water. Blend 45-60 s on high until smooth. Pass through 4 mm sieve; discard fibrous residue. Re-weigh puree. If puree reads below L4 on fork drip, add thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point; record actual grams used. Stir, rest 2 min, re-test. No second sieving, no added sugar.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork loaded with ~5 ml puree; puree sits as a coherent mound above the tines, does not drip or run through gaps. Pass if mound holds 5 s.
Fork pressure: press fork tines into puree on a flat plate; puree yields to pressure, shows clear tine marks, but does not run back into the grooves. Pass if tine marks remain visible after the fork lifts.
Spoon tilt: load a spoon, tilt to 90 degrees over a plate; puree clings, slides only as a single mass, no pour. Pass if mass stays cohesive.
No separate thin liquid pools around the mound. No syringe test at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is matte-glossy, uniform, with no visible fibre flecks or water separation. Colour is even cream to pale beige from end to end. Spoon retains a clean rounded heap; fork tines come up coated but the spaces between tines remain filled by puree, not empty.

5. FAIL MODES
Puree too wet: runs through fork tines or pools liquid on plate. Re-make: add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml steps, rest 2 min, retest.
Puree too stiff: cracks on the spoon, falls off in chunks. Re-make: blend in 5 ml water, retest; do not exceed 60 ml total added water.
Visible fibre or skin specks: re-sieve once; if still present, re-blend 15 s and re-sieve.
