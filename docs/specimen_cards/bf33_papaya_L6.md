1. PREP
Select a ripe papaya, peel, halve, and scoop out seeds. Dice the flesh on a clean board, then pass pieces through a 4 mm sieve to catch fragments above 15 mm; re-dice any oversize bits until the whole batch passes. You need 200 g of sieved pieces for the specimen plate. Do not blend — pureeing destroys the L6 bite-sized texture. Do not add thickener; L6 is solid pieces, not a thickened liquid. Do not add water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the flat of a fork onto one piece until your thumbnail blanches white, hold 10 s, then lift. Pass = piece squashes and does not recover its shape. Spoon-tilt test: rest five pieces on a metal spoon and tilt to a 45-degree angle for 10 s. Pass = all pieces sit on the spoon, no slide, no drip. Fork-drip test is not used at L6. No syringe flow test at L6.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces on the fork should look moist and glossy with sharp 15 mm edges visible against the ruler. Surface should hold its shape when lifted — no weeping juice pooling on the spoon. Color uniform pale orange, no translucent mush at the cut faces.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces recover after fork press — too firm, re-test a riper section or discard. Pieces smear or crush to paste — overripe or blended, restart with a firmer fruit. Pieces larger than 15 mm — re-dice and re-sieve before capture.
