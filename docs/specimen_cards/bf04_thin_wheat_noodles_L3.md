1. PREP — Weigh 250 g cooked thin wheat noodles on the digital scale. Blend with 180 ml room-temperature water on high for 45-60 s until fully smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; press residue with the back of a spoon and discard what remains. For each 100 ml of sieved liquid, add 1.5 g thickener as the calibration starting point; the operator must record the actual grams used to hit target. Stir 30 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Re-weigh and record final mass. Per-level note says blend-thin: confirm the blend is fully smooth before sieving; if strands remain, re-blend 20 s.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork drip: lift fork from specimen; liquid drips slowly in a continuous thin stream through the tines, not in dollops. Fork press: tines leave a clear, shallow impression on the surface with no flow back into the groove. Spoon tilt: spoon holds its shape when tilted to 45 degrees, then slides off in one mass within 1-2 s, not as a free pour. Syringe flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe, release for 10 s; 8-10 ml must remain in the barrel. All four must pass before capture.

3. VISUAL QC — Surface looks glossy and homogenous with no noodle strands, lumps, or bubbles. Coats the spoon back in an even film about 1-2 mm thick. Holds a soft mound on the fork without running through the tines between lifts.

5. FAIL MODES — Too runny: drip is a free stream or syringe leaves under 6 ml; add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest, retest. Too thick: fork drag is heavy or spoon does not slide; blend in 10 ml water per 100 ml and retest. Visible strands on sieve or in QC: re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if still present, discard and remake.
