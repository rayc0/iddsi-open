1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed pumpkin flesh. Blend 45 s on high until smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Weigh the sieved purée; add 5 g thickener per 100 ml of purée (record the actual grams used). Stir 30 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. No water added.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the tines into the mound on the spoon. Pass criterion — the purée keeps a clear thumb-nail print but does not split or run.
Spoon tilt test: tilt the spoon. Pass criterion — the mound sits, no drip, no slide.
Fork drip test: lift the fork loaded with purée. Pass criterion — purée mounds above the tines and falls as one piece or sits; nothing drips through the gaps.
L4 requires all three.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: matte surface, no liquid pooling at the rim, no skin or fibre flecks. On the fork after lift: a single soft clump sitting above the tines, tines still visible underneath.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny — purée flows through fork gaps or drips from a tilted spoon: re-blend 10 s with +2 g thickener per 100 ml, retest.
Rubbery — fork leaves no mark: discard, re-blend fresh batch, halve thickener to 2.5 g per 100 ml.
Stringy with orange flecks — sieve was skipped or rushed: discard, re-sieve the same batch, retest.
