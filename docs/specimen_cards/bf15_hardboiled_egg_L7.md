PREP
Start with one whole hard-boiled egg as purchased, weighed on the digital scale; record starting mass in grams (typical 50–60 g). Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve. Do NOT add thickener, water, or liquid. Per the per-level note, present the egg whole or halved as boiled, cooled to room temperature (≈20–25 °C, ~5 min rest). This is the reference specimen. No processing step applies.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the tines of a fork into the egg white until the thumbnail blanches white. The egg must squash completely, change shape, and not return to its original form when the fork is removed. Pass = full deformation, no recovery.
Spoon tilt test: place a spoonful of egg (white and yolk) on a spoon and tilt until the spoon is nearly vertical. The specimen must slide/fall off easily with little or no residue left on the spoon. Pass = slides off readily.
Fork drip test: lift the specimen on the fork; no drip applies (solid). N/A — record as not applicable.
No syringe test required at L7.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: matte, opaque white and yellow yolk surface; clean tine marks visible after press; no pooled liquid, no glossy sheen, no liquid weeping from the yolk. On the spoon: dense, cohesive mound that drops cleanly when tilted.

FAIL MODES
Undercooking → soft or jammy yolk that spreads on tilt; discard, rebatch. Cracked or rubbery overcooked white that springs back under fork pressure → discard, rebatch. Wet condensation or yolk film pooling on the plate → pat dry, retake; if persistent, rebatch.
