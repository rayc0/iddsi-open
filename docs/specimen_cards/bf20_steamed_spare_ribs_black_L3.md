1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed spare ribs (meat only, bones removed). Add 120 ml warm water to the blender jar first, then ribs. Blend 45 s on high. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard solids retained. Measure the sieved volume. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 4.0 g per 100 ml of liquid; whisk in, rest 90 s, re-whisk. If the sample pours when the cup tilts, it is too thin; if it mounds and does not drip from the fork, it is too thick. Record the actual thickener mass and final volume on the capture sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork horizontal 1 cm above the cup; liquid drips in slow, continuous drops within 10 s — pass.
Spoon tilt: spoon tilted 45 degrees lets the sample flow off in a slow ribbon, not a pour and not a stack — pass.
Fork pressure: tines pressed into 1 cm of sample leave a clear impression that does not refill — pass.
Syringe flow: fill to 10 ml, release; 8-10 ml remain after 10 s — pass. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface looks matte and smooth, no lumps, no meat fibres. Holds a soft peak on the fork that slowly slumps. No free liquid pools around the specimen on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny: drips in a stream from fork, syringe leaves under 8 ml — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest, retest.
Too stiff: mounds on spoon, no drip, syringe leaves over 10 ml — thin with 5-10 ml water, retest.
Fibrous: strands catch on sieve or fork tines — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if still fibrous, the food is unsuitable at L3.
