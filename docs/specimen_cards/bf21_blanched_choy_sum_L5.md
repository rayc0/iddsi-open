1. PREP
Start with 300 g blanched choy sum, drained, patted dry on a clean cloth. Chop the stems and leaves together with a knife until the largest pieces are no more than 4 mm; discard any stalk core that resists chopping. Do not force the whole mass through the 4 mm sieve — L5 is a lumpy level (lumps ~4 mm) and sieving it makes it lump-free L4. Use the 4 mm sieve as a gauge only: hold a pinch of chopped mash against it, and any piece larger than a 4 mm hole goes back on the board to be re-chopped. Weigh the chopped mash and record the grams. Press and smear with a fork in a circular motion for 60-90 seconds until it holds together as a coarse mash with visible flecks but no whole leaves. Sprinkle 3 g thickener per 100 g of mash, fold in with the fork, rest 60 seconds, then re-mash briefly. The thickener must bind moisture only — the ~4 mm lumps and flecks must stay visible and distinct after it is folded in. Add 10-15 ml warm water only if the mash is crumbly; the final mix must feel moist but not wet, and must show no separate thin liquid. Record actual thickener grams and water ml on the card.

2. IDDSI TEST
Applicable at L5 (CAPTURE_PROTOCOL §5.2, with §5.1 as a supporting check only): spoon tilt is the deciding test; fork pressure is a supporting check. Fork drip and syringe flow are L3/L4 tests and must not be run at L5.
Spoon tilt: a teaspoon-sized portion must hold its shape on the spoon when tilted to 45 degrees without sliding off or dripping; slight slumping is acceptable, collapse is not.
Fork pressure (supporting check only): press the tines flat onto the mash for 5 seconds; the mash must squash, the surface must blanch pale, and it must not spring back when the fork is lifted.
All applicable tests must pass; if any fails, re-mash or re-thicken, do not film.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: cohesive clump, no loose bits falling, surface shows green flecks and lumps up to ~4 mm with no whole leaf blades and no smooth glossy paste (a smooth, paste-glossy surface means the specimen is L4, not L5). On the spoon: holds a rounded mound, slight sheen from moisture, no pooling liquid at the rim.

5. FAIL MODES
Mash too wet: a watery halo forms on the plate; add 1 g thickener per 100 g, rest, re-mash, re-film. Pieces too coarse: stalks remain larger than the 4 mm holes of the gauge sieve; re-chop and re-check, do not patch with extra water. Mash too dry and crumbly: fork-pressure leaves clean tine marks without squashing; add 5 ml water and 0.5 g thickener, re-mash, re-test. Mash has gone smooth and glossy with no visible lumps: the specimen is now L4, not L5 — reduce the thickener by 0.5 g per 100 g and re-mash; if the flecks and lumps cannot be brought back, restart the batch with fresh blanched greens.
