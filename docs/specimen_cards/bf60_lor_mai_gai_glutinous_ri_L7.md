**Bought form & allergens:** bought as a ready-made steamed lor mai gai (lotus-leaf glutinous rice parcel), frozen or chilled, reheated per pack. Allergens: soy and wheat (soy sauce), sesame (usually), shellfish (dried scallop, often), plus chicken and shiitake — the allergen load is pack-specific, so check the label.

1. PREP
- Reheat one whole parcel per pack — typically 20–25 min steamed from frozen, or 8–10 min from chilled — until the centre probes ≥75 °C.
- Remove and discard the outer lotus leaf and any foil. Keep the rice mass with its fillings (chicken, shiitake, sausage, dried scallop) as served — do not pick them out. If any single filling piece is over 25 mm across, halve it with a knife so nothing exceeds 25 mm.
- With a knife or spoon, portion the rice mass into regular pieces 15–25 mm across, measured against the 100 mm ruler (the 25 mm figure is an operational cap for the capture frame, not a texture limit — L7 requires only ≥15 mm). Discard any fragment under 15 mm: it reads L6.
- Do not blend. Do not sieve — a 4 mm sieve cannot enforce a 15 mm limit. Do not add thickener or liquid — L7 is the whole-food reference.
- Weigh 200 g of portioned pieces onto the plate; keep covered and warm (≤10 min) until testing.

2. IDDSI TEST
- ACTIVE — 5.1 fork pressure. Press the tines of a standard metal fork flat onto one 15–25 mm piece with steady thumb pressure until the nail blanches white; hold 5 s; lift. PASS: the piece holds its shape, the tines leave a clear, persistent indentation, the fork does not cut through in one press, and the piece does not crumble into loose grains. Test 3 pieces; all 3 must pass. This is what rules out L5 (fork-mashable) and L6 (squashes flat).
- SKIP — 5.2 spoon tilt. Applies at L3/L4/L5 only; an L7 specimen is a solid that must stay put on a tilted spoon, so the test carries no discriminating information.
- SKIP — 5.3 fork drip. Applies at L3/L4 only; L7 carries no free liquid to drip through the tines.
- SKIP — 5.4 syringe flow. Every L3 only; 15–25 mm pieces cannot be drawn into a 10 ml syringe.
- L7 verdict = all pieces 15–25 mm AND 5.1 fork pressure passes.

3. VISUAL QC
- Grains are distinct and bound only by their own stickiness; no sauce pooling and no free liquid at the parcel base.
- Surface matte, not glossy with free liquid.
- Every piece 15–25 mm on the ruler; no fragment below 15 mm.
- Fork tines leave visible imprints; the rice does not smear or ooze between the tines.

5. FAIL MODES
- Free liquid pools at the parcel base (reads L6 or wetter): dab the surface and base with paper towel for 30 s before testing; if liquid returns, remake with a fresh parcel and add no sauce.
- The rice mass crushes through the fork in one press (too soft — reads L5/L6): re-steam a fresh parcel 5 min longer so it sets firmer; if it still crushes, that parcel is too wet — use a drier pack.
- Any piece under 15 mm, or the mass crumbling into single grains: re-cut from a fresh parcel into 15–25 mm regular pieces and discard the fines — do not press them back together.
- The mass falls apart into loose grains when lifted (binding lost, under-steamed): re-steam 5 min longer, rest 2 min, re-portion.
- Grains slide off the spoon when tilted: not a fail criterion at L7 — 5.2 spoon tilt is skipped. Judge the specimen on piece size and fork pressure only; if the piece also crumbles, apply the binding fail mode above.
