1. PREP
Weigh 60 g white bread (crust off). Cut into 2 cm cubes. Add 90 ml water. Blend 30 s on high, scrape, blend 20 s more. Press 1/3 of the puree through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; remaining puree should pass without grit. Return sieved portion. Add thickener per the calibration starting point stated on the kit card (1.5 g/100 ml as starting reference) and stir 20 s; let stand 2 min. Re-weigh final specimen and record. Target: smooth, cohesive, no lumps over 4 mm.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen until thumbnail blanches white; specimen should compress and hold together, not flow through the gaps.
Fork drip test: lift fork with a heaped mound; specimen must sit in a clear mound above the tines, not drip or run off.
Spoon tilt test: scoop and tilt spoon; specimen must hold its shape and slide as one lump or stay, not flow off.
Pass criterion for all three: specimen remains cohesive, holds shape, shows zero separation of thin liquid.

3. VISUAL QC
Puree on spoon: matte, uniform off-white, no crust streaks or dry flecks. Fork tines after pressure: puree shears clean between tines, gaps stay open, no thin tail dripping from the underside. Surface of specimen in bowl: smooth, soft peaks that slump slowly when tapped.

4. FAIL MODES
Too runny: drips off spoon or through fork tines; re-blend with 0.5 g thickener per 50 ml and retest after 2 min stand.
Lumps over 4 mm or visible crust: sieve again; if still gritty, re-blend 10 s with 10 ml extra water and re-thicken to calibration.
Thin liquid separates on standing: under-thickened; add 0.5 g thickener, stir, rest 2 min, retest.
