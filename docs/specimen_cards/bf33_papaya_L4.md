PREP
Weigh 250 g ripe papaya flesh, peel and dice. Add to consumer blender with 60 ml water; blend 45 s. Pass through 4 mm sieve; discard coarse residue. Return puree to blender, add 2.0 g thickener per 100 ml as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Blend 20 s, rest 60 s, blend 10 s. Target final mass approximately 300 g.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork; puree must sit in a mound above the tines, not drip through. Pass = no drip in 10 s.
Fork pressure: press fork tines into specimen on a flat plate; puree must flatten and hold, not run back through gaps. Pass = clean tine spacing, no flow.
Spoon tilt: scoop, tilt spoon 45°. Pass = specimen stays on spoon, no sliding off.

VISUAL QC
Surface glossy and uniform, no pulp specks or liquid ring on the plate. Color even orange-pink, no streaks. Holds a soft peak when mounded with the fork.

FAIL MODES
Too thin: puree flows through fork tines or drips in 10 s; re-blend and add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, retest.
Too thick: specimen cracks or holds tine imprint without spreading; re-blend with 10-15 ml water, retest.
Visible liquid pooling around mound: under-thickened or over-blended; sieve again and add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml steps.
