1. PREP
Take a fresh apple. Core and slice into 8 wedges, skin on, about 15-20 mm thick at the widest point, matching how an apple is normally eaten. Steam 4-6 minutes until just tender when pierced with a fork but still holding shape. Drain on a paper towel for 60 seconds. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add liquid. Working amount: 150-200 g steamed wedges. Serve at eating temperature. This is the reference specimen for L7 Regular.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: Press a standard dinner fork onto a wedge on a flat plate with normal thumb pressure for 10 seconds. Pass if the wedge flattens, cracks, or breaks apart but does not pass through the slots. Spoon tilt test: Pick up a wedge on a spoon and tilt until the spoon is near vertical. Pass if the wedge slides off or stays on the spoon without oozing through the tines. Fork drip test is not applicable at L7; record as N/A.

3. VISUAL QC
Wedge holds its shape with no puree or juice pooling on the plate. Skin intact, edges defined, steam-moist surface but not wet or dripping. Fork tines leave a clean dent without the wedge collapsing into mush.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-steamed wedge turns to mush and passes through fork slots: re-make with 60 seconds less steam. Apple variety too soft (e.g., overripe McIntosh): re-make with firmer variety such as Granny Smith or Pink Lady. Sauce or juice pools on plate from under-draining: re-make with a full 90-second drain.
