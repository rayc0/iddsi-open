PREP
Weigh 250 g thick-style takeaway or chilled plain congee into the blender cup. Add 60 ml hot water to loosen the chilled starch matrix. Blend on high for 45 seconds, stopping once to scrape the wall. Press through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard retained solids and lumps — the per-level note "blend-hold" confirms sieving is required. Return puree to the cup and rest 30 seconds so the starch relaxes. If the mound flattens on the spoon, stir in thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used and the resulting appearance.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: a single fork pressed into the surface leaves a clear, clean-edged indent that holds its shape when the fork is lifted. Spoon tilt: a loaded spoon held at 45 degrees keeps the full mound on the bowl with no slide or drip. Fork drip: lifting a fork dipped 2 cm deep and held vertically over the plate, the puree sits as a cohesive mound above the tines — none flows through, none drips in 5 seconds. No separate thin liquid pools around the specimen.

VISUAL QC
Surface reads matte and even, not glossy or watery. A spoonful mounds and peaks rather than spreading. No grains, skin flecks, or ginger shreds visible against the fork tines.

FAIL MODES
Specimen flows through the tines or pools thin liquid — re-blend 15 seconds, rest, re-test. Specimen cracks or falls off the spoon in a chunk — add water 10 ml, blend 10 seconds, re-test. Lumps or skin remain after sieving — re-sieve; do not proceed.
