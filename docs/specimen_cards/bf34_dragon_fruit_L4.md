1. PREP
Weigh 250 g fresh ripe dragon fruit flesh on the digital scale. Cube into 2 cm pieces. Add to the consumer blender with 50 ml water. Blend 45 s on high, stop, scrape down, blend 30 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue and seeds that do not pass. Weigh the sieved puree. Per 100 ml puree, add 1.0 g thickener as the calibration starting point — record the actual grams used. Stir with a fork for 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 10 s. If the mound collapses, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 2 min, and re-test. Do not exceed 2.0 g per 100 ml; if L4 is not reached, re-make with longer sieve work and re-blend rather than over-thickening.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the mound on a flat plate; the surface must hold a clear indentation without the mound splitting or liquid bleeding — pass if the print stays. Spoon tilt test: load 5 ml onto a spoon and tilt to 45 degrees; the specimen must sit in a mound and not flow or drip off — pass if it stays piled. Fork drip test: lift the fork; the specimen must sit in a mound above the tines, not drip continuously through them — pass if zero drips in 10 s. No separate thin liquid must pool on the plate.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a soft, glossy magenta mound with no visible seed flecks or white pulp chunks and a clean indentation under the tines. On the spoon: a rounded dome that holds its peak when tilted. On the plate: matte surface, no watery halo around the base of the mound.

5. FAIL MODES
Fail 1: watery pink liquid pools on the plate within 30 s of plating — puree was under-thickened or not rested; re-make and add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml. Fail 2: mound slumps flat on spoon tilt — too much water or insufficient rest; re-make with 30 ml water instead of 50 ml. Fail 3: visible seed specks or fibrous strands on the surface — sieve was skipped or rushed; re-blend and re-sieve, do not over-thicken to hide it. Maximum two re-makes per take; on the third failure, swap fruit batch and restart.
