1. PREP
Peel and core the apple. Steam 200 g of flesh until fork-tender, ~6 min. Transfer to the blender with 60 ml of the cooking water. Blend 45 s on high, then scrape and blend 20 s more. Press through the 4 mm sieve; discard coarse residue. Return puree to blender. Decant into a measuring jug and read the volume to the nearest 10 ml (expect ~180-200 ml) and record it on the log — every thickener dose below is per 100 ml of this measured volume. Add 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml of measured puree (calibration starting point; for 200 ml that is 3.0 g). Blend 15 s to fully disperse. Total thickener is capped at 2.5 g per 100 ml (5.0 g for a 200 ml batch). If the puree still spreads when spooned at that cap, the batch is not L4 by this method — discard and re-prep; do not keep adding. Target ~250 g finished specimen (200 g flesh + 60 ml water + thickener, minus steam loss); record actual mass.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: scoop puree onto the fork; it must sit as a mound above the tines with no drip falling through in 5 s.
Fork pressure: the fork tines should leave a clean imprint without the surface snapping back or splitting.
Spoon tilt: a spoonful should hold its shape when the spoon is tilted 45 degrees; slow slump is acceptable, flow is not.
All three tests above must pass for L4 — these are the three §5 tests that apply at L4. The thin-liquid pooling check is a visual criterion recorded under VISUAL QC, not a fourth test.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is smooth and matte with no visible fibers or lumps. Color is uniform pale cream to light beige. Holds a peaked mound on the spoon tip without slumping off the edges. No separate thin liquid pools on the plate next to the spoon.

5. FAIL MODES
Too wet: puree flows through the fork or pools thin liquid on the plate; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml of measured puree volume, blend 10 s, re-test. Stop at the 2.5 g per 100 ml cap (5.0 g total for this batch); at the cap and still too wet, discard and re-prep — do not walk the batch back with water.
Too stiff: mound cracks or crumbles instead of yielding; add 5 ml water per 100 ml of measured puree, re-blend 15 s, re-test. Maximum 10 ml water per 100 ml in total; still stiff after that, discard and re-prep.
Lumpy or fibrous: visible specks survive the sieve; re-blend 20 s and re-sieve through the 4 mm sieve.
One re-make allowed; second failure, discard and note on the log.
