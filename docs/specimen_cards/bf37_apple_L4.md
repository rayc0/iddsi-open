1. PREP
Peel and core the apple. Steam 200 g of flesh until fork-tender, ~6 min. Transfer to the blender with 60 ml of the cooking water. Blend 45 s on high, then scrape and blend 20 s more. Press through the 4 mm sieve; discard coarse residue. Return puree to blender and add 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml of puree (calibration starting point; record actual grams on the log). Blend 15 s to fully disperse. If the puree spreads when spooned, add thickener in 0.5 g increments and blend 10 s between additions. Target ~220 g finished specimen.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: scoop puree onto the fork; it must sit as a mound above the tines with no drip falling through in 5 s. Fork pressure: the fork tines should leave a clean imprint without the surface snapping back or splitting. Spoon tilt: a spoonful should hold its shape when the spoon is tilted 45 degrees; slow slump is acceptable, flow is not. No separate thin liquid should pool on the plate next to the spoon. All four must pass for L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is smooth and matte with no visible fibers or lumps. Color is uniform pale cream to light beige. Holds a peaked mound on the spoon tip without slumping off the edges.

5. FAIL MODES
Too wet: puree flows through the fork or pools thin liquid on the plate; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-test. Too stiff: mound cracks or crumbles instead of yielding; add 5 ml water and re-blend 15 s. Lumpy or fibrous: visible specks survive the sieve; re-sieve or blend longer. One re-make allowed; second failure, discard and note on the log.
