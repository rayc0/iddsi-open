1. PREP
Weigh 200 g of set mango pudding into the blender cup; keep it chilled, do not warm it. Blend on high for 10-15 s only — just long enough to come smooth with no visible fruit fibre or chunks. Do not blend 25-35 s: high-speed blending shears the set gel and thins the specimen below L4. Sieve is NOT used — the per-level note is "blend-hold", so the texture is set by the set pudding plus a short blend and a 60 s hold, not by sieving and not by thickener in the base run. Do NOT add water, milk, or thickener to the base run. Pour into a clean cup and rest 60 s before testing so the gel can re-form.
Calibration: the no-add blend is tested first. If it does not hold on a fork, correct by thickening — weigh 1 g thickener per 100 ml of pudding, pulse-blend 5 s, rest 60 s, and re-test. Record the actual grams of thickener added per 100 ml. Never thin with water or milk: a sheared gel cannot be re-set on the bench, so a too-thin batch cannot be brought back to L4 by resting or re-blending — discard it and start a fresh 200 g batch.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: scoop a heaped teaspoon, turn the fork over the cup — the sample must SIT in a mound above the tines, not drip, flow, or drop through. Pass: cohesive mound that holds for the full 10 s observation.
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat into the surface. The surface must keep the fork-prong marks clean and not flow back into the grooves when the fork lifts. Pass: clear tine imprints, no runnability.
Spoon tilt test: tilt a heaped spoon to 90°. Sample must stay on the spoon; a thin tail is acceptable only if it does not drip off. Pass: holds shape on the spoon at full tilt.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy uniform orange-yellow surface, no liquid halo at the cup wall, no specks of skin or pulp. On the fork: a soft peak that rounds at the top, no sharp slump. On the spoon: the mound stays on the spoon as one piece at full tilt, with no run-off and no drip.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen flows through the fork tines or drips off the spoon → too thin (gel sheared too long, or a soft formulation): correct by THICKENING — 1 g thickener per 100 ml, pulse-blend 5 s, rest 60 s, re-test. Do not add water or milk.
Grainy mouthfeel with visible pulp → blend incomplete; extend the blend to 20 s total, never longer. Re-sieving is NOT permitted at L4.
Thin yellow liquid pools around the mound → serum separation; re-blend 5 s and rest 30 s, not 60 s, then re-test.
Achievability limit: if a fresh 200 g batch still will not hold a mound after one measured thickener addition, this pudding's gel is below L4 and cannot be brought to L4 by blending alone on the bench — record the specimen as too thin, do not photograph it as L4 evidence, and flag the card for re-specification (the closest reliable alternative is the unblended set pudding straight from the cup, which already holds a soft shape).
Any fail → discard the cup, start a fresh 200 g batch, log the actual thickener g/100 ml used, if any.
