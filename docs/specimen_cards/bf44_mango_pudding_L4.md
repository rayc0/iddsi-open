1. PREP
Weigh 200 g of mango pudding into the blender cup. Blend on high for 25–35 s until completely smooth with no visible fruit fibre or chunks. Sieve is NOT used — the per-level note is "blend-hold", so the texture is set by blending, not by sieving or by thickener. Do NOT add water, milk, or thickener. The calibration starting point is the unadjusted blend; if it does not hold on a fork, the operator records the actual grams of thickener added per 100 ml and re-blends, but the no-add run comes first. Pour into a clean cup and rest 60 s before testing.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: scoop a heaped teaspoon, turn the fork over the cup — the sample must SIT in a mound above the tines, not drip, flow, or drop through. Pass: cohesive mound that holds for the full 10 s observation.
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat into the surface. The surface must keep the fork-prong marks clean and not flow back into the grooves when the fork lifts. Pass: clear tine imprints, no runnability.
Spoon tilt test: tilt a heaped spoon to 90°. Sample must stay on the spoon; a thin tail is acceptable only if it does not drip off. Pass: holds shape on the spoon at full tilt.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy uniform orange-yellow surface, no liquid halo at the cup wall, no specks of skin or pulp. On the fork: a soft peak that rounds at the top, no sharp slump. On the spoon: the mound drops cleanly as one piece when tilted.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen flows through fork tines or drips off spoon → blend was too short or too much liquid present; re-blend 15 s, retest. Grainy mouthfeel with visible pulp → blend incomplete; re-blend 20 s and re-sieve is NOT permitted at L4, so extend blend only. Thin yellow liquid pools around the mound → serum separation; re-blend and rest only 30 s next time. Any fail → discard cup, start a fresh 200 g batch, log actual thickener g/100 ml if any was used.
