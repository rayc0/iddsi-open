1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed carrot, soft enough to crush with a fork. Add 120 ml cool water. Blend 45-60 s until completely smooth, no visible flecks. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard anything that will not push through and note the loss. Measure the sieved volume in ml. Stir in thickener at the calibration starting point of 1.5 g per 100 ml of puree, then rest 2 min and stir again. The operator records the actual grams used and the final ml. The flow should be a thick pour that drops in a continuous ribbon from the spoon. Adjust with small extra thickener (0.1-0.2 g) or water (5 ml) only if the IDDSI test fails, and re-record.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: lift the fork; the puree drips slowly in a thick continuous ribbon, not in separate drops. Spoon tilt: the specimen holds its shape on a tilted spoon, then slides off as one lump when the spoon is nearly vertical. Fork pressure: pressing the tines leaves a clear imprint but no liquid pools around it. Syringe flow: fill to the 10 ml mark, start the timer for 10 s; pass if 8-10 ml remains in the syringe.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface looks glossy and even, no orange specks, no bubbles. Coats the back of the spoon in a uniform layer about 2-3 mm thick. Holds a soft peak that slowly flattens.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny like a drink: too little thickener, add 0.2 g per 100 ml and re-test. Holds shape too firmly on spoon tilt and blocks the syringe: over-thickened, whisk in 5 ml water and re-test. Visible fibre or specks catching on the sieve: blend a further 20 s and re-sieve. Any fail means remix from the recorded quantities; do not patch the existing specimen.
