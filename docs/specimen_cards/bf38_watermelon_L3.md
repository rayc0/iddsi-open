1. PREP
Weigh 250 g seedless watermelon flesh. Blend on high 45-60 s until smooth liquid; scrape jar, blend 10 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve, pressing with the back of a spoon; discard pulp residue. Measure the sieved puree volume (ml). Add thickener at 3 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point: weigh thickener to 1 decimal, whisk into puree 30 s, rest 2 min, whisk 10 s. Record actual grams used per 100 ml. If the flow test result is outside window, adjust in 0.5 g/100 ml steps and re-test. No extra water.

2. IDDSI TEST
- Fork drip: lift fork; puree drips slowly in a continuous thin stream, not a steady pour, not in lumps.
- Fork pressure: puree flattens and spreads against the plate under fork tines, does not hold shape, leaves a clear fork-tine pattern.
- Spoon tilt: spoon tilted to ~45 degrees; puree flows off in a single slow ribbon, not a dollop, not runny.
- IDDSI flow test (10 ml syringe, 10 s): 8-10 ml remaining in syringe. <8 ml = too thin; >10 ml = too thick. Re-make.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, uniform pink with no red pulp specks; coats the spoon back evenly without pooling fast; drips from fork tines leave a brief tail that snaps off within 1 second.

5. FAIL MODES
- Too runny (drips in a steady pour, <8 ml retained): add thickener in 0.5 g/100 ml steps, re-test.
- Too stiff (holds mound, >10 ml retained, slow ribbon only): thin with 5 ml puree per 100 ml batch, re-test.
- Grainy / seeds visible: re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if specks persist, reject and re-make from a de-seeded batch.
