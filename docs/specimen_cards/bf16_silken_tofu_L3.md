PREP
Weigh 150 g silken tofu. Add 90 ml water. Blend 25 s on high until pourable. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve; press solids through with the back of a spoon, discard coarse residue retained on the mesh. Weigh blended yield, then thicken to the L3 calibration starting point of 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml blended liquid. Whisk in the powder with a fork, rest 90 s, stir 10 s. Re-weigh and record the actual thickener grams per 100 ml used on the capture sheet before filming.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: tilt a fork from a loaded spoon; liquid must drip in slow, continuous drops through the tines, not run in a stream. Spoon tilt: a teaspoon tilts and the specimen flows off in a steady ribbon, not a pour, not a slide. Fork pressure: the fork prong leaves a clear, clean imprint on the surface under light thumb pressure with no surface rupture or separation. Syringe flow test: fill the IDDSI 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release, wait 10 s; 8-10 ml must remain in the barrel. Any value below 8 ml means re-thicken; above 10 ml means it is too thin.

VISUAL QC
Specimen on the spoon is glossy, uniform off-white, with no visible curds, lumps, or whey pooling. On the fork it clings as a smooth continuous film that releases as one slow drip when tilted. No grain, no streaks, no free liquid running off ahead of the body.

FAIL MODES
Too runny: passes the fork as a stream or leaves under 8 ml in the syringe; add 0.1 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, retest. Too thick: clings to the spoon without dripping or blocks the syringe above 10 ml; blend in 5 ml water, retest. Grainy or curdled: sieve step skipped or over-blended; discard and remake.
