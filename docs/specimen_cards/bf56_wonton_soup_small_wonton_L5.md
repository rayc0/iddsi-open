1. PREP — Weigh 250 g cooked wonton + broth as the starting portion. Separate wontons from broth. Chop wontons with a fork until pieces measure ≤4 mm on the ruler. If pieces cling or ball, pulse in the consumer blender 2–3 s and re-check particle size against the 4 mm sieve. Sieve chopped mass through the 4 mm sieve; reject any retained lumps. Return the sieved mince to the broth. Mash the combined mass thoroughly with the fork until no separate thin liquid pools on the plate; broth coats the mince uniformly. Do not add thickener unless spoon tilt or fork drip shows free liquid; if free liquid appears, add thickener at 1 g/100 ml of free liquid as the calibration starting point and record the actual mass used. Per-level note: chop ≤4 mm with broth is achievable with fork + brief blender pulse; no sieving failures expected.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen until thumbnail blanches; the specimen must squash and not recover when fork is lifted. Spoon tilt: a heaped teaspoon tilted to 90° must hold its shape with no flow off the spoon. Fork drip: lifting fork at 20 cm above the plate, no more than a slow drip falls in 10 s; continuous pour = fail.

3. VISUAL QC — Mince visible as distinct ≤4 mm flecks across the spoon surface. Broth coats particles with a glossy sheen, no pooled clear liquid. Specimen mounds on the fork and holds its shape as a single cohesive mass, not a slurry.

4. SHOTS — Capture three stills and two clips. Stills: 45-degree hero of the specimen on the plate with 100 mm ruler and 15 mm checkerboard in frame; top-down on the checkerboard showing particle distribution; fork-press close-up mid-press with tines clearly indenting the mass. Clips: 8–12 s continuous showing fork-press then spoon-tilt in one take; 8–12 s IDDSI test film covering fork drip, fork pressure, and spoon tilt. 5. FAIL MODES — Particles exceed 4 mm after chopping; re-pulse blender and re-sieve. Thin broth separates and pools on the plate; add thickener 1 g/100 ml increments, remix, retest. Specimen flows off spoon at tilt or drips continuously from fork; re-mash longer and re-add thickener before re-shooting. Two consecutive fails on any single criterion = re-cook a fresh 250 g portion.
