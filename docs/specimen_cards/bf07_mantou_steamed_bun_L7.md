1. PREP

Remove mantou from pack. Steam per pack instructions until heated through and soft. Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve. Do NOT add thickener, water, or liquid. Use the bun whole as steamed. Record final specimen mass in grams on the capture sheet. Set aside on the plate of the capture kit; keep covered until shot time to prevent surface drying.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork pressure test (primary for L7): press the tines of a standard fork fully into the specimen until the tines nearly meet the surface. The specimen must not compress, squash, or change shape; the fork should lift clean. Pass = specimen returns to original shape and shows no fingernail-depth indentation, no spread under the fork.

Spoon tilt test: scoop a representative piece onto a spoon and tilt the spoon to a steep angle. Pass = piece sits on the spoon and does not slide off, flow, or drop.

Fork drip test: lift the fork loaded with specimen above the plate. Pass = nothing drips or runs through or between the tines.

Syringe flow test: not applicable to L7.

3. VISUAL QC

Piece holds its domed bun shape with no slumping. Surface looks evenly matte-porous, springy to the touch, no wet sheen, no liquid pooling on the plate around the specimen.

5. FAIL MODES

Specimen too dense or under-steamed: fork leaves a clear dent or splits the bun → fails fork pressure. Re-steam 2–3 minutes, re-test, re-shoot.
Specimen surface wet from condensation or split leaking steam: liquid pools on plate and drips from fork → fails drip test. Pat surface dry with paper towel, re-shoot; do not re-steam.
Specimen collapsed from being squeezed in pack: bun loses domed shape → fails visual QC. Use a fresh bun from the pack, do not reshape.
