1. PREP
Weigh 250 g curry fish balls plus sauce into the blender jar. Blend 45 s on high, scraping the jar wall once at 25 s. Sieve through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard coarse residue. Measure the blended volume in ml. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of blend as the calibration starting point; whisk 15 s, rest 60 s, whisk 5 s. If the blend reads under 8 ml in the syringe after 10 s, add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml and retest. Record the final grams per 100 ml on the card. The food list note is blend-thin, so sieving and thinning with thickener are required. Do not thin with water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: tilt a fork with a heaped teaspoon of specimen; drips slowly in 4 to 6 discrete drops over 10 s. Fork pressure: the white of the prong shows through the specimen with light pressure and the surface returns flat when pressure is released. Spoon tilt: a teaspoonful slides off when the spoon is tilted, leaving a thin coating. Syringe flow test: 10 ml specimen in the IDDSI syringe, 10 s release; 8 to 10 ml remains. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Specimen is smooth, glossy, uniform orange-brown, with no visible fish ball pieces, herbs, or oil pooling. On the spoon it mounds then levels in under 1 s. A lifted fork leaves a thin continuous trail that drops, not pours.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen runs off the spoon like a drink; the syringe empties below 8 ml; sieve residue contains intact fish ball chunks. Re-make from step 1, adjusting thickener in 0.3 g per 100 ml increments and re-sieving. A second failure of the syringe band means the batch is out of spec; re-blend fresh material.
