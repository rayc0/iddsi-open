PREP
Use whole blanched choy sum stalks as served, no cutting, no blending, no sieving, no thickener, no added liquid. Working mass 80–120 g per specimen; record actual mass on the scale. Keep stems intact so the fork-press lands on the natural stalk cross-section. Do not reprocess — L7 requires the food as normally served, and the per-level note explicitly keeps stalks as blanched.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press a standard fork flat onto a stalk with thumb pressure equal to pressing your thumbnail to blanch white. Pass when the stalk flattens, splits, or breaks; it must not return to its original shape. Spoon tilt: scoop a stalk and tilt the spoon 45 degrees; pass if the piece sits on the spoon without flowing or dripping. Fork drip: lift a stalk from the liquid and hold over a fork; pass when no liquid runs through the fork prongs in drips. No syringe test at L7.

VISUAL QC
Stalk sits flat on the fork tines without sagging. Cut end shows distinct fibrous rings under bright light. Surface holds a slight natural sheen from blanching, not a pooled sauce film.

FAIL MODES
Stalk bends but springs back — under-blanched, too elastic, re-blanch 20 s and retest. Stalk crushes to mush — over-blanched, discard and use a fresher bundle. Liquid pools and drips continuously through the fork — reassess as L6 or below, re-classify, do not label L7.
