1. PREP
Weigh 200 g total of tofu fa plus its syrup into the blender cup (record the syrup weight; expect 20–30 g). Pulse-blend in 1 s bursts for 5–8 s total — the per-level note is brief blend, blend-hold, so stop the moment the surface turns glossy and stop before it loosens. Do not exceed 10 s total, including any fail-mode remedy. Do not sieve — a 4 mm sieve will shear the curd into a pourable liquid and is incompatible with L4. Do not add water, milk, or extra syrup; do not add thickener. Hold the cup still for 60 s before use so the structure recovers. Spoon onto a white plate, mounding, for testing and capture.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip (applies at L3/L4): lift a loaded fork; the specimen must sit in a cohesive mound above the tines with no continuous flow; at most a single slow tail may form over the 10 s hold.
Fork pressure (primary at L4): press the fork tines into the mound; the surface must dent cleanly, the tines must blanch white, and no thin liquid may pool around the prongs.
Spoon tilt (applies at L3/L4/L5): tilt a heaped spoonful to 45° — the specimen must stay on the spoon with no run, at most a slow slump. Invert to 90° — it must hold a soft mound and must NOT pour off as a stream or slide off as one mass. No fork prongs should show through the specimen when lifted (L4 holds shape).

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, smooth, jiggle-stable surface; soft sheen, no matte dryness and no wet syrup halo. Mound keeps its peaks from the spoon, softening slowly at 90° inversion rather than dropping off in one mass. No curd lumps, no separated clear syrup around the base.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended → specimen flows through the fork tines continuously or pours off the spoon at 90°: discard, re-make with a fresh 200 g tub and only 5 s total pulse.
Under-blended → visible curds or lumps after the full 8 s: add ONE 2 s burst, hard limit 10 s total; if lumps persist, discard and re-make from a fresh 200 g tub with 5–8 s in 1 s bursts — do not keep pulsing a lumpy batch, extra blending loosens the curd past L4.
Syrup separated → drain off free liquid, blot, re-test; if it still drips continuously through the tines, re-make.
