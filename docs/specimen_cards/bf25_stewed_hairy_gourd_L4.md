1. PREP
Weigh 300 g stewed hairy gourd (skin + seeds removed, cooked soft). Add 60 ml hot water. Blend 45–60 s on high until fully smooth, scraping sides once. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Do NOT add thickener — hairy gourd puree already holds at L4. If the puree sits below the fork-tine line on the fork-drip test, remix with a 0.5–1 g thickener per 100 ml increment (calibration starting point only; record actual grams on the run sheet).

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate; white shows briefly through the puree and the puree does not recover fully — passes L4. Fork drip: scoop puree, turn fork tines down over a plate; the puree sits in a mound above the tines and does not flow or drip through — passes L4. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon; puree holds shape and does not slide off — passes L4. No separate thin liquid should pool on the plate. (10 ml syringe flow test is not required for L4.)

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale green-beige color with no visible fiber strands or skin flecks. Surface matte and slightly glossy, not wet or shiny. Holds a clean ridge when drawn with the fork tines.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny: puree flows through fork tines or pools liquid on plate — re-blend 15 s, re-sieve, or step thickener up by 0.5 g/100 ml and re-test.
Too stiff: puree cracks or crumbles on the spoon instead of holding soft ridges — loosen with a 5 ml water splash and re-blend 10 s.
Visible fiber or skin specks caught in sieve residue — re-sieve or discard batch; do not submit a coarse specimen as L4.
