1. PREP
Weigh 300 g stewed hairy gourd (skin and seeds removed, cooked soft), drained 2 min in the sieve so that the water below is the only added liquid. Add 60 ml hot water. Blend 60 s on high, stopping once at 30 s to scrape the sides down, until fully smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon, pressing until only fibre remains; discard the fibrous residue. The 4 mm sieve is the gauge for lump-free: puree that will not pass is re-blended, never pushed through as lumps. No thickener goes into the base prep — stewed hairy gourd puree holds at L4 on its own mucilage, and the fork-drip test in section 2 is the gate. If that test fails, correct it in this order: (a) re-make the batch with the hot water cut to 40 ml, then to 20 ml; (b) only if it still runs, add thickener weighed to 0.1 g in 0.5 g per 100 g of puree increments, log every addition in prep.thickener_g, rest 60 s, re-stir and re-test. Plate a 120 g aliquot for capture.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the bowl of the fork down onto the puree on a flat plate with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white; the puree squashes and does not recover — passes L4. Fork drip: scoop puree and turn the fork tines down over a plate; the puree sits in a mound above the tines and does not flow or drip through within 10 s — passes L4. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon to 90°; the puree holds its shape and slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue — passes L4. No separate thin liquid may pool on the spoon or the plate at any point before the clip ends. (The 10 ml / 10 s syringe flow test is the L3 criterion and is not used here.)

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale green-beige color with no visible fiber strands or skin flecks, and no particle larger than 4 mm on the cut surface of the mound. Surface matte and slightly glossy, not wet or shiny. Holds a clean ridge when drawn with the fork tines.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny — puree flows through the fork tines or separate liquid pools on the plate; the specimen is too thin, so correct by thickening: re-make the batch with the hot water cut to 40 ml, then to 20 ml, and if it still runs add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g of puree, weighed to 0.1 g and logged, rest 60 s and re-test. Too stiff — puree cracks or crumbles on the spoon instead of holding soft ridges; the specimen is too thick, so correct by thinning: add 5 ml water (measured, not splashed), re-blend 10 s, and re-run the fork-drip test, treating any run-through as the runny branch above. Visible fiber or skin specks caught in the sieve residue — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve, or discard the batch; do not submit a coarse specimen as L4. Separate thin liquid pooling after the puree rests — fails L4; skim and re-blend 15 s, or discard, and do not photograph a separated specimen. If the puree will not hold a mound even at 20 ml added water plus logged thickener, mark the cell FAIL rather than submit a runny specimen as L4.
