1. PREP
Weigh 200 g peeled soft ripe persimmon flesh; discard skin and any firm core. Blend 25-35 s in consumer blender until fully smooth, scraping once. Do not sieve — the per-level note says blend-thin, so fibre is driven by blender time, not sieve. Measure puree volume; target 100 ml. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 3.0 g per 100 ml, sprinkle while blending 5-10 s. Stir, rest 60 s, stir again. If the IDDSI flow test leaves less than 8 ml or more than 10 ml in the syringe after 10 s, adjust by 0.25 g per 100 ml increments and record the actual grams used. Re-test after every change.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: specimen flows slowly through fork tines in a continuous thin stream, not in dollops. Spoon tilt: spoonful sits in a mound and slides off slowly when tilted to 70-90 degrees, leaving a thick coating. Fork pressure: tines leave a clear, lasting line on the surface with no recovery. Syringe flow test: fill 10 ml, release, after 10 s 8-10 ml remains. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy, homogenous orange-tan surface on the spoon. No lumps, no skin specks, no liquid separation pooling at edges. Coats a fork evenly; no translucent thin patches.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny: passes 10 ml leaving under 8 ml — add 0.25 g thickener per 100 ml and re-test. Too thick: drips in dollops or syringe retains over 10 ml — thin with 5 ml water per 100 ml and re-test. Grainy or stringy: blender time under 25 s — re-blend 10 s. Any single failure: re-make, do not patch.
