1. PREP
Take 200 g thawed mantou. Cut into ~2 cm cubes. Place in blender; add 60 ml water. Blend 10 s. Gauge the mash against the 4 mm sieve instead of screening it (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the particles fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap of the spoon back. Do not press the batch through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Any lump held back on the mesh is re-chopped with the knife and re-mashed, never pushed through. Weigh the mash. Add water 10 ml at a time until the mash holds its shape on a spoon but a light shake flows, staying within L5 — do not exceed 100 ml total added water. Stir in 1.0 g thickener per 100 ml of mash and rest 60 s. Re-mash with a fork until uniform.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test (L5 proof): press fork tines into the specimen on a plate until the thumbnail blanches white. Specimen must squash and not spring back. Pass = visible deformation, no recovery. Spoon tilt test: load 5 ml onto a spoon, tilt to full vertical. Specimen must stay on the spoon without sliding or dripping. Pass = zero drop in 10 s. Fork drip test (absence-of-thin-liquid proof): not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence. No syringe test required for L5.

3. VISUAL QC
Mash sits in a soft mound, not a puddle; gloss on the surface but no free liquid pooling around it; tiny pale flecks visible at the sieve grain, all clearly under 4 mm against the ruler. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): lift fork holding 5 ml above plate for 10 s; pass = no drip falls.

5. FAIL MODES
Too dry: mash cracks on the spoon tilt; add 5 ml water and re-rest. Too wet: free liquid pools or drips from fork; fold in 0.5 g thickener and rest 60 s. Lumps >4 mm: re-sieve before filming.
