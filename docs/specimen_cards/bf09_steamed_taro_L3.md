1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed taro into the blender jar. Add 80 ml water. Blend on high for 45 s, stopping to scrape the walls once. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve, pressing with the back of a ladle; discard residue. Measure the volume of sieved purée in a jug and record it (target ~300 ml). Stir in thickener at 1.5 g per 100 ml of measured purée (grams = 1.5 × measured ml / 100) as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Stir for 30 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Calibrate against the pass window, not against appearance: if the purée pours freely in a fast stream or <8 ml remains in the syringe, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml and re-stir; if it drops off the fork as a lump or holds a mound on the 30° spoon, whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml. Record the final figure. Sieving is mandatory; coarser taro fibres will collapse the syringe reading.

2. IDDSI TEST
Applicable tests at L3 (CAPTURE_PROTOCOL §5.2, §5.3, §5.4): spoon tilt, fork drip, 10 ml syringe flow. All three must pass. Fork pressure is not an L3 test — do not run it, do not film it, and do not use its "no flow-back within 5 s" criterion to decide anything.
Fork drip: hold fork vertically 50 mm above an empty spoon; correct specimen flows in slow drips — a fine thread or separate drops — not a stream and not a lump drop.
Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped teaspoon 30°; specimen slides off slowly in a single ribbon, leaves a thin film. Pass when it flows slowly; a ribbon or slow trail IS the L3 pass. Fail if it pours freely and fast (too thin) or sits in a mound that does not move (too thick).
Flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe, release for 10 s; pass when 8-10 ml remains in the barrel.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and smooth, no visible fibre or specks. Drips slowly through the fork tines in a fine thread with no lump drop — it must not hang as a curtain. Coats the back of the spoon evenly, masking the metal, and slides off in a slow ribbon.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny (too thin): drip streams off the fork in a fast pour, or <8 ml remains in the syringe — add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, retest. Too thick: drops as a lump, holds a mound on the 30° spoon, or >10 ml remains in the syringe — whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml, rest, retest. Lumpy after blending: sieve failed — re-blend 15 s and re-sieve; do not photograph. If the specimen still will not sit inside the 8-10 ml window after two thickener steps (total ≤2.0 g per 100 ml added), remake from a fresh batch rather than chasing the syringe window with more thickener — that is how an L3 becomes an L4.
