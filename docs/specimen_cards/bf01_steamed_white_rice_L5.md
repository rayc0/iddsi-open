Applicable tests at L5: spoon-tilt (primary) and fork-pressure (supporting check only). The 4 mm sieve is a GAUGE in PREP, not a production step.

1. PREP

Bought form and allergens (docs/BASE_FOOD_LIST.md, BF01): cooked white rice, rice cooker or takeaway; allergen tag = none (operator still confirms the pack label at portioning). Per-level note for BF01 L5 is "chop to <= 4 mm, moisten with warm water" — both parts are performed here.

Weigh 150 g cooked white rice on the 0.1 g scale. Chop on the board with a small knife for 60 s, or pulse the blender 3 x 3 s (scrape and rest between pulses), until grains are broken to particles <= 4 mm but still visibly granular; stop before it turns into a sticky paste. Add 40 ml warm water and fold through with a fork until evenly moist; final specimen ≈ 190 g — record actual mass. SIEVE AS GAUGE ONLY: scoop one 15 g sub-sample and press it through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon. Pass = the sample passes with light-to-moderate pressure, confirming particles <= 4 mm; fold the passed sample back into the batch. If it will not pass, chop a further 30 s and re-gauge with a fresh 15 g sub-sample. Do NOT press the whole batch through the sieve and do NOT discard retained material — at L5 the <= 4 mm grains are the specimen. Thickener is not an L5 calibration step; if free moisture separates after folding, sprinkle in gum-based thickener at 0.5 g per 100 g of mash (0.95 g for 190 g), weighed to 0.1 g, stir, rest 60 s, re-test; cap 2.0 g per 100 g (3.8 g for 190 g). Record every addition in prep.thickener_g. Microwave 10 s before plating. Keep a 50 g reserve of cooked rice for a re-make.

2. IDDSI TEST

Spoon tilt (primary, L5): load a heaped teaspoon, hold level, tilt to 45°. Pass = the moist grains hold a loose mound that slumps only when shaken, with no separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon or tray; any pooling = fail.
Fork pressure (supporting check only, not the L5 discriminator): press the fork tines flat into the specimen until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass = the grains squash and do not recover shape when the fork is lifted.
Fork drip: skip — L3/L4 test only; an L5 specimen is a cohesive moist mince with no flowing stream to measure, so no drip is recorded.
Syringe flow: skip — the 10 ml / 10 s test is L3 only; do not run it and do not record it.

3. VISUAL QC

On the fork: moist grains, every particle <= 4 mm, visible granule texture, uniform damp sheen — matte-wet, never glossy with pooling liquid. On the plate: no shiny free liquid at the rim. The mound holds a fork-pressed indentation without crumbling or flowing. If the surface reads completely smooth with no granule you can see or feel, the batch has gone to L4 — correct per FAIL MODES before shooting.

4. FAIL MODES

Too wet — thin liquid pools on the plate or runs off the spoon (reads L4/L3, i.e. too THIN): sprinkle in gum thickener at 0.5 g per 100 g of mash, rest 60 s, re-test; cap 2.0 g per 100 g. Thickening moves the specimen toward L5.
Too dry — crumbs fall off the fork and no mound forms: fold in 5 ml warm water at a time, re-mash, re-test; cap 40 ml total added water. Moistening moves the specimen toward L5.
Grains above 4 mm on the gauge (reads L6, too coarse): chop a further 30 s and re-gauge with a fresh 15 g sub-sample. Never sieve the batch to correct this — sieving removes exactly the grains that make the specimen L5.
Over-processed to a smooth sticky paste (reads L4): discard and re-make from 150 g fresh rice with a shorter pulse, or fold through 50 g of freshly chopped coarse rice and re-gauge.
Any specimen failing its level test is re-made once from the reserve aliquot or marked FAIL in the log (tests.pass = false, reason in prep.notes); its media is never renamed into another level.
