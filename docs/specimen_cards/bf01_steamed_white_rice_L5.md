PREP
Weigh 150 g cooked white rice into the blender bowl. Add 45 ml warm water. Blend 20–25 s until coarse, sticky, with visible granules. Stop and check; rice must still feel granular, not puréed. Pass the blended rice through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard anything that will not pass or re-blend small residue for 5 s and re-sieve. Weigh the sieved mash. Stir in thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml of mash (record actual g used) to bind free moisture and reach minced & moist cohesion. Target final specimen ≈ 150–180 g. Warm 10 s before plating.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines flat into the specimen until thumbnail blanches through the food; the rice must squash, not spring back, and must not return to shape. Pass = full squash, no recovery.
Spoon tilt: scoop heaped, tilt spoon 45°; the mass must hold together and slide off in one clump, not flow or drip. Pass = cohesive clump, no run-off.
Fork drip: lift forkful 5 cm above plate; no thin liquid must drip within 10 s. Pass = zero drips.

VISUAL QC
Grainy texture visible but every particle ≤ 4 mm; uniform moist sheen, no shiny free liquid pooling on the plate; holds a fork-pressed indentation without crumbling.

FAIL MODES
Over-blended to a sticky paste that drips through sieve — discard, re-start with 150 g fresh rice.
Under-mashed with whole grains > 4 mm remaining on sieve — re-blend residue and re-sieve; do not just dilute.
Wet run-off on plate means thickener under-dosed — add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, re-mix, re-test within 10 min; beyond that, re-make.
