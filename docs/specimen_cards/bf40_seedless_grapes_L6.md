PREP
Wash 200 g fresh seedless grapes; pat dry. Halve each grape lengthwise, then quarter any piece exceeding 15 mm until every piece measures <=15 mm with the 100 mm ruler. Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add water or thickener. Discard any piece whose seed or fibrous core resists the thumbnail press.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the fork tines onto one piece with thumb force equal to pressing the thumbnail until the nail blanches white. Pass: piece squashes, does not spring back, edges crumble or flatten. Repeat on three pieces from different parts of the batch.
Spoon tilt: not applicable at L6 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence.
Fork drip: not applicable at L6 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence. No L3 syringe test applies.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: pieces hold a soft, moist cube-like shape with visible skin sheen and no juice pooling on the mat. Pieces should dent visibly under light thumb pressure and show a crushed, non-elastic cross-section, not a clean sliced face. Colour should match the starting fresh grape. By-eye check only (not a section 5.2 test at L6): load a teaspoon, tilt 45 degrees over the capture mat; pass: pieces sit cohesively without sliding off or dripping liquid. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L6): lift a loaded fork; no continuous liquid stream falls within 10 seconds.

FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm after quartering → re-trim and re-measure. Piece rebounds after fork pressure → grapes under-ripe or too firm; re-spec from softer fruit or discard. Excess juice pools on mat → pat pieces drier before retest.
