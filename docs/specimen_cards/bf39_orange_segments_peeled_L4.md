1. PREP
Weigh 200 g fresh peeled orange segments on the digital scale. Add 60 ml water. Blend 45 s on high. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard coarser residue. Blend the sieved puree 15 s more to homogenize. Serve immediately; do not hold. Per-level note compliance: this food cannot reach L4 without thinning and re-thickening — record as a failure if the operator judges texture cannot be met.

2. IDDSI TESTS
Fork drip test: tip a fork loaded with 3-4 g of specimen; it sits as a mound above the tines, does not drip or run through. Pass = no flow through gaps.
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch white. Specimen retains shape, does not spring back fully, leaves a clear indentation. Pass = clear thumbprint-style mark, no recovery.
Spoon tilt test: scoop 5-7 g onto a spoon, tilt to 90 degrees. Specimen sits, does not pour off. Pass = no slide.
No separate thin liquid must be visible in the bowl. If any liquid pools, FAIL.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: glossy orange mound, uniform color, no flecks of pulp or membrane, no liquid pooled at the tine bases. On the spoon: cohesive heap that holds its dome after tilting. Smooth surface with no graininess.

5. FAIL MODES
Liquefaction: puree runs through the fork — re-blend briefly with 2 g thickener per 100 ml, retest.
Graininess from membrane residue: visible pulp or fibrous flecks — re-sieve and re-blend; if persists, record FAIL.
Liquid separation in bowl: thin layer pools — re-blend and serve at once; second separation = FAIL, do not re-make.
