1. PREP
Weigh 200 g peeled hard-boiled egg (white and yolk). Chop on board with knife into pieces <=15 mm per the level note. Do not blend — the list note says chop, not puree. Do not sieve. Do not add water or thickener. Record actual mass of chopped specimen in g.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines onto a piece until thumbnail blanches white. Pass — piece squashes and does not return to shape; it breaks apart, not springs back. Spoon tilt: skip — L6 is not spoonable, so tilt is not the deciding test. Fork drip: skip — L6 pieces do not drip. Run fork-pressure as the primary test.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces are irregular, soft-looking, yellow-cream, with visible yolk and white mixed. Hold a piece on the fork — it visibly deforms under its own weight and when pressed. No liquid weeps from the piece. All pieces measure <=15 mm when checked against the 100 mm ruler.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces over 15 mm — re-chop. Egg rubbery and springs back under fork — egg overcooked, start again with a freshly boiled egg. Pieces crumble to powder rather than squash — egg undercooked, re-boil.
