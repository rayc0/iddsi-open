1. PREP
Peel and steam 300 g fresh taro until fork-tender (~20 min). Cube into ~2 cm pieces, add 60 ml warm water. Blend in the consumer blender on high for 45-60 s until smooth. Stop and scrape the wall at 30 s if needed. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue retained on the sieve. Weigh the dry sieve before first use and record its tare mass on the capture sheet, so retained residue can be weighed rather than estimated by eye. The target consistency is a soft mound, not a pourable puree. Do NOT add thickener at this stage — L4 taro holds shape from its own starch. If the mound slumps after sieving, measure the puree volume in a jug to the nearest 10 ml first, then work in 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml of that measured volume, blend 10 s, and re-test.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: mound on the fork sits in a clear pile above the tines, does not drip or run through gaps — pass.
Fork pressure: pressing the fork tines into the specimen leaves a clear indentation that does not bounce back fully; the surface cracks slightly — pass.
Spoon tilt: a heaped spoonful holds its shape when the spoon is tilted 45 degrees and does not slide off in under 10 s — pass.
Fork drip (tine-gap form): no puree flows through the 4 mm tine gaps when the loaded fork is lifted and held 5 s — pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Matte surface, no wet sheen. Uniform pale lavender-grey colour with no purple flecks. A peak stays where the spoon drops it.

5. FAIL MODES
Too wet: puree flows through fork gaps or drips — re-blend 10 s; if still wet, measure the puree volume and add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml of measured volume, then re-test.
Too stiff: fork cracks the mound cleanly with no give — add 10 ml water per 200 g of puree (15 ml for a ~300 g batch), blend 10 s, re-test. If it is still too stiff after 30 ml total added water, re-prep rather than keep thinning.
Lumpy: purple fibre strands visible — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve through the 4 mm sieve. Weigh sieve plus residue and subtract the recorded tare: retained residue must be ≤15 g (5% of the 300 g starting weight). If it is still above 15 g after that second pass, the batch fails — re-prep with a 25 min steam and a longer blend.
