1. PREP
Weigh 200 g braised beef brisket noodles into blender cup. Add 80 ml warm water and 6.0 g thickener (calibration starting point — record the actual mass used). Blend on high for 45 s, stopping to scrape down once. Add another 40 ml warm water and 2.0 g thickener; blend 30 s. Push through a 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard any fibres or noodle clumps that will not pass (per-level note: blend-thin — sieving is required because brisket sinew and noodle skins will not break down fully). Stir, rest 2 min. If the slurry pours in a slow ribbon and drips in 1-2 drops per second off the fork tines, proceed; if it runs or sits in a lump, adjust in 1.0 g thickener steps up to a maximum of 12.0 g total before re-blending 15 s.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork vertically over plate — slurry drips in slow, individual drops through the tines, not a stream. Fork pressure: press fork tines into 15 mm of slurry on a spoon — tines leave a clear outline that does not fill back in; slurry does not hold the fork up. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon 30° — slurry flows off slowly as one mass, not a fast pour and not a solid lump. Syringe flow (10 ml / 10 s): draw 10 ml, release, 8-10 ml remaining at 10 s — that is the pass band; under 6 ml = too thin, over 11 ml = too thick.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform brown, no visible noodle strands, no meat shreds, no skin pieces. Coats the back of the spoon in an even, glossy film. Flows off the spoon as a slow continuous ribbon that breaks after 1-2 cm.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin (<6 ml remaining) — runoff, no coating on spoon: whisk in 1.0 g thickener, rest 1 min, re-test.
Too thick (>11 ml remaining), lumpy, catches on sieve: whisk in 10 ml warm water, blend 15 s, re-test.
Visible solids after sieving — do not pick them out; re-blend 20 s with extra 20 ml water, re-sieve; max one re-make, then re-weigh and start fresh.
