1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed fish fillet, free of skin and bones. Add to blender with 80 ml water. Blend 45 s on high until smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using a spoon; discard retained solids. Weigh the sieved purée. Add thickener at 2.5 g per 100 ml of purée as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Stir 20 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. If flow test fails, add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml and repeat. Note: the per-level note states blend-thin; steaming yields soft flesh that blends to a pourable purée, so no knife work is required before blending.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork vertically; purée drips slowly in 3-4 drops, not a continuous stream. Spoon tilt: 5 ml on spoon; specimen slides off slowly when spoon is tilted, leaving a thick coating. Fork pressure: blade presses surface; purée yields to light pressure and spreads, leaving a clear imprint. Syringe flow: transfer 10 ml into the syringe, release for 10 s; 8-10 ml remains in the syringe. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale ivory, no flakes, no dark specks. Coats spoon back in an even layer with no run-off. Holds a soft ridge when fork is drawn through, ridge levels in 2-3 s.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny, passes through sieve as liquid, syringe empties below 8 ml: add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml, rest, re-test. Too thick, mounds on spoon, blocks syringe above 10 ml: add 5 ml water per 100 ml, blend 10 s, re-test. Grainy texture, dark specks visible after sieving: re-sieve or discard and re-blend with extra 20 ml water.
