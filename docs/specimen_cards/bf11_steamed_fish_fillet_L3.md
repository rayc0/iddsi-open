Target: IDDSI Level 3 — lump-free, flows freely, 8–10 ml remaining in the 10 ml syringe at t = 10 s.

1. PREP
Bought form: 200 g fresh (or fully thawed frozen) skinless, boneless white-fish fillet — cod, sea bass or similar firm white fish — as sold, unbreaded and unmarinated. Allergen: FISH; the specimen itself is the allergen, so do not substitute a non-fish protein.
Lay the fillet in a shallow heatproof dish and steam over a rolling boil, covered, for 10 min; the flesh must flake and show no translucency. Cool 2 min. Tip the fish and any released liquid into the blender, add 80 ml cold water, and blend 45 s on high, scraping the wall once at 20 s. Pass through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon and discard the retained fibres and skin so the purée is lump-free. Weigh the sieved purée (expect 220–250 g). Add thickener (modified maize starch or equivalent) at 2.0 g per 100 g of purée as the calibration start; stir 20 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s, then run the syringe test. If less than 8 ml remains, add a further 0.3 g per 100 g, stir 20 s, rest 60 s and retest, up to a ceiling of 3.0 g per 100 g. Record the actual grams used.

2. IDDSI TEST
- 5.4 Syringe flow — ACTIVE, the deciding L3 test. Fill the IDDSI 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release, and time 10 s. Pass = 8–10 ml remains in the barrel.
- 5.2 Spoon tilt — ACTIVE. Place a level 5 ml scoop on a spoon and tilt to 45°. Pass = the purée slides off slowly in one mass over about 2–4 s and coats the spoon; it must not pour instantly and must not sit unmoved.
- 5.3 Fork drip — ACTIVE. Lift a fork from the purée. Pass = the purée drips slowly in thin streams or single drops through the tines — not a continuous sheet, and not a mound that will not drip at all.
- 5.1 Fork pressure — skip: fork pressure is a Level 4/6/7 test (supporting check at L5 only) and has no pass criterion for a Level 3 liquid; do not use it to accept or reject this specimen.
All three active tests must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale ivory, glossy and completely smooth — no flakes, no dark specks, nothing catching the spoon. Coats the back of a spoon in an even layer with no bare streaks. A line drawn through the surface with the back of a spoon levels out within 3–5 s. No standing liquid separates from the mound on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin — syringe empties below 8 ml at 10 s, or the purée pours off the spoon in under 2 s: add 0.3 g thickener per 100 g, stir 20 s, rest 60 s, retest (ceiling 3.0 g per 100 g).
Too thick — the syringe has not moved at 10 s, or the purée sits as a mound that will not slide off the spoon: add 5 ml water per 100 g, blend 10 s, retest.
Grainy or flaky, or dark specks visible after sieving: re-blend 20 s and sieve once more; if still not smooth, discard and re-make from a fresh 200 g fillet.
Any specimen failing two consecutive tests is discarded and the batch remade.
