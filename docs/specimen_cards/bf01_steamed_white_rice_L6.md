**Bought form & allergens:** bought as dry long-grain white rice, cooked in water only — no oil, salt, stock, or sauce added. Allergens: none of the declarable major allergens (rice is not a gluten cereal); check the pack for wheat/soy cross-contact warnings.

1. PREP
- Weigh 75 g dry long-grain white rice, rinse, then simmer in 135 ml water, covered, 12 min; rest 5 min off the heat. Target: grains tender with no chalky core, surface matte and not sticky — this yields ≈200 g cooked. If starting from already-cooked rice, weigh 200 g cooked and skip this step.
- Turn the cooked rice onto a clean plate. Working in batches of ≤40 g, break the grains apart against the plate wall with the back of a fork (or a knife tip) until every piece measures 8–15 mm on the 100 mm ruler. Re-cut anything over 15 mm; discard anything under 8 mm (it reads L5, ~4 mm).
- Do not blend — blending makes rice paste, incompatible with L6.
- Do not sieve — a 4 mm sieve cannot enforce a 15 mm limit, and passing L6 material through one destroys the piece structure.
- Do not add thickener, stock, or any liquid — L6 is achieved by cutting, not by thickening.
- Weigh the finished specimen and record the mass, plus the grams discarded as fines.

2. IDDSI TEST
- ACTIVE — 5.1 fork pressure. Lay one 8–15 mm piece on a flat white plate. Press the fork tines flat onto it with thumb pressure until the nail blanches white; hold 3 s; lift. PASS: the piece squashes, does not spring back, does not return to its original shape, and does not smear into paste. Test 3 separate pieces; all 3 must pass.
- SKIP — 5.2 spoon tilt. Applies at L3/L4/L5 only; an L6 specimen is discrete pieces that must sit on a tilted spoon rather than flow, so the test cannot separate L6 from L7 — the 8–15 mm size window does that.
- SKIP — 5.3 fork drip. Applies at L3/L4 only; pressed rice carries no free liquid to drip through the tines, so the test always passes and carries no information.
- SKIP — 5.4 syringe flow. Every L3 only; 8–15 mm pieces will not enter or leave a 10 ml syringe.
- L6 verdict = 8–15 mm size window met AND 5.1 fork pressure passes.

3. VISUAL QC
- Grains are no longer intact; each piece holds its own shape, matte and yielding, not glossy or sticky.
- Every piece measures 8–15 mm against the 100 mm ruler — nothing above 15 mm, nothing below 8 mm.
- The pile on the fork sits in a heap with visible gaps between pieces, not a smear and not a single cohesive bolus.
- No whole grains visible; no free liquid or starch film on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
- A piece measures >15 mm: re-cut with the knife tip to 8–15 mm and re-measure against the ruler. Do NOT press harder to break it down — extra pressure makes paste (L5/L4), not smaller L6 pieces.
- A piece measures <8 mm: remove it from the specimen — it reads L5. Re-press the remaining grains more gently, in smaller batches.
- A piece does not yield to the thumbnail, or whole grains remain: the rice is undercooked. Return it to the pot with 20 ml water, simmer 5 min, drain 60 s, then re-press.
- A piece rebounds to its original shape after the press: undercooked or under-pressed; discard and remake from a batch cooked 5 min longer.
- The batch has smeared into paste: over-pressed. Start again from freshly cooked rice and stop pressing as soon as pieces reach 8–15 mm. Do not try to firm a paste back up with thickener — L6 is reached by cutting, never by thickening.
