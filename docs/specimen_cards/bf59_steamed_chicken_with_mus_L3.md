PREP
Weigh 150 g steamed chicken with mushrooms, place in blender. Blend 45 s, stop, scrape sides, blend 30 s more. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Add 60 ml water, blend 15 s. Measure volume of puree. Add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 ml of puree as the calibration starting point; sprinkle, blend 10 s, rest 60 s, re-blend 5 s. Record actual grams used. Target: coats spoon, pours in a slow ribbon.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: spoon puree, let it fall from fork tines; passes if it drips slowly in continuous blobs, does not run in a thin stream. Spoon tilt: tilt a spoon holding 5 ml; passes if puree slides off slowly, does not pour. Fork pressure: press fork tines into puree and lift; passes if tines leave clear indent, puree mounds without running. Syringe flow test: fill 10 ml syringe, release, read at 10 s; passes if 8-10 ml remains in barrel.

VISUAL QC
Smooth, glossy surface with no visible mushroom or chicken flecks after sieving. Holds a soft peak when mounded on spoon, then settles slowly. Color uniform pale beige, no separation of liquid around edges.

FAIL MODES
Puree too thin, drips through fork in a steady stream, or syringe reads below 8 ml — add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml, re-blend, re-test. Visible solids or fibers post-sieve — re-blend 20 s, re-sieve. Puree too stiff, mounds hold sharp peak, syringe reads above 10 ml — add 5 ml water per 100 ml, re-blend, re-test. Two failed re-makes: discard, restart prep, record new starting weight.
