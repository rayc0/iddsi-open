1. PREP
Weigh 250 g of mapo tofu (sauce and curds together) into the blender jar. Add 40 ml of water. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any residue retained. Return the sieved puree to the blender. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of puree (this is the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used). Blend 15 seconds to incorporate. Let stand 2 minutes. Re-weigh and record final specimen mass.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: lift a forkful; the puree must sit in a clear mound above the tines with no drip falling in 5 seconds. Fork pressure test: press the fork prongs into the specimen on a flat plate; the puree should yield and leave a clear indentation without running back to fill it. Spoon tilt test: a spoonful must hold its shape when the spoon is tilted to 45 degrees; no flow over the edge. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and uniform with no visible oil pools or tofu flecks. Color is even brick-red throughout with no white streaks. A forkful holds a soft peak that slumps only slightly; no liquid separates at the edge of the mound.

5. FAIL MODES
Puree is too thin and flows through fork tines: add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml increments and retest. Visible oil or thin liquid pools on the surface: re-blend with an extra 15 seconds and sieve again. Grainy texture from unblended tofu curds: re-blend 30 seconds and re-sieve. Any failure means re-make from the 250 g starting weight; do not patch.
