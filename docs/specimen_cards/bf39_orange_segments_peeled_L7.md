1. PREP

Remove any pith, seeds, or membrane; keep segments whole. Do NOT blend, sieve, or add thickener or water — L7 requires the food as normally served. Weigh 200 g of intact segments on the digital scale for the capture tray. This is a reference specimen, not a calibration sample, so no thickener grams per 100 ml apply. Set aside one whole segment aside for the fork-press close-up.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork pressure test: press the tines of the fork fully into one segment on a flat plate with enough force to blanch the fingernail. The segment must NOT deform, mash, or break apart; it must return to its original shape when the fork is withdrawn. Spoon tilt test: place a segment on a spoon and tilt to 90 degrees. The segment must NOT slide, drip, or run off. Fork drip test: lift a segment on fork tines and hold for 5 seconds — no liquid must drip through the tines. Syringe flow test is N/A at L7.

3. VISUAL QC

Segment holds a defined curved, glossy orange shape with visible juice vesicles on the surface. No white pith, membrane, or seed attached. No liquid pooling on the plate beneath the specimen.

4. SHOTS

Still 1: hero shot at 45° angle, ruler and 15 mm checkerboard visible in frame. Still 2: top-down with checkerboard only, segments spread but not overlapping. Still 3: fork-press close-up mid-press, fork tines clearly indenting one segment. Clip 1: 8–12 s fork-press and spoon-tilt sequence in one continuous take. Clip 2: full IDDSI test film including fork pressure, spoon tilt, and fork drip. Name all files: orange_segments_peeled_L7_take1.jpg / .mp4, increment take number on retake.

5. FAIL MODES

Segments break, crush, or weep juice under fork pressure — fails L7; re-make, select firmer fruit. Visible pith, membrane, or seeds remain — fails cleanliness; re-make and trim more carefully. Liquid pools on plate beneath specimen — fails fork drip; pat segments dry with paper towel before reshooting, do not re-make unless fruit itself is overripe.
