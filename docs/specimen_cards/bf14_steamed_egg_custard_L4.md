1. PREP
Crack 2 large eggs (~100 g) into a measuring jug. Add 150 ml room-temperature water and whisk until fully combined with no visible streaks. Pour through the 4 mm sieve into a heatproof bowl to remove any chalazae or unbroken yolk membrane. Steam covered for 12–15 minutes until just set; cool to room temperature. Weigh out 200 g of the set custard, transfer to the consumer blender, and blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth with no grain. Do NOT add thickener — egg custard at this ratio sets to L4 directly after blending. Record final specimen mass on the data sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen on a flat white plate with normal thumb pressure. Specimen must compress and leave a clear dent, but must not ooze liquid through the tines or run between the prongs.
Fork drip test: lift the fork with a loaded mound. The specimen must sit as a cohesive mound above the tines for the full 8 s hold; zero drip.
Spoon tilt test: scoop a heaped teaspoon, invert the spoon 90° over the plate. Specimen must hold on the spoon without sliding or pouring off. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and uniform pale yellow with zero white streaks or grain. The mound on the fork has clean, defined edges with no slump. A scooped portion drops cleanly off the spoon as one mass, leaving a thin, even film behind — no separation of clear liquid.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny after blending: egg-to-water ratio was off or steam was insufficient — re-make with 1.5 eggs per 100 ml water and re-steam.
Grainy or streaky texture: blender under-cycled or sieve skipped — re-blend 30 s and re-sieve.
Visible liquid pools on the plate after spoon tilt: custard was over-steamed and syneresis occurred — discard batch and start fresh.
