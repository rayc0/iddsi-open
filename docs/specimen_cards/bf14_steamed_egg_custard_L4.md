1. PREP
Crack 3 large eggs (~150 g) into a measuring jug. Add 150 ml room-temperature water and whisk until fully combined with no visible streaks. Pour through the 4 mm sieve into a heatproof bowl to remove chalazae and unbroken yolk membrane (this is a lump-free level, so sieving the raw mix is correct). Steam covered for 12–15 min until just set — the surface no longer wobbles as liquid and the centre reads 80–85 °C; cool to room temperature (~30 min). Weigh 200 g of the set custard into the blender and blend on high for 45–60 s until completely smooth with no grain. Do NOT add thickener — at 1 egg : 50 ml water the custard sets firmly enough to blend straight to L4. Record final specimen mass on the data sheet (target 200 g ± 5 g).

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure (primary at L4): press the fork tines into the specimen on a flat white plate with normal thumb pressure. The tines must blanch white, the specimen must take a clean dent and not spring back, and no thin liquid may run between the prongs.
Fork drip (applies at L3/L4): lift the fork with a loaded mound. The specimen must sit as a cohesive mound above the tines for the full 8 s hold; at most a single slow tail may form — no continuous flow through the tines.
Spoon tilt (applies at L3/L4/L5): scoop a heaped teaspoon, invert the spoon 90° over the plate. The specimen must hold a soft mound and must NOT pour off as a stream, and must NOT slide off as one mass; slow slump and flow down the spoon over the 8–10 s hold is the expected, passing L4 result. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and uniform pale yellow with zero white streaks or grain. The mound on the fork has clean, defined edges that soften slowly rather than slumping flat. On the spoon at 90°: the scoop holds a soft mound and flows slowly, leaving a thin, even film behind — it does not drop off as one mass. No separation of clear liquid.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny — flows continuously through the fork tines, or pours off the spoon as a stream at 90°: the egg:water ratio was low or the steam was short. Re-make with 4 eggs (200 g) per 150 ml water and steam to the same endpoint. Do not try to thicken an already-blended batch with starch.
Too thick — holds a stiff shape with no flow at 90° inversion, fork tines do not blanch: thin it. Re-make with 2 eggs (100 g) per 150 ml water, or blend in 10 ml water at a time (5 s each) and re-run the fork-pressure and spoon-tilt gates.
Grainy or streaky texture: blender under-cycled or sieve skipped — re-blend 30 s and re-sieve.
Clear liquid pools on the plate after spoon tilt: syneresis from over-steaming — discard the batch and re-make with 4 eggs (200 g) per 150 ml water, steaming no longer than 12–15 min.
