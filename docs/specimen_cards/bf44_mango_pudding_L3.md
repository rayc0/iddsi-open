Mango pudding — L3 Liquidised — desk-specimen capture (level L3; applicable tests: spoon tilt, fork drip, 10 ml syringe flow test)

1. PREP
Weigh 120 g of chilled mango pudding into the blender jar. Blend 20-25 s on high until completely smooth and pourable. Do not sieve — the gel matrix will clog the 4 mm mesh and stall. Add 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml of pudding (1.8 g total); this is the calibration starting point, record the actual grams used. Stir 30 s with the fork, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Target: pours in a slow ribbon, drips slowly through fork tines. Add water only if flow test reads above 10 ml; record any added ml.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: pudding drops slowly in thick drips through tines, not a steady stream. Spoon tilt: spoon at 45°, pudding slides off in 2-4 s as one mass, not a quick pour. Syringe flow test: fill to 10 ml, release 10 s, remaining volume 8-10 ml. All three must pass.
Fork pressure is not an L3 test (protocol 5.1 covers L4, L6, L7); no fork-pressure criterion appears on this card and none may be added as a gate.

3. VISUAL QC
On fork tines: glossy, uniform, opaque orange-yellow, no gel lumps or fruit fibre strands. 4 mm gauge: press a teaspoonful through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon — no discrete lump larger than 4 mm may remain on the mesh (a smooth film that passes slowly is expected and is not a lump). On spoon: holds a soft mound that begins to slump within 1-2 s and levels off; it must not hold a defined peak. In flow test syringe: no separation, no air pockets.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: reads under 8 ml in syringe, drips through fork in a stream — add 0.3 g thickener, stir 10 s, rest 60 s, retest. Too thick: reads above 10 ml, holds a stiff peak on the spoon or does not slide off within 4 s — blend in 5 ml water, rest 60 s, retest; water is the only permitted correction here, and no fork-pressure criterion may be invoked to rescue it. Lumpy or weeping: blending time too short or pudding over-thinned with water — discard and remake; do not re-blend a failed batch.
