PREP
Weigh 120 g of chilled mango pudding into the blender jar. Blend 20-25 s on high until completely smooth and pourable. Do not sieve — the gel matrix will clog the 4 mm mesh and stall. Add 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml of pudding (1.8 g total); this is the calibration starting point, record the actual grams used. Stir 30 s with the fork, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Target: pours in a slow ribbon, drips slowly through fork tines. Add water only if flow test reads above 10 ml; record any added ml.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: pudding drops slowly in thick drips through tines, not a steady stream. Fork pressure: full thumb-press blanches nail bed, pudding deforms and holds the pressed shape, no recovery. Spoon tilt: spoon at 45°, pudding slides off in 2-4 s as one mass, not a quick pour. Syringe flow test: fill to 10 ml, release 10 s, remaining volume 8-10 ml. All four must pass.

VISUAL QC
On fork tines: glossy, uniform, opaque orange-yellow, no gel lumps or fruit fibre strands. On spoon: holds a soft peak 3-4 s before slumping, surface smooth with no syneresis beads. In flow test syringe: no separation, no air pockets.

FAIL MODES
Too thin: reads under 8 ml in syringe, drips through fork in a stream — add 0.3 g thickener, rest 60 s, retest. Too thick: reads above 10 ml, holds stiff peak on spoon — blend in 5 ml water, retest. Lumpy or weeping: blending time too short or pudding over-thinned with water — discard and remake; do not re-blend a failed batch.
