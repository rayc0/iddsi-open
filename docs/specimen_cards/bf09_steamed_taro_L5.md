Applicable tests: spoon tilt (primary) and fork pressure (supporting check only). The 4 mm sieve is a GAUGE in PREP, not a production step. Fork drip and syringe flow are not L5 tests and must not be recorded as pass/fail.

1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed taro on the digital scale. Place in bowl and mash with a fork to a coarse, moist mash that still shows discernible 2-4 mm lumps throughout — do NOT mash it "until uniform": a uniform mash is the L4 puree, and at L5 the 2-4 mm lumps ARE the specimen. Pick out individually the woody core strands that will not mash — this is inedible-core removal, not a particle-size step. Re-weigh the mashable material, target 200 g. SIEVE AS GAUGE ONLY, AND IT IS A TWO-SIDED GAUGE: scoop one 15 g sample and press it through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon. (a) The sample must pass under light-to-moderate pressure, so no particle is over 4 mm. (b) Visible 2-4 mm lumps must be left on the mesh: a sample that passes clean with nothing retained means the mash has gone smooth and the specimen is L4, not L5 — stop and correct per FAIL MODES before shooting. Do NOT press the whole batch through the sieve and do NOT discard the retained material — at L5 the 2-4 mm lumps are the specimen. Add 20 ml warm water and thickener at 2.0 g per 100 g of mash (4.0 g for a 200 g batch), sprinkling it in while stirring; record actual water (ml) and thickener (g) used. Rest 60 s, stir again. If any piece fails the fork-pressure check, re-mash 30 s and re-gauge.

2. IDDSI TEST
Spoon tilt (primary, L5, 5.2): load a teaspoon, tilt to 45 degrees. Pass = soft moist particles hold a loose mound that slumps rather than pours, and the spoon is not swimming in separate liquid (5.2, L5). A continuous stream off the spoon is L4 — fail. A puree that holds a peaked shape and slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue is L4, not L5 — fail.
Fork pressure (5.1 — the supporting check for L5, and here the L5/L4 discriminator): press the fork bowl into the specimen on a flat plate with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white, then lift. Pass = the specimen squashes with no spring-back and visibly distinct 2-4 mm lumps remain on the fork and on the plate. A smear with no visible lumps is an L4 puree — fail.
Fork drip and syringe flow are not applicable at L5; do not record them.

3. VISUAL QC
On fork tines: uniformly tan-purple paste with discernible 2-4 mm lumps throughout and no lump above 4 mm, slight surface sheen from added moisture. On spoon: a loose mound of soft moist particles that slumps when the spoon tilts, not a peaked puree that holds its shape. No free liquid pooling at the rim of the plate. If the surface reads completely smooth with no lump you can see or feel, the batch has gone to L4 — stop, and correct per FAIL MODES before shooting.

5. FAIL MODES
Batch has gone lump-free and smooth (reads as L4): it was over-worked or over-sieved. Weigh out a further 50 g steamed taro, mash it coarsely with the back of a fork, fold it through the batch, and re-gauge. Never sieve to correct this — sieving removes exactly the lumps that make the specimen L5.
Woody core strands left intact: pick them out individually. Spot-picking inedible core is correct here; do not re-sieve the whole batch, which would also strip the L5 lumps.
Gauge sample will not pass the 4 mm sieve (side (a) fails): mash a further 30 s only and re-gauge once. If it still will not pass, the batch is too coarse for L5 — discard and record. Do NOT keep mashing to force the gauge: two or more long mashes destroy the 2-4 mm lumps and hand back the L4 puree this batch must not become.
Too dry, specimen cracks under fork instead of squashing: add 5 ml water at a time, re-test, cap at 40 ml total.
Too wet, free liquid pools at the plate rim: sprinkle thickener at 0.5 g per 100 g of mash (1.0 g for a 200 g batch), rest 60 s, re-test, cap total thickener at 3.0 g per 100 g. Never exceed the cap to clear pooling liquid: over-thickened taro stops reading as moist particles and reads as a cohesive paste — L4 — which fails VISUAL QC.
Any fail after two remakes, discard batch and record.
