Applicable tests: spoon tilt (primary) and fork pressure (supporting check only). The 4 mm sieve is a GAUGE in PREP, not a production step. Fork drip and syringe flow are not L5 tests and must not be recorded as pass/fail.

1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed taro on the digital scale. Place in bowl, mash thoroughly with fork until uniform. Pick out individually the woody core strands that will not mash — this is inedible-core removal, not a particle-size step. Re-weigh the mashable material, target 200 g. SIEVE AS GAUGE ONLY: scoop one 15 g sample and press it through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon. If the sample passes with light-to-moderate pressure, particle size is within L5 (≤4 mm); if it will not pass, mash a further 60 s and re-gauge. Do NOT press the whole batch through the sieve and do NOT discard the retained material — at L5 the 2-4 mm lumps are the specimen. Add 20 ml warm water and thickener at 2.0 g per 100 g of mash (4.0 g for a 200 g batch), sprinkling it in while stirring; record actual water (ml) and thickener (g) used. Rest 60 s, stir again. If any piece fails the fork-pressure check, re-mash 30 s and re-gauge.

2. IDDSI TEST
Spoon tilt (primary, L5): load a teaspoon, tilt to 45 degrees; the specimen must sit in a cohesive lump, not flow or slide off. Pass = mound holds shape, falls only if shaken.
Fork pressure (supporting check only, not the L5 discriminator): press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate with firm pressure; the specimen must squash and not recover shape. Pass = no spring-back, edges flatten.
Fork drip and syringe flow are not applicable at L5; do not record them.

3. VISUAL QC
On fork tines: uniformly tan-purple paste with discernible 2-4 mm lumps throughout and no lump above 4 mm, slight surface sheen from added moisture. On spoon: cohesive rounded mound that holds its peak after the tilt. No free liquid pooling at the rim of the plate. If the surface reads completely smooth with no lump you can see or feel, the batch has gone to L4 — stop, and correct per FAIL MODES before shooting.

5. FAIL MODES
Batch has gone lump-free and smooth (reads as L4): it was over-worked or over-sieved. Weigh out a further 50 g steamed taro, mash it coarsely with the back of a fork, fold it through the batch, and re-gauge. Never sieve to correct this — sieving removes exactly the lumps that make the specimen L5.
Woody core strands left intact: pick them out individually. Spot-picking inedible core is correct here; do not re-sieve the whole batch, which would also strip the L5 lumps.
Gauge sample will not pass the 4 mm sieve: mash a further 60 s and re-gauge; if it still will not pass after two mashes, the batch is too coarse for L5 — discard and record.
Too dry, specimen cracks under fork instead of squashing: add 5 ml water at a time, re-test, cap at 40 ml total.
Too wet, free liquid pools at the plate rim: sprinkle thickener at 0.5 g per 100 g of mash (1.0 g for a 200 g batch), rest 60 s, re-test, cap total thickener at 3.0 g per 100 g.
Any fail after two remakes, discard batch and record.
