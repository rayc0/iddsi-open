PREP
Weigh 150 g canned or frozen sweetcorn kernels, drained. Add to consumer blender with 60 ml water. Blend 45 s on high, then pass through the 4 mm sieve to remove skins and fibrous hulls per the per-level note; discard the sieve residue. Return the sieved puree to the blender and blend a further 30 s until smooth. Transfer puree to a small bowl. Add thickener at the L3 calibration starting point of 2.0 g per 100 ml, stir vigorously 15 s, rest 60 s, stir 5 s. If flow fails, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml increments, stir, rest, re-test; if too stiff, add 1 ml water. Record the actual grams of thickener per 100 ml and total water used on the run sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat white plate. Specimen must compress and leave a clear full tine-pattern indentation with no surface recovery for several seconds. Pass = clean impression holds. Spoon tilt test: scoop 5 ml onto a teaspoon, tilt to 90 degrees. Specimen must sit in a mound on the spoon, not pour or drip. Pass = holds mound without sliding. Fork drip test: lift fork from the bowl; specimen sits in a mound above the tines and does not flow or drip through the gaps. Pass = mound stays, tines visible underneath.

VISUAL QC
Uniform yellow colour, no visible yellow flecks, skins, or fibrous threads. Smooth glossy surface that mounds on the fork rather than coating it thinly. Holds its own shape on the spoon without pooling liquid at the edges.

FAIL MODES
Too thin, flows through fork tines or drips off spoon: add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, re-test; re-shoot if remake. Visible skins or fibrous strands after sieving: re-blend and re-sieve; one pass only, do not push residue through. Grainy texture with visible yellow flecks: blend a further 15-20 s and re-test; if still grainy, remake.
