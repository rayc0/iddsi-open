PREP
Weigh 200 g of stewed hairy gourd as lifted from the pot; do not blend, do not sieve, do not add water, do not add thickener. Transfer the whole chunks straight to a white plate. Keep pieces in the size they were cooked to per the food-list note "chunks as stewed" — no cutting down, no mashing. The reference specimen is the food as served.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press a standard dinner fork onto a chunk until the tines visibly blanch the nail of your thumb held beside the piece. Pass if the chunk does not fully squish flat between fork and plate. Spoon tilt: not applicable at L7 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L7 evidence. Fork drip: not applicable at L7 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L7 evidence. Syringe flow test is not required at L7.

VISUAL QC
Chunk holds its cooked shape on the fork with clear edges. Surface shows soft, moist stewed texture, not a smooth puree. Gentle press with fork leaves a visible indentation but the piece stays intact on the plate. By-eye check only (not a section 5.2 test at L7): tilt a dessert spoon 45 degrees; pass if the chunk sits on the spoon without flowing or dripping off. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L7): lift forkful 15 cm above plate; pass if no liquid runs off between fork tines.

FAIL MODES
Chunk collapses to puree under fork pressure — re-make and check cooking time was not excessive. Free liquid pools on the plate — drain briefly on the spoon before capture, re-shoot. Pieces cut smaller than "chunks as stewed" — re-make from fresh batch, do not re-cut.
