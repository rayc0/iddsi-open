PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed pumpkin flesh, no skin or seeds. Add 60 ml water. Blend 30 s on high. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Measure the sieved volume. Add thickener at 2 g per 100 ml of puree as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Stir 15 s, rest 60 s. If the slurry is too runny, add thickener 0.2 g at a time; if too stiff, add water 5 ml at a time. The puree must pour in a slow ribbon and drip through the fork tines in 1-2 s drops.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: the slurry drops in 1-2 second drips through the tine gaps, not a continuous stream. Spoon tilt: a 5 ml spoonful slides off a tilted spoon within 2-4 s, leaving a thin coating. Fork pressure: light thumb press on the spoon-blade leaves a clear dent that does not refill quickly; no crumb break. Syringe flow test: fill to 10 ml, release 10 s; 8-10 ml remains in the barrel. Pass only if all four match.

VISUAL QC
On the fork tines, the slurry hangs as a glossy, uniform orange ribbon with no fibre streaks or lumps. On the spoon, it levels flat with no peaks and no free liquid pooling at the edges. Colour stays steady pumpkin orange; no pale water rings.

FAIL MODES
Too thin, runs through tines in a stream or leaves under 8 ml in the syringe: whisk in thickener 0.2 g per 100 ml and retest. Too thick, sits in a blob on the spoon or leaves over 10 ml: whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml and retest. Grainy or fibrous after sieving: re-blend 10 s and re-sieve.
