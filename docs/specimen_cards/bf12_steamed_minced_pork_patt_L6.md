1. PREP
Weigh 200 g seasoned minced pork; form into a patty and steam 12–15 min until just cooked through (no pink core). Cool until firm enough for a clean knife cut (≈10–15 min). Do NOT fork-crumble. Cut the cooled patty into 8–15 mm cubes with a sharp knife, measuring each against the 15 mm checkerboard: every piece must be ≥8 mm AND ≤15 mm. Re-cut any piece over 15 mm; set aside any crumb that falls under 8 mm. Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve. Do NOT add thickener. Do NOT add water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure (the only L6 test): place 5 representative pieces (each 8–15 mm) in a row on a flat plate. Press the fork tines flat onto one piece until the thumbnail blanches white. The piece must squash and not spring back; lift and repeat on each piece. Pass = all five pieces squash and stay squashed.
Spoon tilt: not applicable for L6.
Fork drip: not applicable for L6.
Syringe flow: not applicable for L6.
L6 is confirmed by fork-pressure alone plus the 8–15 mm size window on the checkerboard — a piece must be neither over 15 mm nor under 8 mm.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces are irregular soft cubes; no strand longer than one checkerboard square and no crumb under 8 mm. Surface looks moist, not dry or crusty. Color is uniform cooked pork brown with no pink core.

5. FAIL MODES
Any piece over 15 mm: re-cut smaller, re-measure, re-shoot. Any crumb under 8 mm: the batch is too fine — re-cut from a firmer patty; do not certify sub-8 mm crumbs as L6.
Pieces spring back under fork pressure (under-cooked, too-dense mince): steam a fresh patty 2 min longer, cool until firm, re-dice — a too-firm piece needs more softening so it will squash.
Pieces crumble to powder (over-steamed): reform a new patty with 200 g mince, steam 10 min only — a firmer piece so the 8–15 mm cubes hold.
Reliability: steamed mince has a narrow softness window (springy if under-cooked, powdery if over-cooked). If a batch will not hold 8–15 mm cubes that squash without springing back, mince cannot be certified L6 reliably by this bench method — re-form with a firmer/coarser grind; do not shoot fines or springy pieces as L6.
