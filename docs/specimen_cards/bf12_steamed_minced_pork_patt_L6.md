1. PREP
Weigh 200 g seasoned minced pork onto a plate. Steam 12-15 min until just cooked through; cool 60 s. Crumble patty with a fork against the plate. Press and tear each crumble so every piece measures ≤15 mm against the 15 mm checkerboard squares. Pick out any strands longer than 15 mm and re-tear or discard. Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve. Do NOT add thickener. Do NOT add water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: place 5 representative pieces (each ≤15 mm) in a row on the 4 mm sieve. Press the fork tines flat onto one piece until the thumbnail blanches white. The piece must squash and not spring back; release and repeat on each piece. Pass = all five pieces squash and stay squashed.
Spoon tilt: not applicable for L6.
Fork drip: not applicable for L6.
Syringe flow: not applicable for L6.
L6 is confirmed by fork-pressure alone plus the ≤15 mm size check on the checkerboard.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces are irregular crumbs, no strand longer than one checkerboard square. Surface looks moist, not dry or crusty. Color is uniform cooked pork brown with no pink core.

5. FAIL MODES
Any piece longer than 15 mm: re-tear, re-measure, re-shoot.
Pieces spring back under fork pressure (under-cooked or too-dense mince): steam a fresh patty 2 min longer, cool, re-crumble.
Pieces crumble to powder (over-mixed or over-steamed): reform a new patty with 200 g mince, steam 10 min only.
