PREP
Start with 200 g of steamed chicken with mushrooms, keeping the cooking juices that accompany the dish. Strip any visible bone, skin, or gristle. Mince finely with a clean knife, then work the mince through the juices with a fork until the pieces are uniform. Pulse briefly in the consumer blender for 5-8 seconds only; the goal is to bring the particles to the 4 mm ceiling, not to puree. Press the mash through a 4 mm sieve, discarding anything retained above. Stir in 2-5 ml of the reserved juices per 100 g of mash to keep it moist; no thin liquid should pool on the surface. No thickener is required at L5; if the mash releases free liquid on standing, blot, do not add thickener.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat against the mash; the specimen must squash and the slots must be clearly visible through it. Release pressure; the mash must not spring back. Spoon tilt test: a teaspoon of the mash must hold its shape on the spoon when tilted, not flow or drip off. Fork drip test: lift the fork; no thin liquid should drip through or run off the tines within 10 seconds.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: cohesive clump with no loose drips, individual mince grains still visible but all smaller than a rice grain. On the spoon: a soft mound that holds its shape when tilted, surface glistening but no pooling liquid at the rim.

FAIL MODES
Particles above 4 mm after sieving: re-mince and re-sieve. Free thin liquid pools on the plate: blot and re-test, do not add thickener. Mash recovers shape after fork pressure: more fork work, then re-test.
