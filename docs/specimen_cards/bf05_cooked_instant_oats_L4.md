1. PREP
Weigh 100 g rolled oats into the blender. Add 200 ml hot water. Blend 45–60 s on high until visually smooth with no intact flakes; record the actual blend time. Sieve a 30 g portion through the 4 mm sieve — at L4 the specimen is lump-free, so the whole 30 g must pass the mesh. Do not press or force the portion through, and do not read pass-through as a defect: material passing through IS the pass. Pass = no more than 1 g (3 %) residue left on the mesh. If more than 1 g remains on the mesh, blend a further 30 s and re-sieve. L4 is a lump-free level, so the 4 mm sieve is the gauge here and it must run clean; a mound retained on the mesh is a fail, not a target. Thickener is not needed for the 1:2 base ratio; record 0 g/100 ml as the calibration starting point, and record the actual grams used if any is added at the FAIL MODES stage. No additional water or milk.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: lift a forkful and hold it above the bowl for 5 s — the specimen must sit as a mound above the tines and may shed at most a single dollop; a continuous stream through the tines is a fail. Pass = mound on the tines, at most one dollop in 5 s. Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate with thumb pressure — the surface must blanch white but the tines must not cut cleanly through; pass = blanch without full separation. Spoon tilt test: scoop the specimen, then tilt the spoon 45° — the mass must not slide, pour, or fall off; pass = holds shape with no flow. No separate thin liquid must pool at the rim of the specimen on the plate. No syringe flow test at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is uniform cream/beige with no visible oat flecks or bubbles. Coats the spoon back evenly with no run-back. Holds a peaked mound on the fork tines with clean edges.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen runs continuously through the fork tines, slumps off the tilted spoon, or will not hold a mound → too thin; THICKEN. Do not re-blend and do not add water. Stir in 1.0 g thickener per 100 ml of specimen (3 g for the ≈300 g batch), rest 2 min, and re-run all three tests. Tines cut cleanly through, or the mass is stiff and will not level on the plate → too thick; thin by stirring in 10 ml hot water and re-testing. Residue on the 4 mm sieve exceeds 1 g after the second blend, or visible oat flecks remain → under-blended; blend a further 30 s and re-sieve. White liquid pools at the plate rim → syneresis; discard and re-make with a fresh batch using the same 100 g oats : 200 ml water ratio, and record the syneresis in the log.
