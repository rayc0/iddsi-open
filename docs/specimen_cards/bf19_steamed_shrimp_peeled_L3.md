1. PREP

Weigh 150 g steamed peeled shrimp on the scale. Add to blender with 80 ml cool water. Blend on high for 45 seconds. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any retained solids. Weigh the sieved puree and add thickener at 3.0 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point (record the actual grams used; per-level note requires blend-thin, so adjust downward by 0.2 g increments if the mixture is too stiff). Stir 30 seconds, rest 2 minutes, stir again. Re-weigh final specimen and note total volume in ml.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork pressure: pressing the fork against a white plate leaves a clear pattern; the groove does not close. Spoon tilt: the specimen slides slowly off a tilted spoon, not a runny pour. Fork drip: drips slowly in continuous blobs through the tines, not a steady stream. Syringe flow test: fill to 10 ml, release; after 10 seconds, 8-10 ml remains in the syringe. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC

Surface is smooth, glossy, and uniform pink-white with no visible shrimp chunks or fiber specks. Coats the spoon back in an even layer without dripping off the edge. Holds a soft mound on a flat plate for 2-3 seconds before slowly spreading.

5. FAIL MODES

Too thick: syringe retains more than 10 ml; remake with 0.2 g less thickener. Grainy texture from incomplete blending: re-blend 15 seconds and re-sieve. Syneresis or weeping on standing: discard and remake with a slightly higher thickener dose.
