1. PREP
Weigh 150 g peeled dragon fruit flesh on the scale. Place in the blender; pulse 6–8 s only — stop while seeds are still visibly whole. Do NOT sieve; the 4 mm sieve is not used because the per-level note states tiny seeds pass through and must be recorded, not removed. Dice the pulsed pulp with a knife to <=4 mm, keeping its own juice. Mash firmly with a fork until uniformly coarse and visibly moist. Do not add water. Do not add thickener. Log the juice volume released (ml) and confirm no separate thin liquid pools on the plate.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen; it must squash and NOT spring back when pressure is released. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon to 45°; specimen must hold together with no drip, no run. Fork drip: lift fork and hold over plate 5 s; zero drops falling. Particle check: lift and inspect — visible bits must all measure <=4 mm against the ruler.

3. VISUAL QC
Coarse, glossy pink pulp with fine black seed specks evenly distributed. Clings to fork tines as a single mass. No free juice pooling under the mound on the plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended → smooth puree with ruptured seeds: fails fork-pressure (squirts) and particle check; re-make and pulse shorter. Under-processed → chunks >4 mm: fails particle check; re-dice finer, do not re-blend. Standing >10 min releases thin liquid: fails spoon-tilt and fork drip; re-mash and re-shoot immediately.
