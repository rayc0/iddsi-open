1. PREP
Open the ready-to-eat pack and weigh 200 g of black sesame paste into the blender cup. Blend on high for 20 s, scrape the wall, then blend 10 s more. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue. Do not add water, milk, or thickener — the per-level note says serve as-is if the paste holds shape with no separate thin liquid. Calibrate from this point only.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork loaded with paste; the mound must sit clearly above the tines with no drip falling in 5 s. Fork pressure: press the fork tines into the paste on a flat plate; the paste must hold a clear dent without separating liquid, and the tines must not cut through cleanly — the lump stays as one piece. Spoon tilt: scoop, then tilt the spoon 45°; the paste must not slide off, only a slow tongue of paste may move. No separate thin liquid should pool on the plate under the mound.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform matte black, glossy only where smeared, with no grain or husk specks after sieving. Spoon holds a sharp peak that slumps only slightly. Fork tines stay coated, never exposed by running paste.

5. FAIL MODES
Paste runs through fork tines or drips in under 5 s: too thin, add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-blend 10 s, retest. Gritty specks visible after sieving: sieve again or blend 10 s more before retest. Thin black liquid pools under the mound: too wet, do not add more paste to compensate — discard and start with a fresh pack.
