1. PREP
Open the ready-to-eat pack and weigh 200 g of black sesame paste into the blender cup. Blend on high for 20 s, scrape the wall, then blend 10 s more. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue — the sieve is a gauge for lumps only: sub-4 mm sesame grit passes it, so grit is controlled by blend time, not by sieving. The starting point is the paste as-is: add no water, milk, or thickener until the first IDDSI test has been run, and add nothing at all if the paste already holds shape with no separate thin liquid. If the fork-drip or spoon-tilt test fails, thicken from this point only, in 0.5 g per 100 g increments (1.0 g per 200 g pack), re-blending 10 s and re-testing after each increment, to a maximum of 2.0 g per 100 g. Water or milk are added only to thin a paste that cracks under fork pressure, never to rescue a separated pack. Record whether thickener or water was used, the grams and ml used, and the final specimen mass (g).

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork loaded with paste; the mound must sit clearly above the tines with no drip falling in 5 s. Fork pressure: press the fork tines into the paste on a flat plate; the paste must hold a clear dent without separating liquid, and the tines must not cut through cleanly — the lump stays as one piece. Spoon tilt: scoop, then tilt the spoon 45°; the paste must not slide off, only a slow tongue of paste may move. No separate thin liquid should pool on the plate under the mound.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform matte black, glossy only where smeared, with no visible lumps after sieving and no grain or husk specks — the 4 mm sieve removes lumps, not sub-4 mm grit, so grit is judged by eye on the plate. Spoon holds a sharp peak that slumps only slightly. Fork tines stay coated, never exposed by running paste.

5. FAIL MODES
Paste runs through fork tines or drips in under 5 s: too thin — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g (1.0 g per 200 g pack), re-blend 10 s, rest 2 min, retest; maximum 2.0 g per 100 g.
Too stiff — cracks or breaks under fork pressure instead of taking a clean dent: too thick — thin with 5 ml water per 100 g (10 ml per 200 g), re-blend 10 s, re-test; if it still cracks after 15 ml per 200 g, the pack is not usable for L4 — record and discard.
Gritty specks visible after sieving: the 4 mm sieve will not remove sub-4 mm grit — blend 10–20 s more and re-sieve; if grit persists, the pack is not usable.
Thin black liquid pools under the mound: too wet — do not add more paste to compensate; discard and start with a fresh pack.
