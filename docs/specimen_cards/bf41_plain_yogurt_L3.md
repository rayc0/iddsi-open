1. PREP
Weigh 150 g plain yogurt into a cup. Add 25 ml water and 30 g thickener (1.2 g per 100 ml starting calibration). Blend 30 s on low. Press through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard residue. Rest 2 min, stir 5 s. Record actual grams per 100 ml on the card. Per-level note: thinning with liquid + thickener applied to hit the flow window.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: 9-12 drips per 10 s when fork is lifted.
Fork pressure: thumbnail blanches under fork tines with light pressure, food keeps shape.
Spoon tilt: holds a mound on spoon tilted 30 degrees, does not slide off.
Flow test: fill syringe to 10 ml, release 10 s; 8-10 ml remains. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Smooth, glossy, uniform surface, no lumps or grain. Coats spoon back without running. Drips as continuous ribbons, not droplets.

5. FAIL MODES
Runny, falls through sieve fast, less than 8 ml in syringe: too thin, add 5 g thickener per 100 ml, re-blend 15 s, re-test.
Holds shape on spoon, syringe reads over 10 ml: too thick, add 5 ml water per 100 ml, re-blend, re-test.
Grainy or streaked: under-blended or under-hydrated thickener, blend 15 s and rest 3 min, re-test.
