PREP
Weigh 150 g chilled plain yogurt straight from the tub into a cup. Whisk 30 s until smooth and glossy — plain yogurt is homogeneous, so there is nothing to gauge or strain: do NOT press it through a 4 mm mesh (the sieve is an L4/L5 tool and there are no particles to retain). Served as-is this yogurt holds its shape on a spoon, which is the BF41 L4 form, so it must be thinned to reach L3. Thin with cold milk — not water, the per-level note specifies milk. Add 10 ml at a time and whisk 20 s after each addition, starting from 25 ml per 150 g; the fork-drip, spoon-tilt and syringe tests in section 2 decide when to stop, not the volume. If the mix pours too freely (syringe leaves less than 8 ml at t=10 s), bring it back with 0.5 g thickener per 100 g of specimen at a time, whisking 30 s and resting 60 s after each addition; never exceed 2.0 g per 100 g in total. No blending is needed. Rest 60 s, re-whisk 5 s, then run the syringe flow test as the deciding test. Record the actual milk ml and the actual thickener g per 100 g on the capture sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Spoon tilt (protocol 5.2, L3): scoop a full spoon, hold it level, then tilt. Pass = the sample pours off the tilted spoon in a slow continuous stream, and no separate thin liquid (whey) pools on the spoon or tray — any pooling fails the level. A spoon that holds its shape is the L4 form of this food, not L3, and must not be accepted here.
Fork drip (protocol 5.3, L3): scoop with the fork and hold it over the tray. Pass = the sample drips slowly through the fork tines in a continuous flow, leaving a coating on the fork. Counted single droplets are not the L3 look.
Syringe flow test (protocol 5.4, deciding test at L3): fill a 10 ml slip-tip syringe to the 10 ml mark, release with the nozzle open, and time 10 s. Pass = 8-10 ml remaining in the syringe (0-2 ml flowed out), the flow continuous through the full 10 s. Film the whole 10 s with the fill level at t=0 and the reading at t=10 both legible.
Fork pressure is not applicable at L3 (protocol 5.1 covers L4, L6 and L7, and L5 as a supporting check only): do not run it and do not record it as level evidence — a liquidised specimen must not be required to hold a pressure imprint. All three tests above must pass.

VISUAL QC
Smooth, glossy, uniform surface, no lumps, grains or skin. On the tilted spoon the mass pours as a slow continuous stream and coats the spoon back in an even film; through the fork tines it runs as continuous ribbons, not counted droplets. No whey or other separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon or the tray, and no standing pool on the plate.

FAIL MODES
Too thin (pours off the spoon in a fast stream; syringe leaves less than 8 ml at t=10 s, i.e. more than 2 ml flowed out): add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g of specimen, whisk 30 s, rest 60 s, re-test; repeat in 0.5 g steps only, and never exceed 2.0 g per 100 g in total.
Too thick (mound stands and will not move on the tilted spoon; syringe still reads 10 ml at t=10 s with no measurable flow): that is the L4 form of this food — thin with a further 10 ml milk per 150 g of specimen, whisk, re-test; do not add water, and do not pass the specimen as L3 while it still holds a spoon shape.
Whey separates and pools on the spoon or tray: whisk 15 s and re-test at once; if it re-separates within 2 min the batch has broken — re-make from a fresh tub, do not capture.
Grainy or streaked: under-hydrated thickener — whisk 30 s, rest 60 s, re-test; if grains persist, re-make.
