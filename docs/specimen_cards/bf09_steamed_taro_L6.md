PREP
Start with 250 g steamed taro. Cut into 15 mm cubes with a knife and ruler check; reject any cube above 15 mm. Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add thickener or liquid — L6 pieces must hold shape under fork pressure, and liquid would mask the failure mode we are capturing. Do not mash, rice, or pulse. Keep cubes whole. Portion 8–10 cubes (≈60–80 g) onto the fork for the press test; keep remainder on plate for hero and top-down stills. If any cube exceeds 15 mm after cutting, re-trim before capture.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the tines into a cube with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: cube squashes, breaks apart, does not return to original shape. Cube must break under thumbnail pressure, not just dent. Size check: all cubes measure ≤15 mm on the ruler — any cube above is a fail. Spoon tilt is not the gate test for L6 but perform it on a single cube to confirm the piece does not flow; it should sit and slide, not pour. Fork drip is not applicable — pieces do not drip.

VISUAL QC
Cube edges are soft, slightly rounded, no crisp raw-looking corners. Surface looks moist but not wet or pooled with liquid. Colour is even pale lavender-grey with no dark firm core visible at the cut face. Cube compresses visibly under fork with no spring-back.

FAIL MODES
Cube exceeds 15 mm — re-trim and reshoot. Cube springs back under thumbnail — pieces under-steamed, re-steam 5 min and reshoot. Cube crumbles to paste before pressure — over-steamed, cut from a fresh batch at 12 min steam.
