1. PREP
Weigh out 200 g of braised beef brisket noodles onto the capture tray. Using the fork, separate the noodles and brisket; cut noodles with the fork edge and dice brisket pieces so every dimension is ≤15 mm — discard any piece that exceeds 15 mm, do not blend and do not sieve (sieving would destroy the noodle form required for L6). No thickener, no added water. Specimen is taken as served from the takeaway container.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: place one diced brisket piece on a flat plate, press the fork tines flat until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass = piece squashes, does not recover its shape, and the fork edge does not fully cut through it. Spoon-tilt test (noodle strand): not applicable at L6 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence. Fork-drip test: not applicable at L6 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence. All three must pass to confirm L6.

3. VISUAL QC
— Noodle strands visibly cut to short lengths ≤15 mm; no long strands hanging off the fork.
— Brisket dice uniform, roughly cube-shaped, no piece visibly larger than the 15 mm squares on the checkerboard beside the specimen.
— Surface glistening but no pooled liquid on the tray. By-eye check only (not a section 5.2 test at L6): load a noodle strand on the spoon and tilt; the strand must sit cohesively and slide off in one motion, not flow, drip, or run. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L6): lift a forkful; little to no liquid drips through the tines before the fork-pressure test is performed.

5. FAIL MODES
Noodle strands left longer than 15 mm after cutting — re-cut, do not re-plate. Brisket piece resists the fork-press and springs back, indicating under-cook or wrong cut size — re-cut smaller and retest. Pooled broth on the tray causing fork-drip fail — drain briefly on absorbent paper and re-shoot.
