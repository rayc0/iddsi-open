1. PREP
- Weigh 200 g of chilled coconut jelly directly from the pack.
- Cut into cubes on a clean board with a chef's knife. Trim so every cube is ≤15 mm on all sides; discard any piece over 15 mm or with a hard skin.
- Do NOT blend. Blending would destroy the cube structure and is incompatible with L6.
- Do NOT sieve. Cubes must remain intact.
- Do NOT add thickener or liquid. L6 calibration starts dry.
- Record final specimen mass and the number of cubes produced.

2. IDDSI TEST
- Fork pressure: press the fork tines firmly onto one cube against a white plate until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: cube squashes, does not spring back, does not return to original shape when fork is removed. If it bounces back, re-test on a fresh cube; if it still recovers, re-make with a colder pack.
- Fork drip test: lift five cubes on the fork; no liquid should drip through the tines. Pass: no drip within 10 s.
- Spoon tilt is not required for L6; skip it.

3. VISUAL QC
- Cubes hold their shape on the fork, glossy and translucent, edges clean and ≤15 mm.
- Surface looks moist but no free liquid pools on the plate.
- Pressed cube leaves a visible flattened dent that stays.

5. FAIL MODES
- Cubes >15 mm: re-cut and re-measure; re-shoot only.
- Cube recovers shape after fork pressure (rubbery gel): chill the pack 30 min longer, then re-cut and re-test.
- Liquid weeps onto plate: drain cubes on paper towel 30 s, blot gently, re-shoot.
