1. PREP
Boil 60 g dry thin wheat noodles (or 150 g fresh) in 1 L unsalted water until just tender to the bite. Drain, shake off surface moisture for 30 s, portion 30 g onto the fork and plate. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener. Target is whole noodles as normally served; no processing is permitted at L7.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines fully into the specimen; noodles compress/deform and change colour at the bite mark, and do not return to shape when fork is withdrawn. Pass = clear bite mark visible, noodle holds together, no recovery.
Spoon tilt: spoon a small portion and tilt to 45 degrees; noodles slide off in a bolus and fall. Pass = full slide in 1-2 s.
Fork drip: lift forkful clear of plate; no continuous drip from noodles between fork and plate. Pass = no drip.

3. VISUAL QC
- Noodles are distinct, firm strands, ~2 mm diameter, pale yellow, no clumping or mash.
- Bite mark on fork shows dented, lighter-coloured core; strand does not spring back.
- Loose strands lift cleanly off plate; no liquid pooling.

5. FAIL MODES
1. Overcooked: noodles squash fully under fork with no spring, pass fork pressure by mashing — re-cook a fresh batch to 1 min less.
2. Sauce or oil film present: noodles slide off spoon without bite test, dripping observed — drain 60 s longer, blot, re-shoot.
3. Strands stuck together in a clump: cannot isolate a single forkful — re-portion with tongs, reject clump, re-shoot.

Re-make rule: any single fail = discard specimen, re-cook, re-shoot full set; do not patch a take.
