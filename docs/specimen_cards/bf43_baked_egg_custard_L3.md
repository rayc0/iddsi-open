1. PREP
Weigh 200 g of chilled baked egg custard into the blender cup. Add 30 ml water. Blend 25-30 s on high until fully smooth and pourable. Sieve through the 4 mm sieve into a bowl; press with the back of a spoon to pass; discard residue. Weigh the sieved liquid on the digital scale. Stir in thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml of sieved liquid as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Stir 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 5 s. If the IDDSI flow test shows more than 10 ml retained, add thickener in 0.1 g per 100 ml increments, stir, rest, retest. If less than 8 ml is retained, thin with 2-3 ml water, stir, rest, retest.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a forkful 50 mm above the plate; liquid drips slowly in a continuous thin stream through the tines. Fork pressure: the fork tines leave a clear, persistent impression on the surface when pressed, and the surface does not flow back to level. Spoon tilt: on a spoon held at 45 degrees, the specimen sits in a mound and slides off slowly as a cohesive blob, not as a fast pour. IDDSI 10 ml syringe flow test: fill the syringe to the 10 ml mark, release for 10 s; 8-10 ml remains in the syringe.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a smooth glossy film that drips slowly without breaking into droplets. On the spoon: a soft, rounded mound that holds shape then slumps. In the sieve residue: minimal, only fine specks.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: flows like cream, leaves under 8 ml in the syringe, runs off a tilted spoon in under 1 s. Re-make: add thickener at 0.1 g per 100 ml increments. Too thick: sits as a stiff blob on the spoon, syringe retains over 10 ml, fork drip stops. Re-make: thin with 2-3 ml water per 100 ml and retest. Grainy or lumpy: visible specks after sieving, residue over roughly 10 percent of starting weight. Re-make: blend an extra 10 s and re-sieve; if it persists, the batch is rejected and a new 200 g portion is weighed out.
