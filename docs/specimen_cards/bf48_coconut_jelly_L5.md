1. PREP
Weigh 200 g chilled coconut jelly directly from the pack. Chop finely by hand with a knife on a white plate, then crush lightly with the back of the fork, pressing against the plate and scraping — stop while visible 2-4 mm lumps remain; do not keep crushing to a smooth paste. If blending, pulse 3-5 s only; stop and re-check with the sieve. Sieve check (gauge, not a filter): press one spoonful of the mince against the 4 mm mesh with the back of a spoon. Pass = the mash passes under pressure while visible 2-4 mm lumps remain in the bowl and nothing coarser than 4 mm is retained. Do NOT pass the whole batch through the sieve and discard what is retained — L5 must contain ~4 mm lumps, and a clean lump-free pass is L4. If the pack liquid runs thin and pools, fold in thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml of expressed liquid and stir until homogeneous. Record actual thickener mass, any added liquid (ml), and final weight (g) on the card.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure (supporting check only at L5): press the tines into a heaped spoonful until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass: the tines mash the visible lumps and leave a clear print; the specimen does not spring back.
Spoon tilt: scoop, then tilt the spoon 45 degrees. Pass: specimen sits in a mound, holds shape, and does not slide or drip as a continuous pour; a small tongue of paste may slump but no separate thin liquid flows.
Particle size: confirm visually against the 4 mm sieve reference — single lumps pass the 4 mm mesh when pressed, and the bulk still shows visible ~4 mm lumps.

3. VISUAL QC
Even pale white to translucent moist mince with visible ~4 mm lumps. No reflective pools of free liquid in the plate. A pressed smear must show fine visible lumps, not a continuous film — a continuous film with no lumps means the specimen has been taken to L4, not L5.

5. FAIL MODES
Clear liquid separates, or the specimen slides off the spoon as a continuous pour — too wet: re-mix; if liquid still pools, fold in thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml of expressed liquid in 0.25 g/100 ml increments until the spoon-tilt test holds. Do not add water.
Pieces above 4 mm — too coarse: mince finer by hand until the largest piece is ~4 mm. Do not fix this by sieving the batch to a clean pass; that removes the lumps and produces L4.
Smear reads as a continuous film with no visible lumps, or the sieve passes with nothing retained — the specimen is L4, not L5: it cannot be re-lumped; re-make by hand mincing only and re-check with the sieve gauge at ~4 mm.
Springy, recovers shape on the fork test — over-blended: re-make by hand chopping only, no blender. Water will not fix this and will create free liquid.
