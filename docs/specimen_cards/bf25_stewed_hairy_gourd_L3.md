1. PREP
Weigh 250 g stewed hairy gourd (including liquor) on the digital scale. Blend 30-45 s until fully smooth. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve, pressing with the back of a spoon; discard retained fibre. Add the calibration starting point of 3.0 g thickener per 100 ml of sieved puree, stirring to fully disperse. The per-level note permits blend-thinning, so add 50 ml water to bring the puree to pouring consistency, then re-stir. Record the actual thickener mass used. The specimen must pour in a steady stream from the cup.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: tilt the fork; the puree drips slowly in a continuous, thick ribbon, not a thin stream. Fork pressure test: pressing the fork against the cup leaves a clear pattern that does not recover; the puree does not flow back into the grooves. Spoon tilt test: a spoonful holds shape when the spoon is tilted, then drops off as one cohesive lump. IDDSI syringe flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe, release for 10 s; 8-10 ml must remain in the syringe. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Even pale-green puree with no visible fibre. Smooth, glossy surface, no liquid separating around the edges. Coats the back of the spoon in a thin, even layer.

4. SHOTS
Still 1, 45-degree hero, ruler and checkerboard in frame. Still 2, top-down, checkerboard centred. Still 3, fork-press close-up, mid-press. Clip 1, 8-12 s fork-press and spoon-tilt. Clip 2, full IDDSI 10 ml syringe flow test, timer visible. Name all files stewed_hairy_gourd_L3_take1.jpg or .mp4.

5. FAIL MODES
Thin and runny like a drink: sieve was skipped or thickener under-dosed; re-make and add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml. Lumpy with visible fibre: blend time too short; re-blend 20 s and re-sieve. Liquid pools around puree on the spoon: water added before thickener, or under-gelled; re-make from scratch.
