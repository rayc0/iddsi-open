1. PREP
Weigh 200 g peanut paste into blender. Add 80 ml water. Blend 30 s until smooth. Pass through 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue. Measure sieved volume. Add thickener at 3 g per 100 ml of sieved volume as the calibration starting point; whisk briskly for 20 s, rest 60 s, then whisk 10 s more. Record the actual thickener mass and final volume on the card. If the 10 ml/10 s syringe leaves less than 8 ml, add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-whisk. If it leaves more than 10 ml, add 5 ml water per 100 ml and re-whisk. Target pour: continuous ribbon that breaks within 1-2 cm of the surface.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork clear of cup; liquid should drip slowly in thick strands, not run as a steady stream. Spoon tilt: spoon at 45 degrees; specimen slides off slowly, not in one quick sheet. Fork pressure: pressed flat, the surface shows clear tine marks with no spring-back; thumb-blanket lift holds shape only briefly. Syringe flow test: fill 10 ml, release 10 s; pass = 8-10 ml remaining in the syringe barrel. All four must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform tan, glossy, no oil pooling on the surface. No visible peanut grit on fork tines after drip. Coats the spoon back evenly with no bare metal showing.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thick, leaves more than 10 ml in syringe or sits in a mound on the fork: thin with 5 ml water per 100 ml, re-whisk, re-test. Too thin, runs through fork tines and leaves less than 8 ml: add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, re-whisk, rest 60 s, re-test. Gritty or oily with visible separation: re-blend and re-sieve before judging. Re-make from scratch after two failed corrections.
