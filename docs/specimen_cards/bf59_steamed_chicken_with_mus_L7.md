PREP
Take 250 g of steamed chicken with mushrooms as served from the home-cooked batch. Do not blend, sieve, or add thickener — L7 is the unmodified reference specimen. Place the chicken piece with mushrooms on a plate, keep sauce that clings naturally to the food; do not drain or pat dry. Record actual mass on the digital scale before plating. Set the A4 capture kit, ruler, and 15 mm checkerboard beside the plate within one arm's reach. If a blender step is attempted, stop: L7 forbids processing — re-make from fresh.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the four tines flat onto the chicken surface with steady thumb force. The food must not fully compress, ooze liquid, or pass between tines when lifted. Fork drip: lift the fork vertically above the plate; no continuous drip should fall — only clinging sauce is acceptable. Spoon tilt: rest a spoonful on the spoon and tilt 45 degrees; the chicken must sit stably without sliding off. All three pass criteria are required; if any fails, re-classify and re-make.

VISUAL QC
Chicken holds its shape on the fork without slumping. Mushrooms remain intact and distinguishable. Surface looks moist, not pooling.

FAIL MODES
Over-steamed chicken collapses under fork — re-make with shorter steam. Loose sauce runs off fork — spoon off excess before test. Mushrooms turn to puree — pick firmer specimens.
