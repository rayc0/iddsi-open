PREP
Weigh 200 g fresh peeled orange segments on the scale. Do not blend; L6 requires visible soft pieces. Working over the tray, cut every segment crosswise into pieces no larger than 15 mm using the fork edge as a gauge against the 100 mm ruler — reject any seed, membrane strand, or piece exceeding 15 mm. If the per-level note applies, sizing is the only mechanical step; sieving is impossible for solid fruit, so do not sieve. No thickener, no water, no liquid added — L6 specimen is served moist only as the fruit's own juice. Do not perform the L3 calibration step; it does not apply to L6. Wipe the tray rim dry before capture so juice does not lift the checkerboard.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat onto a single 15 mm piece until your thumbnail blanches white. Pass when the piece squashes, does not spring back, and spreads cleanly without releasing free liquid. Spoon tilt test: load a teaspoon, tilt to 90 degrees over the tray. Pass when the piece slides as a solid lump, not as separate drops or flowing liquid. Fork drip test is not diagnostic at L6; do not weigh it as evidence. No syringe flow test; L3 only.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: one soft cube, edges rounded by the cut, no juice pool on the fork face, no seed visible. On the spoon: piece holds its cut shape until tilted, then releases as a lump. A correct specimen looks moist, never wet, and never fibrous.

FAIL MODES
Piece exceeds 15 mm — re-cut and re-weigh. Piece springs back under fork or leaks free juice — re-cut smaller, discard the wet batch. Seed or membrane strand present — strip and re-cut.
