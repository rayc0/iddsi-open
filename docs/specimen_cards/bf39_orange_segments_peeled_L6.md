PREP
Weigh 200 g fresh peeled orange segments on the scale — ripe, sweet segments with seeds and membrane strands stripped. Do not blend; L6 requires visible soft pieces. Working over the tray, cut every segment crosswise on the board with a knife into pieces measuring 8-15 mm on the longest edge, checking each cut batch against the 100 mm ruler in frame; reject any seed, membrane strand, or piece longer than 15 mm. Never cut a piece below 8 mm: the BF39 L5 form is a chop to <=4 mm with the fruit's own juice, so a batch worked down toward 4 mm is an L5 specimen and must be filed as L5, not L6. Sieving is impossible for solid fruit, so do not sieve — a 4 mm mesh cannot enforce a 15 mm limit and would shred the batch to L5. No thickener, no water, no liquid added — the L6 specimen is served moist only as the fruit's own juice. Do not perform the L3 calibration step; it does not apply to L6. Lift off any juice that has pooled on the tray just before capture, and capture promptly, so the plate reads moist and not swimming. Wipe the tray rim dry before capture so juice does not lift the checkerboard.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure is the only IDDSI test that applies at L6 (protocol 5.1): press the flat of the fork onto one 8-15 mm piece, pressure through the thumbnail until the thumbnail blanches white, hold, then lift. Pass = the piece squashes, changes shape, and does not recover its shape when the fork is lifted. Juice that squeezes out under the press is the fruit's own juice and does NOT fail the piece: the L6 form of this food is cut segments served in their own juice, and the fork-pressure test carries no "no free liquid" clause — do not re-cut a piece smaller to chase a drier press. Size confirmation in the same take (protocol 5.1, L6 sizing check): hold two or three pieces against the 100 mm ruler; every piece measures <=15 mm on its longest edge and none is below 8 mm. Spoon tilt is not an L6 test (protocol 5.2 covers L3, L4 and L5 only): do not run it and do not record it as level evidence. Fork drip is not an L6 test (5.3 is L3 and L4). No syringe flow test; L3 only.

VISUAL QC
Pieces read as soft cut cubes, 8-15 mm on the ruler, edges clean-cut and not ragged, no piece fragmented toward 4 mm. Moist with the fruit's own juice, never wet: no pool of separated juice standing on the plate at the moment of capture. No seed and no membrane strand visible on any piece. Colour uniform, no bruised or collapsed cut faces.

FAIL MODES
Piece exceeds 15 mm — re-cut to 8-15 mm and re-verify every piece against the ruler. Piece springs back under fork pressure — the segment is firm or under-ripe, which is whole-segment (L7) material, not L6: use riper segments for the L6 specimen; do NOT re-cut smaller to force a squash, and if no ripe fruit can be sourced, file the specimen at the level its filmed test actually supports rather than forcing the L6 label. Juice squeezes out under the fork — expected for orange and not a failure; only a pool of separated juice standing on the plate fails QC: lift it off, capture promptly, and never shrink pieces below 8 mm to reduce juiciness. Seed or membrane strand present — strip and re-cut. Batch has fragmented to <=4 mm — that is the BF39 L5 form (chop <=4 mm with its juice): re-test it and file it as L5, never as L6.
