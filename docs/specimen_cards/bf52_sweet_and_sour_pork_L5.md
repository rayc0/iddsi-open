1. PREP
Weigh 250 g takeaway sweet and sour pork (meat + sauce) on the digital scale. Pick out only the pork pieces; discard loose sauce pooling in the container. Place pork in the consumer blender. Pulse 10 s, scrape down, pulse 10 s until coarsely minced. Gauge the mince against the 4 mm sieve instead of screening it (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the particles fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap of the spoon back. Do not press the batch through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Re-chop what stays behind and gauge again. Return the mince to the bowl. Mash with a fork for 30 s, pressing firmly. Slowly fold in 30 ml of the original sauce to moisten (do not add water). L3 thickener is not required for L5; this is the calibration starting point and the operator records the actual sauce added.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen until the thumbnail blanches white; the mince must squash, not recover, and the tines must leave a clear imprint. Spoon-tilt test: a small mound on a spoon must hold shape when tilted 45°; it may slide slowly but must not pour. Fork-drip test: not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence. If any of these fail, the specimen is not L5.

3. VISUAL QC
Mince sits as soft, distinct particles all visibly ≤4 mm. Surface looks glossy-moist, never dry or crumbly, and never sits in pooled sauce on the plate. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): lift forkful; no thin liquid must drip through the tines.

5. FAIL MODES
Particles >4 mm after sieving — re-blend 5 s and re-sieve; do not skip sieving. Too dry, crumbs fall off fork — fold in another 5–10 ml sauce and re-test. Loose thin sauce pools on the plate — drain before plating; L5 must hold sauce within the mince, not separate.
