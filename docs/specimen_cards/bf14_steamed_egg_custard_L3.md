PREP — Weigh 200 g set steamed egg custard (eggs + water, steamed to set). Break into chunks. Add 120 ml cool water. Blend in consumer blender 30 s, scrape, 15 s more. Sieve through 4 mm sieve into a bowl; press soft residue through with the back of a spoon, discard coarse that won't pass. Add thickener at 3.0 g per 100 ml of thinned custard as the calibration starting point (record actual g used on the data card). Whisk 20 s, rest 2 min, stir again. Adjust with small thickener pinches if it drips too fast, or 5 ml water if too stiff. The per-level note says blend-thin, so sieve is the only texture step.

IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure: a 4 mm line of custard on the spoon holds shape and does not run off when spoon is tilted to 70°. Fork drip: when fork is lifted, drips slowly through the tines (1-2 drips per second, not a stream). Fork pressure: thumb pressure flattens the mound and it does not return. Syringe flow test (L3 mandatory): fill the 10 ml syringe, start 10 s timer, after 10 s the residual in the syringe reads 8-10 ml. All four must pass.

VISUAL QC — Smooth, glossy pale-yellow film across the spoon with no lumps. Surface self-levels in under 2 s after a shake. Drips from fork fall as fat droplets that merge into a continuous slow ribbon. No visible white curds or bubbles.

FAIL MODES — Too thin: residual <8 ml, drips stream from fork — whisk in 0.5 g thickener, rest 60 s, re-test. Too thick: residual >10 ml, no drip through fork — thin 5 ml water, re-blend 10 s, re-test. Lumpy/grainy from curds: sieve failed — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve before thickening; do not retest the original batch.
