1. PREP — Weigh 200 g set steamed egg custard (eggs + water, steamed to set). Break into chunks. Add 120 ml cool water. Blend in consumer blender 30 s, scrape down, 15 s more. Sieve through 4 mm sieve into a bowl; press soft residue through with the back of a spoon, discard coarse that won't pass. Measure the sieved liquid and record the volume V (expect about 250 ml). Add thickener at the calibration starting point for the thickener type in use, taken from docs/BASE_FOOD_LIST.md: gum-based (xanthan/gum blend) 1-2 g per 100 ml of measured sieved liquid, starch-based (grain-derived) 4-8 g per 100 ml. The figure fixed by calibration on BF01-BF05 overrides this and is recorded per specimen; weigh to 0.1 g and record the actual grams used on the data card. Whisk 20 s, rest 2 min, stir again. Titrate to the syringe window in 0.5 g (gum) or 1 g (starch) per 100 ml steps, each weighed to 0.1 g — never pinched. If the window cannot be reached inside the food-list range for the thickener in use, after the FAIL MODES corrections have been tried, the water ratio is wrong and the remedy depends on which way the reading misses: if the syringe still holds more than 10 ml at the top of the range the base is too stiff — discard and re-make with 160 ml water instead of 120 ml; if the residual has already fallen below 8 ml the base is over-thinned — discard and re-make with 90 ml water instead of 120 ml. Never thin a too-thin base with water and never thicken a too-thick one with thickener: on this test a larger residual means a thicker specimen, so each of those moves the reading away from the 8-10 ml window. The BF14 per-level note is blend-thin, and the water plus blend above IS that step; the 4 mm sieve is the lump-free gauge, not the texture step. Log this specimen only under the full level-suffixed ID bf14_steamed_egg_custard_L3 — the bare base-food ID carries L3, L4, L5 and L6 in the food list and must never be used as a ground-truth key.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork drip: lift a loaded fork clear of the bowl; the specimen drips slowly through the tines in a continuous flow (about 1-2 drips per second, never a free stream) and leaves a coating on the fork. Pass = steady slow drip; a stream is too thin, a mound sitting on top of the tines is too thick. Spoon tilt: a full spoon tilted to 45° pours off in a slow continuous stream over 2-4 s — not instantly, not as one lump — with no separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon or tray. Syringe flow test (L3, mandatory): fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release with the nozzle open, start the 10 s timer; pass = 8.0-10.0 ml remaining at t = 10 s with a measurable discharge during the 10 s, and both the t=0 fill level and the t=10 reading legible on film. All three must pass. Fork pressure is not an L3 test and is not performed on this specimen.

3. VISUAL QC — Smooth, glossy pale-yellow film across the spoon with no lumps and no white curds. Pours off the tilted spoon as one continuous slow ribbon that stacks briefly before settling; surface self-levels in under 2 s after a shake. Drips from fork fall as fat droplets that merge into a continuous slow ribbon. No visible bubbles, streaks, or separate liquid running ahead of the body.

5. FAIL MODES — Too thin: residual <8.0 ml, or drips stream from the fork — whisk in 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml (weighed to 0.1 g), rest 60 s, re-test. Too thick: 10.0 ml remains with no measurable discharge at 10 s, or the specimen sits as a mound on the fork with no drip through the tines — thin with 5 ml water, re-blend 10 s, re-test. Never correct a too-thick specimen with thickener: on this test a larger residual means a thicker specimen, so extra thickener moves the reading the wrong way. Lumpy/grainy from curds: sieve failed — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve before thickening; if the re-sieved puree is still grainy, discard the batch and re-make, do not simply re-test the original. Any specimen failing two consecutive tests is discarded and marked FAIL.
