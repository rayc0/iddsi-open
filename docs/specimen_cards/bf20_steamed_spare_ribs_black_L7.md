1. PREP

Use one steamed rib portion exactly as received, meat on bone. Record starting mass on the digital scale; aim for 120–160 g including bone. Do not blend. Do not sieve — an L7 specimen is never reduced below 15 mm. Do not add water, liquid, or thickener. The per-level note requires meat on bone, so processing is not performed: the operator photographs the whole piece as served and keeps the bone-in piece intact for the tests. If the portion arrives cold, steam covered 4–5 min until the meat core reaches 70–75 °C on a probe and no longer — longer steaming tenderises the meat toward L6. Size check: measure the thickest meat cross-section with the 100 mm ruler; every meat dimension must be ≥15 mm, and the meat must be firm, fibrous, and require chewing. Record the measured dimensions in mm on the capture sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST

Applicable at L7: fork pressure only (§5.1). Spoon tilt (§5.2) and fork drip (§5.3) are L3–L5 tests and must not be run, filmed, or recorded at L7. No syringe flow test above L3 (§5.4).

Fork pressure test: press the meatiest non-bone section with the fork tines, normal thumb pressure, until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass = the meat does NOT compress, squash, or change shape; any indentation springs back and the meat does not hold the fork mark, and it does not flake apart under the tines. Fail = the meat squashes and holds a deep indentation, or breaks into flakes — that is an L6 texture, not L7. L7 is passed when fork pressure passes and the ≥15 mm size check in PREP is recorded.

3. VISUAL QC

On the fork the meat shows visible fibre separation with a moist, glossy black-bean coating, no pooled liquid at the fork base or on the plate. On the plate the rib keeps its served shape, bone exposed, sauce clinging, not runny. No lasting fork indentation.

5. FAIL MODES

Sauce runs off and pools on the plate — blot the excess sauce with paper towel and re-shoot; if the sauce is still runny, remake with a fresh portion.
Meat falls cleanly off the bone before pressure, or squashes flat and holds the indentation, or splits into puree-like mash under the fork — too tender for L7. Do NOT re-steam or reheat further, which tenderises it more: pick a firmer piece from the pack or remake with a fresh portion, then re-test.
Meat cross-section under 15 mm — fails the L7 size check; select a larger piece and re-shoot.
Meat dry, tough, and stringy with no fibre separation — not a regular L7 texture; discard and remake with a fresh portion.
