1. PREP — Port 350 g tomato egg stir-fry into blender; add 60 ml warm water. Blend on high for 45 s. Press 200 g of the blend through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any seeds or skin that do not pass through. Return sieved blend to blender. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of blend (calibration starting point — record actual grams used). Blend 15 s to incorporate. Let rest 2 min, then stir once. Do not add extra water after sieving.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork drip: scoop a heaped forkful and hold level over a white plate for 10 s. Pass criterion: the entire mound sits on top of and between the tines with zero drip falling from the fork. Fork pressure: press the flat of the fork tines into the specimen until the thumbnail blanches white (approx. 17 N). Pass criterion: the indent holds clean, no liquid pools, no extrusion of thin liquid around the tines. Spoon tilt: place 5 ml on a spoon and tilt to a steep angle. Pass criterion: the specimen stays in a cohesive mound without sliding off. Do not perform the syringe flow test for L4.

3. VISUAL QC — Uniform glossy orange-pink color with no visible egg white chunks, no tomato skin flecks, no free red liquid pooling at the rim of the spoon. Surface should look smooth and slightly matte, holding a soft peak when scooped.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Blend still separates a watery red layer on standing: under-blended or under-thickened; re-blend 10 s and add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml, retest. (b) Specimen flows through fork tines or drips within 10 s: too thin; add thickener incrementally and retest. (c) Lumpy texture with visible white egg threads after sieving: blend an additional 20 s, re-sieve. Two failed attempts at the same fault → log as reject and re-cook a fresh portion.
