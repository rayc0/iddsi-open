PREP
Weigh 150 g sweetcorn kernels straight from the tin or heated from frozen; drain off packing liquid, blot 10 s on paper towel. Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add water or thickener. Each kernel must remain whole, separate, and not crushed during handling. Specimen is calibration starting point; operator records actual mass weighed.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines fully into the pile of kernels with thumb pressure until thumbnail blanches white. Specimen passes L7 only if every kernel flattens, splits, or smashes apart — no kernel remains hard, rounded, or bounces back. Spoon tilt test: tilt a heaped teaspoon of kernels 45 degrees over a white plate. Specimen passes if 5 or fewer kernels slide off the spoon and zero stay mounded; the rest tumble free as discrete pieces. Fork drip test does not apply at L7.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: kernels show flat, crushed, split faces with maize starch exposed; no whole round kernel survives. On the spoon at tilt: loose, dry, individual pieces; no clumping, no slick glaze, no sauce film binding them. On the plate: kernels sit as separate fragments, not a paste.

FAIL MODES
Any kernel stays whole and hard after full fork pressure: re-make with fresh kernels; reheat to steaming, re-drain, re-blot. Kernels clump or hold shape on spoon tilt because residual syrup is binding them: re-drain, rinse briefly under cold water, re-blot fully before reshooting. Pile looks mashed or creamy rather than discrete fragments: operator over-pressed during prep; re-make, do not crush with fork before the test shot.
