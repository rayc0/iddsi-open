PREP
Weigh 150 g sweetcorn kernels straight from the tin or heated from frozen; drain off packing liquid, blot 10 s on paper towel. Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add water or thickener. Each kernel must remain whole, separate, and not crushed during handling. No bench softening step of any kind is applied to this specimen — no simmer, no halving, no re-cutting: L7 is "food as bought and normally served" (protocol section 2), and the simmer step that bf30_sweetcorn_kernels_L6 requires to pass its firmness gate would make this specimen an L6 specimen. Specimen is calibration starting point; operator records actual mass weighed.

L6/L7 separation gate — read before shooting. For this food the L7 specimen and the L6 specimen are the same physical object: a kernel straight from the tin is already <= 15 mm and already flattens under the fork, which is exactly what the L6 card asks for, so the two hero photographs are identical. The only difference the protocol allows is that L7 carries no preparation step at all. Therefore: log the L6 event for this same batch first, and record on the capture sheet, in words, "BF30 L7 is not photographically separable from BF30 L6 — no bench step applied". If the L7 shot would be the same photograph as the L6 shot, the L7 label is not independently supportable for this food and the event is recorded as such rather than shot as a fresh L7 specimen.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test (5.1) — the only test that applies at L7: press fork tines fully into the pile of kernels with thumb pressure until the thumbnail blanches white. Specimen passes L7 only if every pressed kernel flattens, splits, or smashes apart — no pressed kernel remains hard, rounded, or bounces back. Press at least five kernels taken from different parts of the plate; all five must pass, and a kernel that stays hard is not replaced by a softer one.
Spoon tilt: not applicable at L7 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run.
Fork drip: not applicable at L7 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run.
Syringe flow: not applicable at L7 (5.4 is every L3 specimen only) and is not run.
L7 is confirmed by fork-pressure alone. No spoon-tilt criterion is scored, filmed or recorded for this specimen.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: kernels show flat, crushed, split faces with maize starch exposed; no whole round kernel survives. On the plate: loose, dry, individual pieces; no clumping, no slick glaze, no sauce film binding them, no pooled packing liquid. Kernels sit as separate fragments, not a paste, and no kernel has been halved or re-cut.

FAIL MODES
Any kernel stays whole and hard after full fork pressure: re-make with fresh kernels; reheat to steaming, re-drain, re-blot. Kernels clump or sit in a sticky mass on the plate because residual syrup is binding them: re-drain, rinse briefly under cold water, re-blot fully before reshooting. Pile looks mashed or creamy rather than discrete fragments: operator over-pressed during prep; re-make, do not crush with fork before the test shot. Any kernel found halved, re-cut or simmered: the specimen has been given the L6 bench step and is an L6 specimen, not an L7 one — discard it and re-make from the unopened tin or frozen pack with no bench step. A specimen that has been simmered to softness is never certified L7: L7 is the as-bought reference, and softness bought by simmering is the L6 card's route, not this one.
