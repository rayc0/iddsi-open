1. PREP
Bought form: retail packet of instant/quick rolled oats, dry, bought as packaged goods (check the label — most are not gluten-free). Allergens: wheat/gluten (oats plus shared milling lines); no milk, egg, nut or soy is added by this card. Thickener: xanthan- or starch-based IDDSI thickener, allergen-free on most labels — confirm on the pack and log it.
Weigh 60 g dry oats on the digital scale and 180 ml water heated to 80-85 °C. Combine in the blender, seal, run 25-30 s on high until fully liquidised. Gauge once through the 4 mm sieve: the sieve is a lump-free GAUGE, not a sizing step — anything retained goes back into the blender for 10 s more and the pass is repeated until nothing is retained (target retained mass 0 g; record actual). Measure the specimen volume in the jug (expect ~240 ml). Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 2.0 g per 100 ml of that measured volume (~4.8 g for a 240 ml batch); record actual grams used. Blend 10 s to incorporate, rest 60 s to hydrate, re-blend 5 s. Bring to 50 °C ±5 °C before testing (re-warm; never add cold water to cool). If the syringe reads off-spec, step thickener in 0.2 g/100 ml increments (~0.5 g), blend 5 s, rest 30 s, re-test; record final g/100 ml. Blending is mandatory at L3, not optional.

2. IDDSI TEST
Spoon tilt (5.2) — ACTIVE. Load 5 ml on a dessert spoon and tilt to 45° over a cup. Pass = the specimen slides off in a slow continuous ribbon that takes 3-5 s to clear the bowl of the spoon; it must not sit in a mound (that is L4) and must not pour like water (that is L2).
Fork drip (5.3) — ACTIVE. Load the fork tines and hold 10 mm above the cup, then tilt. Pass = the specimen drips slowly in a continuous thin stream — not a pour, not a blob.
Syringe flow (5.4) — ACTIVE. Fill the 10 ml IDDSI syringe to the 10 ml line, release, and time exactly 10 s with a timer. Pass = 8-10 ml remains in the barrel. Record residual to 0.5 ml: below 8 ml is too thin, above 10 ml is too thick.
Fork pressure (5.1) — SKIP: applies at L4, L6 and L7 (supporting only at L5). Not performed at L3 — an L3 specimen is lump-free and flows freely, so a tine impression carries no level information. Do not film it.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: glossy, uniform beige, no lumps, no separated water pooling at the rim. On the fork tines after drip: a smooth coating with no runny tails and no dry grainy streaks. Nothing above 4 mm survives the gauge. Colour even, no scorched or dry oat flakes on the surface.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin — pours fast off the spoon, drips in a stream from the fork, syringe residual <8 ml. Add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml of specimen (~0.5 g), blend 5 s, rest 30 s, re-test. This thickens the specimen toward the L3 window.
Too thick — drops from the fork in blobs, syringe residual >10 ml, spoon holds a peak. Add 5 ml water per 100 ml (~12 ml), blend 5 s, re-test. This thins the specimen toward the L3 window.
Grainy or weeping — material retained on the 4 mm gauge or water separating at the rim: return to the blender, add 20 ml water, blend 10 s, re-gauge. If it stays grainy after two passes, re-make from scratch; do not patch with thickener.
Never thicken past the point where the syringe holds >10 ml, and never thin past 8 ml — both leave the L3 window.
