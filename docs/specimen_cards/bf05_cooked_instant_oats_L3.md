PREP
Measure 60 g rolled oats and 180 ml hot water onto the digital scale. Pour into the blender, seal, run 25-30 s on high until fully liquidised. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve; discard any retained residue — record yield. Return liquidised oats to the blender. Add thickener at the stated calibration starting point of 2.0 g per 100 ml of specimen (record actual grams used and total volume). Blend 10 s to incorporate, rest 60 s, re-blend 5 s. If fork-drip and syringe read off-spec, step thickener in 0.2 g/100 ml increments and re-blend; record final grams/100 ml. Per-level note is blend-thin: blending is mandatory, not optional.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: tilt fork from spoon at 10 mm gap over a cup — drips slowly in a continuous thin stream, not a pour and not a blob.
Fork pressure: press fork tines flat into specimen on the spoon — surface yields, tines leave a clear, persistent impression; specimen does not flow back level.
Spoon tilt: tilt spoon 45 degrees — specimen slides slowly, holds a thick ribbon, does not run off the edge.
Syringe flow test: fill the 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release for 10 s — 8-10 ml remains in the barrel. Record residual ml.

VISUAL QC
On the spoon: glossy, uniform beige, no lumps, no separated water pooling at the rim. On the fork tines after drip: a smooth coating, no runny tails, no dry grainy streaks.

FAIL MODES
Too thin: pours fast, <8 ml in syringe, no fork impression. Re-make adding 0.2 g/100 ml thickener.
Too thick: drops in a blob, >10 ml residual, spoon holds a peak. Re-make adding 5 ml water, re-blend, re-test.
Grainy or weeping: incomplete blend or over-thinned with water. Re-make from scratch; do not patch.
