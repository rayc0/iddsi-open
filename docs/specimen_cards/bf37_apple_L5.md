1. PREP
Start with 250 g fresh apple, peeled, cored, cut into 2 cm cubes. Steam 8–10 min until fork-tender; drain. Mash thoroughly with a fork. Do not blend to a smooth purée and do not sieve — the per-level note requires mince ≤4 mm, not a smooth paste. Work the mash through a 4 mm sieve only if a first pass shows lumps >4 mm; press with the back of a spoon, discard the coarse residue. Target final specimen mass 200–220 g. No thin liquid, no added water. No thickener required at L5 — thickener is reserved for L3 calibrations. Record final mass on the capture sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into a heaped teaspoon of specimen on a flat plate with normal thumb pressure (~5 kg feel). Pass criterion: specimen squashes, spreads, and does not spring back; tine indent stays.
Fork drip test: lift a loaded fork. Pass criterion: no thin liquid drips through tines; only soft pieces may drop, and they hold shape on the plate.
Spoon tilt test: tilt a heaped teaspoon 45°. Pass criterion: specimen sits cohesive, falls off in one soft clump, slides slowly, leaves residue — no runny pour.
Syringe test: not required at L5.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: lumpy, minced texture with visible soft particles all roughly ≤4 mm, uniform pale apple colour, slight gloss from natural juice, no pooling liquid at the rim. Fork tines should leave a clean impression with edges that don't crumble dry or run wet.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-steaming into a smooth purée → fails visual QC and tine test; re-mash with reserved cubes, not re-blend.
Insufficient mashing leaving chunks >4 mm → fails sieve check; re-press through 4 mm sieve.
Pooling juice separating as thin liquid → fails fork drip; drain on paper towel 30 s, re-test.
