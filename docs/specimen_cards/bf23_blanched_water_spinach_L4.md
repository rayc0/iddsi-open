**BF23 · blanched water spinach 白灼蕹菜 · target L4 (pureed: lump-free, thicker, holds a soft shape)**
**Bought form:** fresh market, blanched plain. **Allergens:** none — confirm against the pack label at portioning and log extras in `prep.notes`.
**Tests that apply at L4:** fork pressure (5.1), spoon tilt (5.2), fork drip (5.3).

1. PREP
- Blanch: bring **1 L** water to a brisk boil, add **200 g** fresh water spinach (leaves with tender stems), push under, blanch **60 s**, lift out and drain **30 s** in the sieve. Keep **100 ml** of the blanching water.
- Trim: cut off and discard the tough bottom **40 mm** of every stalk, and any stalk that will not bend without cracking. Weigh the drained, trimmed greens: target **180 g** (record actual).
- Blend: greens in the blender with **40 ml** of the reserved blanching water (≤40 °C). Blend on high **45 s**, scrape the walls down once, blend **20 s** more (**65 s** total). Start on low and vent the lid if the greens are still warm.
- Sieve: press the blend through the **4 mm sieve** with the back of a spoon and discard what stays on the mesh. At L4 the sieve is the fibre remover (equipment list item 3), and this food's per-level note is "blend, sieve out stem fibres, blend-hold" — **no un-sieved specimen is tested**. Weigh and record the retained fibre mass.
- Weigh the sieved purée: target **200–220 g** (record actual).
- Thicken — required, not optional: the blanched-green purée will not hold a shape on its own. Whisk in thickener at the calibration starting point of **1.5 g gum-based per 100 ml** of purée (if only a starch-based thickener is stocked, start at **6.0 g per 100 ml**). Whisk **20 s**, rest **60 s**, stir **10 s**. Record the actual grams used in `prep.thickener_g`.
- Titrate against section 2 in **0.5 g (gum) or 1.0 g (starch) per 100 ml** steps, up to a ceiling of **4.0 g gum / 12.0 g starch per 100 ml**. Test the specimen at **20–40 °C**.

2. IDDSI TEST
- Fork pressure (5.1): press the fork tines flat into the purée on the tray until the thumbnail blanches white, hold **5 s**, lift. **Pass** = a clear impression remains, the surface does not rebound, and the purée does not squeeze through the tine gaps.
- Spoon tilt (5.2, L4 criterion): scoop a full spoon, hold it level, then tilt to **90°**. **Pass** = the purée holds its shape on the spoon and slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue left behind, and no separate thin liquid separates out.
- Fork drip (5.3, L4 criterion): scoop with the fork and hold it over the tray. **Pass** = the sample sits in a mound above the tines and does **not** flow through them.
- Separation check (QC rule 4): watch the whole clip — no thin green liquid may pool on the spoon or the tray at any point before it ends. Any pooling fails the specimen at L4.
- Syringe flow: **skip** — 5.4 is required on every L3 only. Running the 10 ml / 10 s flow test here would invite thickening toward the L3 window and drop the specimen below L4, so it is not run and is not recorded.

3. VISUAL QC
- Uniform dark-green smooth purée with no stem fibres, no leaf flecks and no grit; the sieve residue has already been discarded.
- Holds a clean peaked mound on the spoon; surface slightly glossy but not wet; nothing runs down the spoon handle.
- A fork press leaves a clean dent with no green liquid weeping around the tines.
- No separate liquid layer at the rim of the spoon or the edge of the tray.

5. FAIL MODES
- Purée pours off the tilted spoon, runs through the fork tines, or a thin green liquid separates and pools (**too thin**): whisk in **0.5 g gum (or 1.0 g starch) per 100 ml** of purée, rest 60 s, re-stir 10 s, re-test. This moves the specimen toward L4.
- Purée cracks or tears under the fork and will not spread (**too stiff** for a level that must hold a soft shape): thin with **10 ml** reserved blanching water per 100 ml of purée, re-blend **20 s**, re-test.
- Visible fibres or flecks after sieving: re-blend **20 s** and re-sieve through the 4 mm sieve; record the retained mass. If fibres still pass through, this bunch cannot be made lump-free on the bench — log the attempt, mark the row FAIL, and re-make from a younger, more tender bunch.
- Purée too loose because the greens released too much water: drain the blend in the sieve **60 s** before thickening, then thicken from the drained mass. Never add dry starch or flour straight in — every addition is weighed to 0.1 g and logged.
- Achievability, stated plainly: water spinach reaches L4 only by blending the trimmed greens and then sieving out the stem fibres, with the thickener titrated to the L4 window. Chopping finer or mashing longer will not produce a lump-free purée — do not try it.
