PREP — Weigh 200 g blanched water spinach (leaves and tender tips; discard thick stems). Add 60 ml of the cooking water. Blend 45 s on high, scrape, blend 20 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue retained on the mesh (stem fibres must be removed, per note). Return purée to blender, add 4.0 g thickener per 100 ml of purée (calibration starting point — record actual grams used). Blend 20 s to incorporate, rest 60 s, stir. Target final mass 240–260 g. If a watery film separates at rest, the blend-hold step has failed: re-blend and add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, then re-test.

IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the specimen until thumb blanching; specimen must compress and hold the indentation without returning to liquid, and must not drip through the tine gaps. Spoon tilt test: load a heaped teaspoon, tilt to 90°; specimen must sit in a mound and not slide or pour off. Fork drip test: lift fork with a loaded mound; specimen must sit above the tines, not flow or drip through. Confirm there is no separate thin liquid pooling on the plate beneath the specimen.

VISUAL QC — Uniform medium-green smooth purée with no visible stem flecks or fibres. Holds a clean peaked mound on the spoon; surface slightly glossy but not wet. When pressed with fork, a clean dent remains and no green liquid weeps around the tines.

FAIL MODES — (1) Thin liquid separates on standing — blend-hold insufficient; re-blend with +0.5 g thickener per 100 ml. (2) Specimen flows through fork tines or drips on tilt — under-thickened; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-blend. (3) Visible stem fibres or grainy texture — sieve step incomplete or blender under-worked; re-sieve through 4 mm mesh and blend an additional 15 s. Two consecutive failures on the same parameter: re-make from fresh 200 g batch.
