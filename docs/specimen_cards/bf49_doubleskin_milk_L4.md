1. PREP
Decant 200 g of fresh double-skin milk from the cup into the blender cup. Blend on high for 25-30 seconds until completely smooth with no curd flecks. Sieve through the 4 mm sieve into a small bowl; press through with the back of a spoon, discard residue. The per-level note is "blend-hold" — do NOT thin with milk or water; if the blend flows through the fork tines, return it to the blender. If L3 calibration is later required, add thickener at the supplier's grams-per-100 ml starting point and record actual grams used. Rest the specimen 2 minutes before testing.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork loaded with ~5 g of specimen to 10 cm. Pass = sits as a mound above the tines, does not drip or flow through the gaps within 10 seconds.
Fork pressure: press the fork tines flat into the specimen until the tines blanch, hold 5 seconds. Pass = tines leave a clear imprint, no separate liquid pools around the press.
Spoon tilt: load a spoon, tilt to 45 degrees. Pass = specimen holds as a cohesive mound, does not slide off in 5 seconds. (L3 syringe 10 ml/10 s test only when calibration requires it.)

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and uniform with no visible whey separation or curd lumps. Holds a sharp peak when mounded with a spoon. Matte finger-print impression holds cleanly when pressed.

5. FAIL MODES
Whey separates as a thin yellow pool around the mound — too wet, re-blend 10 s and re-test.
Specimen flows through fork tines — under-set, return to blender, do not add liquid.
Lumps or grain remain after sieving — blend a further 10 s and re-sieve; if still present, discard and remake.
