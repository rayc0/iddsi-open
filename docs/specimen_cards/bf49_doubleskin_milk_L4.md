1. PREP
Decant 200 g of fresh double-skin milk from the cup into the blender cup. Blend on high for 25-30 seconds until completely smooth with no curd flecks. Sieve through the 4 mm sieve into a small bowl; press through with the back of a spoon, discard residue. L4 is a lump-free level, so the sieve is part of the method here. Reweigh the sieved base and record.

The per-level note is "blend-hold", and the finished specimen must be lump-free and thick enough to hold a soft shape. Blending alone will not get it there: double-skin milk as served is a pourable drink that flows through the fork tines, and returning it to the blender changes nothing. The reliable bench route to L4 is calibrated thickening — whisk in the thickener at the supplier's stated Level 4 dose for this product (grams per 100 g of sieved base), whisk 30 s, rest 2 min, then test against section 2. Titrate in 0.5 g per 100 g steps until the L4 tests pass, and record the actual grams used and the final specimen mass. Do NOT thin with milk or water — that moves the specimen toward L3.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork loaded with ~5 g of specimen to 10 cm. Pass = sits as a mound above the tines, does not drip or flow through the gaps within 10 seconds.
Fork pressure: press the fork tines flat into the specimen until the tines blanch, hold 5 seconds. Pass = tines leave a clear imprint, no separate liquid pools around the press.
Spoon tilt: load a spoon, tilt to 45 degrees. Pass = specimen holds as a cohesive mound, does not slide off in 5 seconds.
Syringe flow: skip — the 10 ml / 10 s syringe test is an L3 test and is not run at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is glossy and uniform with no visible whey separation or curd lumps. Holds a soft peak when mounded with a spoon. Matte finger-print impression holds cleanly when pressed.

5. FAIL MODES
Whey separates as a thin yellow pool around the mound — whisk 20 s to re-incorporate; if free liquid still separates within 2 minutes the specimen is over-thin for L4, so add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g, whisk 30 s, rest 2 min, re-test. If it still weeps, discard and remake.
Specimen flows through the fork tines or slides off the tilted spoon — too thin for L4: add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g, whisk 30 s, rest 2 min, re-test. Do not add milk or water.
Lumps or grain remain after sieving — blend a further 10 s and re-sieve; if still present, discard and remake.
If the as-served double-skin milk cannot be brought to hold a soft shape even at the maximum dose on the supplier's specification, this product cannot reach L4 by a reliable bench method — record the attempt, do not label it L4, and remake with a thicker double-skin milk base or a standardised L4 thickened reference specimen.
