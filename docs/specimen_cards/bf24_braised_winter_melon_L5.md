PREP
Weigh 200 g of braised winter melon, drained of braising liquid. Reserve the liquid in a small cup; you will use a few ml only if needed. Press the melon through a 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon, working in small batches; discard any fibrous bits that will not pass. Mash the sieved pulp with a fork until uniform. Check particle size: spread a teaspoon on the checkerboard A4 sheet and confirm no piece exceeds one small square (~4 mm). If the mash looks dry, fold in 5–10 ml of the reserved braising liquid a teaspoon at a time; do not add so much that free liquid pools. Do not blend — blending destroys the L5 particle structure. Do not add thickener; L5 does not require it, and any added gum will hide the real texture. The blender is unused for this specimen.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the fork tines into a heaped teaspoon of specimen on a flat plate until the blanching shows white under the nail, then lift. Pass: the mash squashes flat, does not spring back, the tines leave a clean print.
Fork drip: lift a forkful 10 cm above the plate and hold 5 s. Pass: only soft dollops may drop; no free liquid runs through the tines.
Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped teaspoon 45°. Pass: the specimen holds its shape and slides as a cohesive mass; it does not pour.
Skip the syringe flow test — that is for L3 only.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: a moist, lumpy mash with visible flecks no bigger than a grain of coarse pepper; clings to the tines without dripping. On the spoon: a soft mound that holds its peak for several seconds, surface slightly glossy from the braising liquid, no puddle forming at the base.

FAIL MODES
Particles larger than 4 mm because the sieve was skipped or pressed too lightly — re-sieve the batch.
Specimen too wet, with thin liquid pooling on the plate — drain longer, then fold in pulp only, no extra liquid; if still failing, re-make from a drier portion of the braise.
Specimen too stiff and crumbly because too much fibrous flesh was retained — discard stringy bits during sieving and fold in a teaspoon of reserved liquid at a time until the fork-pressure test passes.
