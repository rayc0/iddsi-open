1. PREP — Weigh 150 g ready-to-eat black sesame paste into the blender jar. Add 120 ml cool water. Blend on high for 45 s, scraping down at 20 s. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue weighing more than 8 g. Weigh the sieved liquid; add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point (record actual grams used). Whisk by hand 30 s, rest 60 s, whisk 15 s. If drip from fork prongs is faster than 1 drop per second, add thickener 0.2 g per 100 ml, rest 60 s, retest. Record final liquid mass and water added.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork drip: held vertically, liquid drips in slow, continuous strands, not a steady stream; first drop within 1-2 s. Spoon tilt: spoon at 45 degrees, sample flows off in 2-4 s, leaves a thin coating. Fork pressure: prongs leave a clear, persistent line that does not refill. Syringe flow: fill 10 ml, release, at 10 s residual reads 8-10 ml; pass window 7-10 ml. All four must pass before capture.

3. VISUAL QC — On the spoon: matte jet-black, glossy but not pooling, one slow drip forming and falling. On the fork: 4-5 thin continuous strands at 1-2 per second, no run-off between prongs. No grainy specks, no separated oil sheen, no lumps catching on sieve residue.

5. FAIL MODES — Too runny: threads off fork in a continuous stream and syringe reads under 7 ml; re-thicken in 0.2 g per 100 ml steps, max three attempts, then discard batch. Too thick: drops do not fall or syringe holds over 10 ml; whisk in 5 ml water, rest 60 s, retest, max twice. Gritty after sieving: residue over 8 g or visible specks on spoon; re-blend 20 s with extra 10 ml water and re-sieve once.
