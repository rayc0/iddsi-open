1. PREP

Weigh 150 g steamed fish fillet on the digital scale. Add to consumer blender with 60 ml warm water. Blend 45 s, scrape down, blend 30 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any residue remaining in the sieve. Return puree to blender, add thickener at the calibration starting point of 3 g per 100 ml, blend 20 s to incorporate. Rest 60 s, then stir. Record the actual thickener mass used. Final specimen should weigh approximately 210 g. Per-level note is respected: blend then hold on spoon.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate with firm thumb pressure; surface must retain a clear dent, no full recovery, and must not push through the gaps. Pass = visible dent, holds shape.

Fork drip test: lift fork with a loaded mound; specimen must sit in a mound above the tines for 5 s with zero drip. Pass = zero drip.

Spoon tilt test: scoop a heaped teaspoon and tilt to full vertical; specimen must stay on the spoon with no sliding or run-off. Pass = no flow.

3. VISUAL QC

Mound sits cleanly above fork tines with sharp edges, glossy but matte (no pooled shine), and a single uniform cream-white color with no flakes, fibers, or separated liquid around the base.

5. FAIL MODES

Too runny: drips through fork tines or runs off spoon tilt. Re-make: add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, blend, retest.

Grainy or lumpy: visible fibers, flakes, or sieve residue in puree. Re-make: re-sieve or blend an extra 20 s with 10 ml extra water, then re-thicken.

Separate thin liquid pooling around specimen: insufficient hydration or thickener. Re-make: blend 10 s, rest 2 min, stir, retest.
