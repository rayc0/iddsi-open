1. PREP
Cook a fresh batch of white rice (long-grain, non-glutinous) and let it stand 2 min so the surface is dry and no free water remains. Portion 50 g ± 2 g of cooked rice. With dry hands or the back of a spoon, press the 50 g into one compact cake in the centre of the plate — target 25 mm across × 20 mm high, so that the smallest dimension is ≥15 mm (check against the 15 mm checkerboard cell: the cake must be wider than one full cell). Press firmly so the grains bind and the cake holds its moulded shape. Whole grains only. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add liquid — forming is the only manipulation and it does not change the food. Record the actual served mass on the data sheet before capture.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test (the only test that applies at L7): press the tines into the cake with the flat of the fork until the thumbnail blanches white. The cake must compress under the tines and must not return to its original contour when the fork is removed. Pass criterion: full white blanch, dent holds.
Spoon tilt, fork drip and syringe flow do NOT apply at L7 — do not run them and do not record them as pass/fail. The former spoon-tilt criterion ("no individual grains roll off") is withdrawn: loose cooked grains cannot meet it, and it is not an L7 test.

3. VISUAL QC
Grains distinct, glossy, non-glutinous surface. Cake holds the moulded shape with no spreading at the base and no cracking. Smallest dimension ≥15 mm (spans more than one full 15 mm checkerboard cell). No pooling liquid at the plate contact; no steam fogging the lens.

5. FAIL MODES
Grains too wet, pooling liquid on the plate, or the cake will not hold its moulded shape — a loose-grain serving is ~6-8 mm per grain and cannot be certified L7. Re-make from a fresh, drier batch, re-form, re-shoot all frames; do not add thickener or liquid.
Grains overcooked to a paste: the fork test smears instead of holding a dent — too soft, below L7. Re-make from a fresh batch cooked 2 min shorter, re-form, re-shoot all frames.
Grains firm, dent does not hold, cake springs back — re-cook the same batch 2 min longer, re-form, re-test; if it still springs back, reject the specimen.
Long-grain rice that will not bind into a ≥15 mm cake: this specimen cannot be brought to L7 by any bench method. Reject it and re-shoot from a batch that binds; record the substitution. Do not submit loose grains as L7.
