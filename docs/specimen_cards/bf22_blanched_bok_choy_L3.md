1. PREP
Weigh 150 g blanched bok choy (drained). Add 60 ml water. Blend 45 s on high; stop, scrape down, blend 30 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; press with the back of a spoon to extract purée, discard coarse fibre. Measure 200 ml purée. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 2.0 g per 100 ml (record actual mass used). Stir 30 s, rest 60 s, stir 15 s. Re-weigh total specimen and record.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork tines 5 cm; purée drips slowly in a thick continuous ribbon, falls in ≤3 drops over 10 s. Pass = ribbon + ≤3 drops. Fork pressure: press fork tines fully into specimen; tines leave a clear impression and purée does not flow back to level. Pass = clear imprint retained. Spoon tilt: spoon a heaped teaspoon, tilt to 45°; specimen slides slowly off in one cohesive mound, not a pour. Pass = slides as a mound. IDDSI flow test: fill 10 ml syringe, cap tip, invert, release after 10 s. Pass = 8-10 ml remaining (i.e. only 1-2 ml flowed out).

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale-green purée, no visible leaf fragments or fibre. Coats the back of a spoon in an even, opaque layer with no run-through. Hangs from fork tines as a continuous ribbon before falling. No free liquid pooling at the rim of the container.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny: re-add thickener in 0.2 g/100 ml increments, retest. Too thick / does not drip from fork: thin with water in 5 ml steps, retest. Visible fibre after sieving: re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; if fibre persists, L3 is not achievable for this batch — re-make with a finer sieve or switch food.
