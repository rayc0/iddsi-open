1. PREP
Weigh 200 g of mango pudding + 30 g of its syrup into the blender cup. Blend 8–10 s on pulse until a coarse mash forms. Press the mash through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a spoon; discard anything retained. Add 1.0 g thickener per 100 ml of mash, stir 20 s, rest 60 s, stir again 10 s. The stated 1.0 g/100 ml is the calibration starting point only; record the actual grams used on the data sheet. Target final mass 200 g ± 5 g. No added water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the fork tines flat into the specimen until the thumbnail blanches white. The specimen must squash, the gap between tines must NOT close, and it must NOT spring back when the fork is lifted. Spoon tilt: a heaped spoon tilted to 45° holds its shape; it must NOT run off as a thin stream. Fork drip: lifting the fork shows continuous cohesive clumps, not drips. L5 requires no flow test; if it flows through the tines, it is too wet — re-make with +0.25 g/100 ml thickener.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: uniformly yellow-orange mash, glossy but not pooling syrup. On the fork: small moist lumps clearly under 4 mm, no liquid dripping from the bottom edge. Sieve grid lines visible through the mass when pressed flat.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen drips thin liquid off the fork → under-thickened; re-blend with +0.25 g/100 ml, re-test. Particles exceed 4 mm or specimen springs back → under-mashed; re-blend +2 s, re-sieve. Specimen cracks or crumbles dry → over-thickened; re-blend with 5 g extra syrup and 0.25 g/100 ml less thickener, re-test.
