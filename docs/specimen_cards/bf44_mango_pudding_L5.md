1. PREP
Weigh 200 g set mango pudding + 30 g of its syrup into the bowl (230 g moist base). Do NOT blend to a smooth puree and do NOT sieve the lumps away — a lump-free specimen is L4, not L5. L5 must keep distinct moist lumps ≤4 mm. Fold and chop the pudding with a spatula into lumps ≤4 mm; press one spoonful through the 4 mm sieve as a GAUGE only — lumps that pass are correctly ≤4 mm; mince any lump left on the sieve down to ≤4 mm and fold it back in (do not discard it). If the pudding smears smooth and will not hold distinct ≤4 mm lumps, fold in 40 g fresh mango diced to ≤4 mm so 4 mm lumps are present. Add 2.0 g thickener (calibration start; record actual grams), stir 20 s, rest 60 s, stir again 10 s; keep moist with the 30 g syrup. Target final mass ≈232 g ± 5 g (≈272 g ± 5 g if the diced-mango fold-in is used). No added water.

2. IDDSI TEST
Spoon tilt (primary L5 test): a heaped spoon tilted to 45° holds its shape and only slumps; it must NOT run off as a thin stream. Pass = it holds together on the tilted spoon.
Fork pressure (supporting check only at L5): press the fork tines flat into the specimen; the ≤4 mm lumps should mash and the mass must not spring back. This is supporting evidence, not the pass gate.
L5 requires no syringe flow test and no fork-drip test (both are L3/L4 only). If the specimen flows through the tines or runs off the spoon, it is too thin/wet — add +0.5 g thickener and re-test.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: yellow-orange minced-and-moist mass with distinct moist lumps up to 4 mm; glossy with syrup but no free syrup pooling. On the fork: small moist lumps clearly ≤4 mm, no thin liquid running from the bottom edge. No lump larger than 4 mm passes the 4 mm gauge. Distinct ≤4 mm lumps must be visible — a smooth glassy surface means the batch is L4, not L5.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen runs off the spoon or drips thin liquid off the fork → too thin/under-thickened; add +0.5 g thickener, stir 20 s, re-test.
Lumps larger than 4 mm → too coarse; mince finer to ≤4 mm and re-gauge through the 4 mm sieve (keep the lumps, do not sieve them away).
Specimen cracks or crumbles dry → too thick/over-thickened; thin it: add +10 g syrup and reduce thickener by 0.5 g, re-test.
Achievability: mango pudding blended and sieved smooth is L4 (lump-free, holds a shape), not L5 — a smooth pudding has no 4 mm lumps. L5 is reached only if distinct ≤4 mm lumps are present and the mass stays moist yet holds on the spoon. If the pudding will not hold distinct 4 mm lumps even with the diced-mango fold-in, the specimen is L4, not L5 — certify it as L4 or use a food that naturally forms 4 mm lumps; do not certify a smooth specimen as L5.
