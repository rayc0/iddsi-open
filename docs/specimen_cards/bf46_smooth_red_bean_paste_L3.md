**Level 3 — Liquidised.** Tests that apply at L3: **fork-drip, spoon-tilt, and syringe flow** (8–10 ml remaining at t = 10 s). Fork pressure is not a valid test at L3 and is not run on this specimen.

1. PREP
Weigh 200 g ready-to-eat red bean paste into the blender cup. Add 80 ml water and blend 45 s until fully smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard retained skins/fibres; weigh the sieved paste and record the mass M (expect about 130 g).

Calibrate to L3 on a **mass basis only** — do not convert to ml, as no density is specified: 1.5 g thickener per 100 g of sieved paste. Dose = 1.5 g × M / 100, weighed to 0.1 g (M = 130 g → 1.95 g). Stir in, rest 2 min, stir again. Record the actual thickener mass used on the bench sheet.

Target: 8–10 ml of a 10 ml syringe fill remains at t = 10 s; fluid enough to pour in a slow ribbon, thick enough that drips are slow off the fork.
- Under-thick (fewer than 8 ml remains at 10 s): add 0.2 g thickener per 100 g of paste, stir, rest 2 min, and retest.
- Over-thick (more than 10 ml remains, or nothing has moved): stir in 5 ml water at a time, stir, and retest.
Do not sieve after adding thickener.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold the loaded fork clear of the cup for 10 s. PASS — 2–4 slow drips in 10 s, not a stream, not a single drop.
Spoon tilt: place a heaped teaspoon on the spoon and tilt to ~45°. PASS — the paste flows off in a thick ribbon, not a continuous pour, not a lump that drops whole.
IDDSI flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release, and start the stopwatch. PASS — 8–10 ml remains in the syringe at t = 10 s.
All three must pass. Fork pressure is not applicable at L3 and is not run.

3. VISUAL QC
Glossy uniform dark brown-red, no visible bean skins or fibres, no separated liquid pooling at the rim. Coats the back of a spoon smoothly without running into a thin film. Holds a soft peak for about 2 s then flattens.

5. FAIL MODES
Flow test — fewer than 8 ml remaining at 10 s: specimen is too THIN. Add 0.2 g thickener per 100 g of paste, stir, rest 2 min, and retest.
Flow test — more than 10 ml remaining at 10 s, or nothing has moved: specimen is too THICK. Stir in 5 ml water, and retest.
Visible skins, grainy spoon coat, or material retained on the 4 mm sieve: blender time too short or sieve skipped; re-blend 30 s and re-sieve before adding thickener, then re-run all three tests.
Two consecutive failures on the same root cause: re-make from 200 g paste.
