1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed ribs on the scale. Strip meat from bone; discard bones and excess fat. Place meat in blender; pulse 6 s, scrape, pulse 4 s — coarse mince only. Press mince through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; reject overs. You should have roughly 180 g sieved mince. Reserve 60 ml of the cooking juices separately. Fold 30 ml juices through the mince to moisten; if it clumps dry, add 5 ml juice at a time up to 50 ml total. No thickener at L5. Per-level note "mince meat <= 4 mm with juices" is satisfied; do not over-blend to a paste.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines into 15 mm heap on the plate until thumbnail blanches. Heap must squash and not spring back. Pass. Spoon tilt: load 5 ml on spoon and tilt; heap must slide off in one mass with no run-off of thin liquid. Pass. Fork drip: lift fork from specimen; nothing drips through tines. Pass. No syringe flow test at L5.

3. VISUAL QC
Minced particles clearly visible but none larger than a checkerboard square (4 mm). Surface glistens wet; no pooling liquid on the plate. Fork lifts a cohesive mound, not a smear.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended to paste — loses L5 texture; remake, pulse only. Particles exceed 4 mm — rejected by sieve; re-mince the overs. Fat pools free on the plate — separates thin liquid; drain surface and fold in dry mince to absorb. Re-make rule: any fail means discard and start a fresh 250 g batch.
