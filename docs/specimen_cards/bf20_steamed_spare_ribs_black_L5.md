1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed ribs on the scale. Strip meat from bone; discard bones and excess fat. Place meat in blender; pulse 6 s, scrape, pulse 4 s — coarse mince only, target lumps 3–4 mm with no particle larger than 4 mm. Sieve 4 mm as a GAUGE: press the mince through with the back of a spoon and reject the overs; the mince must still read as mince — if most of it drops through as a smooth purée the specimen is L4, not L5, so re-make from a fresh 250 g batch with shorter pulses. You should have 180–200 g sieved mince; below 170 g means the sieve took too much, re-mince rather than continue. Reserve 60 ml of the cooking juices separately. Fold 30 ml juices through the mince to moisten; if it clumps dry, add 5 ml juice at a time up to 50 ml total — final specimen 230–250 g including juice. No thickener at L5. Per-level note "mince meat <= 4 mm with juices" is satisfied; do not over-blend to a paste.

2. IDDSI TEST
Applicable tests at L5: 4 mm sieve as gauge (PREP), spoon tilt, fork pressure as a supporting check only. Spoon tilt (primary): load 5 ml on a spoon and tilt to 45°; the heap must slide off in one mass with no run-off of thin liquid. Pass. Fork pressure (supporting check only — record it, do not use it as the pass criterion): press fork tines into a 15 mm heap (~10 g) on the plate until the thumbnail blanches; the heap must squash and not spring back. Fork drip: NOT APPLICABLE at L5 — §5.3 scopes fork-drip to L3/L4, so do not run it. No syringe flow test at L5.

3. VISUAL QC
Minced particles clearly visible but none larger than a checkerboard square (4 mm); the pile reads as coarse 3–4 mm lumps, not paste. Surface glistens wet; no pooling liquid on the plate. Fork lifts a cohesive mound, not a smear.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended to paste — loses L5 texture; re-make, pulse only. Mince passes the sieve as purée with no visible lumps — reads as L4; re-make from raw, knife-chopped 3–4 mm lumps, sieve as gauge only. Particles exceed 4 mm — rejected by sieve; re-mince the overs. Fat pools free on the plate — separates thin liquid; drain surface and fold in dry mince to absorb. Re-make rule: any fail means discard and start a fresh 250 g batch.
