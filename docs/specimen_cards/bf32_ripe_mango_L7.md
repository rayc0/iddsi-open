Target: IDDSI Level 7 — soft whole pieces ≥15 mm in every direction, regular shape, requiring chewing.

1. PREP
Bought form: one whole fresh ripe mango (250–350 g, Ataulfo/Alphonso type or similar) as sold — skin on, unpeeled, uncooked. Allergens: none of the major declared allergens (no milk, egg, fish, crustacean, mollusc, peanut, tree nut, soy, wheat/gluten, sesame, celery, mustard, lupin, sulphites).
Wash and dry the fruit. Cut one cheek from each side of the flat pit with a chef's knife. Peel each cheek with a vegetable peeler and discard the skin. Cut each cheek into wedges 15–20 mm thick and 20–25 mm wide; lay every piece against the 15 mm checkerboard and set aside any piece under 15 mm in any direction. Weigh 150 g of accepted wedges onto the capture plate. Do not blend, sieve, chop fine, cook, or add thickener, water or any liquid. The specimen is the fruit exactly as served at the table.

2. IDDSI TEST
- 5.1 Fork pressure — ACTIVE, and the only applicable test at L7. Place one wedge on the plate and press the fork tines flat onto it with thumb pressure until the thumbnail blanches. Pass = the wedge squashes, splits or crumbles and does not return to its original shape when the fork is lifted. Test three wedges; all three must pass.
- 5.2 Spoon tilt — skip: applies at L3, L4 and L5 only; it scores flow and hold of smooth and minced textures and has no criterion for a whole piece at L7.
- 5.3 Fork drip — skip: applies at L3 and L4 only; a solid wedge neither drips nor flows, so the test cannot discriminate at L7.
- 5.4 Syringe flow — skip: applies at L3 only; a solid wedge cannot be loaded into the 10 ml syringe.

3. VISUAL QC
Each wedge keeps its own outline on the plate, separated from its neighbours by ≥10 mm; pieces do not mound or stick together. Cut faces show visible fruit fibre with a light juice sheen, and no free juice pools anywhere on the plate. Colour is uniform ripe yellow-orange with no grey or brown bruising and no black oxidised spots on the test wedge. Every piece measures ≥15 mm in all directions against the 15 mm checkerboard.

5. FAIL MODES
Wedge resists fork pressure and springs back (under-ripe) — too firm for L7: replace with a riper mango, re-cut and retest. Do not steam, blend or sieve to soften it; those steps move the specimen toward L4/L3, away from L7.
Wedge collapses to purée or runs as juice under light pressure (over-ripe) — behaves as L3/L4: reject and use a firmer ripe specimen; do not add thickener, which cannot return mashed fruit to whole pieces.
Any piece measures under 15 mm — re-cut a larger wedge; if the fruit is too small to yield ≥15 mm wedges, use a larger mango (≥300 g).
Wedges stick together in a mound with juice pooling — separate onto the plate with a ≥10 mm gap and shoot within 10 min of cutting; do not add liquid.
