1. PREP
Select one ripe mango. Cut two cheeks from the pit, then slice each cheek into pieces roughly the size served at the table (about 2-3 cm wedges, skin off if normally served skin-off). Do not blend. Do not sieve. Do not add thickener. Weigh 150 g of cut pieces onto the plate. No water or liquid added. The food is served as the reference specimen, unchanged.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: Press the tines firmly onto one piece with the fork held flat. The piece must squash, break, or crumble under the pressure — it must not return to its original shape when the fork is lifted. Pass = clear deformation or breakage under thumb-level pressure, no recovery.
Spoon tilt test: Place several pieces on a spoon and tilt to 45 degrees. Pieces slide or fall off; liquid (if any) pours freely. Pass = pieces do not hold shape on the spoon.
Fork drip test: Lift a piece on the fork tines. It does not drip through the tines or form a continuous flow. Pass = no drip, no flow between tines.
No syringe test applies at L7.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces hold individual shape on the plate, do not mound or stick together. Surface shows visible fruit fiber and juice sheen under the checkerboard. Color is uniform ripe-mango yellow-orange with no grey or brown bruising on the test piece.

5. FAIL MODES
Piece is too firm and resists fork pressure — re-cut smaller or replace with a riper specimen, retake all shots. Piece is overripe and collapses to puree under light pressure — reject specimen, use a firmer ripe one. Pieces stick together in a mound (behaves as L6) — separate pieces, do not add thickener, retest.
