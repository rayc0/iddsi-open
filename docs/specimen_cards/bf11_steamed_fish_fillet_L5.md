PREP
Weigh 180 g steamed fish fillet. Break into chunks on a board. Pulse in the consumer blender 6 s, scrape, pulse 4 s more. Gauge the mince against the 4 mm sieve instead of screening it (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the particles fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap of the spoon. Do not press the batch through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Re-chop anything held back on the mesh with the knife and record the retained mass. Weigh the mince. Whisk 1.5 g thickener into 30 ml warm fish broth (calibration starting point; record actual grams per 100 ml on the data sheet). Stir the thickened broth into the mince until it binds and looks glossy-wet, not soupy. Re-weigh final specimen. If it slumps to a puddle, add 0.3 g thickener per 100 g and re-mix.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen until thumbnail blanches; on release the slot holds, no recovery. Pass = visible slot, fish does not spring back. Fork drip: not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence. Spoon tilt: scoop, tilt spoon 30 degrees. Pass = specimen sits as a cohesive mound, slides only as a whole. Fork-drip and spoon-tilt together confirm no separate thin liquid.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: a soft mound of pale flecks, every piece smaller than a peppercorn. Glossy surface, no pooling broth on the plate beside it. Holds the fork-print shape for at least 5 s. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): hold fork with 1 cm of specimen above a plate for 5 s; pass = at most a trace drop, no continuous drip.

FAIL MODES
Specimen too wet, broth pools within 5 s: add 0.3 g thickener per 100 g, re-test. Visible pieces above 4 mm after sieving: re-blend 4 s and re-sieve. Spring-back on fork test, indicates L6 texture: add 5 ml broth per 100 g and re-test once; if it still springs back, re-cut smaller and re-sieve.
