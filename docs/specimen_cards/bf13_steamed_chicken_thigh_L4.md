1. PREP
Weigh 250 g steamed chicken thigh (skin removed, boneless, cooled to ≤40 °C). Add to consumer blender with 60 ml water at ≤40 °C — do not use hot water; if the blend is slow to break down, blend longer rather than hotter, because the specimen must be ≤40 °C when it is tested. Blend on high for 90 s, scrape jar walls, blend 60 s more. Pass purée through a 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any fibrous residue that does not pass — note retained mass. Weigh sieved purée. L4 is lump-free, so this sieve is mandatory: no un-sieved specimen is tested. Add thickener at 1.0 g per 100 g sieved purée as the calibration starting point (3.0 g for a 300 g yield); whisk in, rest 2 min, re-stir 10 s. If the fork-drip test shows flow below the tines, add 0.2 g per 100 g per increment (0.6 g for a 300 g yield) up to a maximum of 1.8 g per 100 g (5.4 g for a 300 g yield), re-testing after each increment. Record final thickener rate (g per 100 g) and final specimen mass (g).

2. IDDSI TEST
Test at 20–40 °C. Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate with normal thumb force for 5 s. Pass — tines leave a clear, persistent impression and the surface does not rebound fully; sample does not squeeze between tines. Spoon tilt: load a dessert spoon, tilt to 90°. Pass — specimen sits in a cohesive mound, does not slide, pour, or drip. Fork drip: lift a forkful 10 cm above the plate, hold 5 s. Pass — sample mounds above the tines, zero drip.

3. VISUAL QC
Matte, uniform pale beige surface; no pink fibres, no separated broth pooling at plate edge; spoon mound holds a defined peak without slumping.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin — slides off spoon or drips through tines: add 0.2 g thickener per 100 g, rest 2 min, re-test (maximum 1.8 g per 100 g). Too stiff — cracks on the plate or surface fractures under fork: thin with 10 ml water at ≤40 °C, re-blend 30 s, re-test. Visible fibres or pooled liquid: re-sieve and discard residue before judging.
