1. PREP
Weigh 250 g chilled coconut jelly straight from the pack into the blender jar. Blend 20 s on high until fully smooth with no lumps. Sieve through the 4 mm mesh into a bowl; discard residue. Measure the sieved volume, then thin to pourable consistency by whisking in thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml of sieved sample — this is the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used and the final volume. Add 20–40 ml water only if the blend is too stiff to pour after thickener. Stir 30 s, rest 60 s, stir again. Final specimen must drip slowly through fork tines, not run.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: sample drips in a slow, continuous thin stream through the tines, not a fast pour and not a solid blob. Spoon tilt: a small mound on the spoon spreads only slightly when tilted to 45°, holds shape. Fork pressure: the tines leave a clear, soft imprint that does not fill back in immediately. Flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark with no air gap; after 10 s the syringe must retain 8–10 ml (i.e. 0–2 ml has dripped). All four pass = L3 confirmed.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: smooth, glossy, no grains or jelly chunks catching the light. On the spoon: a soft mound that flattens at its base within 2 s of settling, no clear liquid pooling around it. Colour uniform off-white throughout, no streaks.

5. FAIL MODES
Too runny: passes the tines in a fast pour and the syringe empties past 2 ml — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, re-test. Too thick: sits on the fork without dripping and the syringe retains the full 10 ml — whisk in 5–10 ml water, re-test. Grainy: visible jelly strands after sieving — re-blend 10 s and re-sieve; if strands persist, the food cannot meet L3 and must be re-recorded as a failed specimen.
