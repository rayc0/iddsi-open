1. PREP
Weigh 200 g cooked ribs with 80 ml pan juices. Blend on high for 45 s, scrape, blend 30 s more. Press through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; L4 is lump-free, so the purée must pass and the mesh must run clean. Discard any fibre that will not pass and weigh it: residue must not exceed 5 g (2.5 % of the 200 g input). Record the residue mass in the log — this is a hard limit, not a note that the step is incomplete. If residue exceeds 5 g, blend a further 20 s and re-sieve once; if it still exceeds 5 g, do not proceed — discard the batch and re-make. Weigh the sieved purée (target 270–275 g). Add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 g of purée as the calibration starting point; whisk in, rest 2 min, re-weigh and record the actual grams used. Target final mass ≈ 275–285 g, smooth, glossy, no visible meat fibre.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: a full fork pressed into the surface for 5 s leaves a clear, persistent dent without the purée spreading or breaking; surface does not flow to refill the gap.
Spoon tilt: a heaped teaspoon held over a plate does not drip, slide, or pour when tilted 45°, then 90°; the mound holds shape.
Fork drip: lifting a loaded fork, no thin liquid drips through the tines; purée sits as a mound on top of the tines.
No flow-test syringe required at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Mound sits proud of the fork tines, no thin-liquid drip. Spoon tilt holds a clean profile with no slumping or pooling on the plate. Surface is uniform, glossy, mid-brown, no meat fibre or black-bean skins visible.

5. FAIL MODES
Mound slumps on spoon tilt or thin liquid drips through the fork → too thin, add thickener 0.5 g per 100 g of purée (≈1.4 g for this batch), rest 2 min, and retest. Visible fibre or bean skins in the finished purée, or sieve residue above 5 g → re-blend 20 s and re-sieve; do not proceed until the mesh runs clean. Puree cracks or splits under fork pressure → too thick, whisk in 10 ml pan juices and retest.
