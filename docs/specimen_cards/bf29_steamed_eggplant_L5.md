1. PREP — Weigh 250 g freshly steamed eggplant (skin on, tender). If any piece exceeds 4 mm, mash with a fork on a plate; if mash remains uneven, pulse in the blender 5–10 s only — do not purée. Press the mash through a 4 mm sieve, discarding what does not pass; re-mash the retained coarse fraction and sieve once more. Add no water, no oil, no thin liquid, no thickener (thickener is not used at L5; this is the L3 calibration field only — leave blank). Target yield: about 200 g of soft mash with no particle larger than 4 mm and no separate liquid pooling on the plate.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure test: press the tines of a fork into a heaped teaspoon of specimen on a flat white plate with firm thumb pressure until the thumbnail blanches; the mash must squash completely and stay squashed — no spring-back, no original shape recovered. Fork drip test: lift the fork with a heaped sample; no thin liquid must drip through or run off the tines within 10 s. Spoon tilt test: place a heaped teaspoon on the spoon and tilt it to almost vertical; the specimen must sit in a pile and not slide off or run. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC — On the fork: damp, soft clumps the size of coarse minced meat, no shiny wet film, no droplets falling between tines. On the spoon: holds a heaped mound when tilted; surface looks matte-moist, not glossy. On the white plate under the specimen: no free liquid ring spreading around the mash.

Clips: (4) 8–12 s continuous fork-press plus spoon-tilt on the same heap; (5) the IDDSI test film combining fork pressure, fork drip, and spoon tilt in one unbroken sequence.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Any particle exceeding 4 mm after sieving: re-mash the retained fraction and sieve again; if it still will not pass, the batch is too fibrous — discard and start with a softer eggplant. (b) Free thin liquid pools on the plate or drips from the fork: the eggplant was over-steamed or watered; drain on a paper towel for 2 min, then re-test. (c) Specimen springs back under fork pressure: it is under-processed — pulse-blend 3–5 s and sieve once more before re-testing.
