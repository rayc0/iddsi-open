1. PREP — Stage the fully ripe persimmon; expect soft flesh, no firm core. Peel, discard skin and stem, quarter by hand. Place 150 g flesh into the blender; pulse 10-15 s to break down, not purée. Pass through the 4 mm sieve, pressing gently with the back of a spoon to express soft pulp; discard any fibrous strands or seeds that remain. Confirm pieces ≤15 mm; this matches the per-level note that spoon pieces must be ≤15 mm. Do not add water, milk, or liquid — extra moisture pushes the specimen toward L5. Do not add thickener — L6 does not require thickener; adding it would push the specimen toward L4/L5 and is incompatible with the level. Calibrate scale to zero before weighing specimen for capture.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork-pressure test: press thumbnail into a 10 mm piece on a flat plate; thumbnail blanches white, piece squashes flat, and does not recover its original form when pressure is released — PASS. Spoon-tilt test: scoop a piece on a spoon and tilt to 45°; piece slides off easily with minimal liquid separation — PASS. Fork-drip test: lift a forkful from the specimen pile; no continuous drip falls through the tines in 5 s — PASS. The 10 ml/10 s syringe flow test does not apply to L6 and should not be run.

3. VISUAL QC — Pieces hold a soft, rounded shape on the spoon without pooling as sauce. Color is uniform deep orange throughout the pile, no pale fibrous lumps. Surface looks moist but not glossy-wet; nothing runs off the spoon edge when level.

4. SHOTS — Capture three stills: hero 45° with ruler and checkerboard in frame, top-down with checkerboard centered, fork-press close-up mid-press with thumbnail blanch visible. Capture two clips: 8-12 s showing fork-press then spoon-tilt; second clip the full IDDSI test sequence. Name all files soft_ripe_persimmon_L6_take1.jpg or .mp4 with take2, take3 suffixes as needed.

5. FAIL MODES — Underripe fruit: pieces stay springy and recover after fork-pressure — re-make with fully ripe persimmon only. Over-blended: specimen turns to purée and flows through the 10 mm fork gaps — re-make with shorter pulses, do not sieve to a paste. Liquid pooling on the plate after the test: moisture has separated — re-make without adding liquid and sieve again.
