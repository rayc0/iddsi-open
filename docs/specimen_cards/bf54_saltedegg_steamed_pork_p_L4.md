1. PREP
Weigh 150 g fresh minced pork and 1 salted-egg yolk (≈20 g) on the digital scale — 170 g solids. Steam 12 min until just cooked; cool to ≤40 °C. Load cooked patty into the consumer blender, add 80 ml water; blend 45 s, scrape down, blend 15 s until smooth. L4 is lump-free, so the 4 mm sieve is the sizing step here: pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue. Water is the only thickness control and 80 ml is the L4 set point — do not add extra water on the first pass. Specimen target 230–250 g (170 g solids + 80 ml water, less sieve loss); a yield under 220 g means the batch lost too much in the sieve, re-make.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: mound on the upturned fork must sit above the tines for ≥10 s with no drip. Fork pressure: pressing the fork tines into the specimen must leave the tine prints clearly visible and the surface must not bounce back liquid. Spoon tilt: a heaped teaspoon must hold its shape when the spoon is tilted 45°, no flow off the edge. All three pass = L4 confirmed. No syringe flow test at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: matte, uniform pale-yellow with no pink pork flecks and no oily pooling. On the fork: sits as a single cohesive lump between/above tines, not as separate streaks. Surface after fork press: clean tine imprints, no glossy liquid return.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen flows through the fork tines (too thin) → THICKEN. Re-blending and re-sieving do not thicken anything; re-make with 60 ml water instead of 80 ml, or fold in 30 g more cooked pork mince, then blend 45 s, sieve, re-test. Do not add thickener to rescue a thin batch. Specimen will not leave the spoon, cracks at the edge, or takes no tine prints (too thick) → THIN: add 10 ml water at a time, up to 100 ml total, re-blend 20 s, re-sieve, re-test — never thin past the spoon-tilt pass, or the specimen becomes L3. Oily yolk separates as a thin layer (fat break) → discard batch; reboil egg, blend hotter mass, re-test. Lumpy with visible pork fibre → sieve was skipped or under-blended; re-sieve, re-shoot. Two consecutive fails = re-make from 150 g pork.
