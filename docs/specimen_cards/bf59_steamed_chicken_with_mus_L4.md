PREP
Weigh 250 g cooked chicken+mushroom mixture. Add 60 ml hot water. Blend 90 s on high, scrape bowl, blend 60 s more. Sieve once through 4 mm; discard residue. Target yield about 300 ml puree. Thickener: start at 2.0 g per 100 ml puree (calibration point — record actual on the scorecard). Whisk thickener into warm puree, rest 2 min, stir, re-weigh. If the fork-drip test (below) is not yet passed, add 0.5 g/100 ml increments, rest, re-test. Do not add more water after sieving — re-blend is the only recovery step.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a plate until the tines blanch. The puree must hold the indentation without liquid pooling around the print. Pass.
Spoon tilt: scoop, tilt spoon 45°. Specimen must sit in a mound, not slide or drip. Pass.
Fork drip: lift fork with a heaped sample; specimen must sit in a mound above the tines, not drip through in 5 s. Pass.
No separate thin liquid film may be visible in the bowl.

VISUAL QC
Surface is matte, no shine or watery ring at the bowl edge. No visible mushroom fibres, skin flecks, or seed specks. Holds a clean spoon-shaped mound when scooped; tip does not slump.

FAIL MODES
Puree flows through fork tines or levels on the plate → under-thick; add 0.5 g/100 ml thickener, rest 2 min, re-test, re-shoot take1.
Loose liquid pools around the mound → over-blended with excess water; re-blend thicker batch, do not dilute.
Visible fibres or mushroom skin after sieving → sieve discard too coarse; re-sieve one pass, re-shoot take2 only.
