**Specimen:** firm tofu, IDDSI L6 (Soft & Bite-Sized) — pieces 8–15 mm, soft, cut.
**Bought form and allergens:** 1 block (≈400 g) chilled water-packed firm tofu from the refrigerated aisle — not silken, not extra-firm. Allergen: soy.

1. PREP
   - Weigh 150 g of drained, patted-dry tofu on the digital scale; record the mass.
   - Cut on the board with a sharp knife: trim the block to a 12 mm-thick slab against the 100 mm ruler, cut the slab into 12 mm strips, then cross-cut into 12 mm cubes.
   - Gauge every cube on the 15 mm checkerboard: each cube must sit inside a 15 mm square. Discard any cube measuring 15 mm or more (that is L7 territory) and any fragment under 8 mm (that is L5).
   - Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. A 4 mm sieve is a gauge, not a production step, and it cannot enforce a 15 mm limit.
   - Lay the cubes in a single layer on a white plate, cover with an upturned plate, rest 2 min at room temperature. Blot surface water once with a dry paper towel.
   - Record: 150 g start, 12 mm cubes, no liquid added, no thickener, sieve not used, blender not used.

2. IDDSI TEST
   - 5.1 Fork-pressure — ACTIVE, and the deciding test at L6. Lay one cube flat on the plate, press the fork tines down with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white, hold 1 s, release. Pass = the cube squashes to about half its height, the tines sink in, and the dent remains after the fork is lifted — no spring back, no cracking into dry crumbs.
   - 5.2 Spoon-tilt — skip: the mapping applies it to L3, L4 and L5 only. An L6 specimen is discrete cut pieces, not a spoonable mass, so a tilt result cannot grade it.
   - 5.3 Fork-drip — skip: the mapping applies it to L3 and L4 only. There is no pourable phase at L6 and nothing can drip through the tines.
   - 5.4 Syringe flow — skip: L3 only (8–10 ml remaining at t = 10 s). A 12 mm cube cannot be drawn into a syringe.
   - Size gate, run before the press test: every cube measures 8–15 mm on the checkerboard.

3. VISUAL QC
   - Cubes are clean-cut, matte pale-white, with square corners; no ragged torn faces, no pooled water on the plate.
   - Cubes hold their shape on the plate without slumping or spreading.
   - Under fork press the cube compresses visibly and the dent stays after release.
   - No free liquid film under the cubes after the 2 min rest.

5. FAIL MODES
   - Any cube measures 15 mm or more — re-cut the batch to 12 mm and re-measure on the checkerboard. Never re-sieve and never blend: L6 is cut, not pureed.
   - Cube springs back to full height after the press (block too firm for L6) — re-make from a softer block (medium-firm tofu that still cuts a clean 12 mm cube) and re-test. Do not add water to soften.
   - Cube smears to paste or weeps water onto the plate (too soft, drifting toward L5) — re-make from a firmer block, chill 10 min, re-cut to 12 mm and re-test.
   - Fragments under 8 mm after trimming — re-cut larger to 12 mm; do not mash the trimmings back in.
   - Batch held over 30 min at room temperature — discard and re-cut fresh.
