1. PREP
Weigh 300 g steamed sweet potato flesh on the digital scale. Load into the consumer blender; blend 25-30 s, scrape down, blend another 15-20 s until glossy. Pass through the 4 mm sieve with a spatula, discarding residue; re-weigh puree. Add water back at roughly 1 ml per 5 g puree lost to fibre to restore mouthfeel — record exact ml. L4 requires no thickener; if the puree slumps below the spoon-tilt line, dust 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml puree, blend 5 s, re-test, and log the actual figure used. Re-weigh final specimen.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a fork-tine-full 5 cm above the surface — puree must mound and drop as a single piece, not drip continuously. Fork pressure: press tines into a 1.5 cm mound on a flat plate; puree should flatten and leave a clear dent, not split or run. Spoon tilt: scoop, tilt spoon 45° — puree holds in the bowl, slides as one clump, no separate liquid pools. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform deep orange, no fibre streaks or dark specks; matte-sheen surface, not glossy or weeping; clings to the spoon without tailing.

5. FAIL MODES
Weeping thin liquid onto the plate — under-blended or over-watered; re-blend 10 s, sieve again. Puree runs through the fork tines — too thin; add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml, re-test. Lumpy with visible fibre — under-sieved; re-sieve or blend longer.
