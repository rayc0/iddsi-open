(level L5 — lumps ~4 mm, moist, fork-mashable; applicable tests: 4 mm sieve as gauge, spoon tilt, fork pressure supporting)

1. PREP

Weigh 120 g of chilled baked egg custard into a bowl. MASH with a fork against the side of the bowl until the largest visible curds are <=4 mm. Do NOT press the mash through the 4 mm sieve — pressing forces the curds through the mesh and yields the glossy, curd-free purée that section 5 calls a failed batch. Use the 4 mm sieve as a GAUGE only: tip 15 g of the mash onto the dry sieve and tilt it 3 times without pressing; return the material that falls through to the bowl, and re-mash by hand any curd retained on the mesh until the 15 g aliquot passes. Report the retained mass. Stir in water only if the mash is dry: 5 ml (1 level teaspoon) at a time, maximum 10 ml total; stop the moment it holds together when pressed with the back of a spoon and the surface looks slightly wet, not watery. Record actual water ml (0–10) on the card. Do NOT blend: blending destroys curd structure. Do NOT add thickener — L5 is calibrated from this fork-mash starting point.

2. IDDSI TEST

Spoon tilt (pass/fail): tilt a spoonful 45°; the mass must sit in a pile, not slide, flow, or pour.
Fork pressure (supporting check only — record it, do not gate on it): press the mash flat with the fork tines; it must SQUASH and NOT recover its shape when the fork is lifted.
Fork drip is NOT in the L5 set — do not apply it. No syringe test at L5.

3. VISUAL QC

On the fork: soft, lumpy curds with a slight sheen, no pooled liquid in the cup of the tines. On the spoon: holds a mounded heap, surface slightly wet not watery, no milky runoff at the rim.

5. FAIL MODES

Smooth, glossy purée with no visible curds (from blending, or from pressing the mash through the sieve): a slight sheen on lumpy curds is fine — visible curds are the L5 requirement — but a curd-free glossy purée is not L5, it is L4 material. Re-make by hand-mash and use the sieve as a gauge only; do not re-blend and do not press the mash through the sieve. L5 is reachable for this food only by hand-mashing plus gauging.
Too wet: milky liquid pools and pours off the spoon → drain on a sieve or fine strainer for 60 s WITHOUT pressing, discard the run-off, re-test.
Dry, or curds >4 mm retained on the gauge → add 5 ml water, mash 30 s more, re-gauge; if curds are still >4 mm after a second attempt the custard is under-set — re-bake or start over.
