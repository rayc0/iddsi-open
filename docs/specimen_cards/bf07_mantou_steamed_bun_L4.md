1. PREP
Weigh 150 g steamed mantou, torn into ~2 cm pieces. Add 90 ml room-temperature water. Blend 60 s on high, scrape down, blend 60 s more. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue if any lump remains larger than a pea. Return puree to blender, add 2.0 g thickener per 100 ml of puree (calibration starting point — record actual grams used). Blend 20 s to incorporate. Rest 2 min, stir, retest. Hold warm is not required; test at ~20–25 °C.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: mound the puree on the fork tines and lift; it must sit in a mound above the tines and not drip continuously through. Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the mound until the blanched nail shows white through the puree; the surface must not separate, no thin liquid must pool. Spoon tilt test: scoop a heaped teaspoon, tilt the spoon 45°; the specimen must hold its shape and slide off as a single mass, not flow. L4 does not use the 10 ml/10 s syringe flow test (L3 only).

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is matte and uniform, no visible lumps or bun-fibre specks. Color is even off-white, no dark streaks from crust. Holds a clean ridge when the fork is dragged across the top; does not level out.

5. FAIL MODES
Specimen flows through fork tines or drips in a continuous thread → under-thickened, add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-blend. Thin liquid separates on the plate after pressure → too wet, return to blender with extra 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml. Lumps >4 mm remain after sieving, or puree cracks when the fork lifts → blend longer and sieve again before re-testing; do not proceed to media.
