PREP
Weigh 200 g tofu fa with syrup into the blender. Pulse-blend 8-10 s until curds break to roughly pea size. Gauge the curds against the 4 mm sieve once instead of screening them (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the curds fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap of the fork. Do not press the batch through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Discard what stays above the gauge. The per-level note allows spoon-curds ≤4 mm in their own syrup, so do not add water or thickener — none needed and none permitted at L5. Mash the curd briefly with the fork so it holds together but still shows soft pieces ≤4 mm. Record actual blender seconds and final mass on the capture sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines into a heaped spoonful until the thumbnail blanches white. The specimen must squash, not spring back, and must not flow through the tines. Spoon-tilt test: tilt a loaded spoon; the specimen sits in a clump, drops slowly only on firm tilt, leaves a thin coat, no free liquid pools. Fork-drip test: not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: soft irregular white curd flecks ≤4 mm, glossy with bound syrup, no loose liquid drop falling. On the spoon: heap holds shape when level, slumps only when tilted, surface matte-wet, not runny. Curds distinguishable, not a puree. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): lift fork from the cup; pieces may cling but no thin syrup drips freely.

FAIL MODES
Curds stay above 4 mm after sieving — re-sieve, do not re-blend to liquid. Syrup pools separate from curds — drain briefly, re-mash, retest. Curds puree to a smooth paste (over-processed, behaves like L4) — discard and remake with shorter blend.
