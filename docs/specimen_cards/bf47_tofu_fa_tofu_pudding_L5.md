PREP
Weigh 200 g tofu fa with syrup into the blender. Pulse-blend 8-10 s until curds break to roughly pea size. Sieve once through the 4 mm sieve, pressing gently with the fork; discard what stays above. The per-level note allows spoon-curds ≤4 mm in their own syrup, so do not add water or thickener — none needed and none permitted at L5. Mash the sieved curd briefly with the fork so it holds together but still shows soft pieces ≤4 mm. Record actual blender seconds and final mass on the capture sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines into a heaped spoonful until the thumbnail blanches white. The specimen must squash, not spring back, and must not flow through the tines. Spoon-tilt test: tilt a loaded spoon; the specimen sits in a clump, drops slowly only on firm tilt, leaves a thin coat, no free liquid pools. Fork-drip test: lift fork from the cup; pieces may cling but no thin syrup drips freely. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: soft irregular white curd flecks ≤4 mm, glossy with bound syrup, no loose liquid drop falling. On the spoon: heap holds shape when level, slumps only when tilted, surface matte-wet, not runny. Curds distinguishable, not a puree.

FAIL MODES
Curds stay above 4 mm after sieving — re-sieve, do not re-blend to liquid. Syrup pools separate from curds — drain briefly, re-mash, retest. Curds puree to a smooth paste (over-processed, behaves like L4) — discard and remake with shorter blend.
