PREP
Weigh 200 g ripe papaya flesh. Blend in consumer blender 30 s on high until smooth; do not sieve — sieving a blend-thin fruit strips the fibres that carry the L3 body. The specimen must pour, so thin with water: add 40 ml water and blend 5 s more. Pour into a cup and weigh total. Calculate thickener at 1.0 g per 100 ml of specimen as the calibration starting point; record the actual grams used. Sprinkle the thickener on the surface, rest 30 s for hydration, then stir 20 s with the fork until homogenous. Wait 2 min before testing. If the mix runs off the spoon like juice, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, stir, rest 1 min, retest. Up to three additions; beyond that, re-make.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press the fork tines into 10 ml of specimen; surface must yield to light pressure with the tines leaving a clear, persistent imprint.
Spoon tilt: a 5 ml spoonful on the spoon must hold its shape when the spoon is tilted to 45 degrees; it should slide off slowly as a single cohesive blob, not run.
Fork drip: lift the fork; the specimen drips continuously but slowly in a thick ribbon through the tines — it must not pour in a stream nor sit solid on the fork.
Syringe flow: fill the 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release, start timer. After 10 s, 8–10 ml must remain in the barrel. Above 10 ml = too thick, below 8 ml = too thin.

VISUAL QC
1. Glossy uniform orange surface with no free liquid pooling around the cup edge. 2. On the fork, it mounds then settles slowly into a soft peak that holds shape. 3. No fruit fibre strands, lumps, or unblended pulp visible.

FAIL MODES
Too runny: drips through fork in a thin stream and syringe reads under 8 ml — add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 1 min, retest once.
Too stiff: holds shape on tilted spoon and syringe reads over 10 ml — whisk in 5 ml water per 100 ml, rest 1 min, retest once.
Grainy or fibrous: blender time was too short — re-blend the batch 15 s on high before any thickener adjustment. If still grainy after one re-blend, discard and re-make from fresh fruit.
