PREP
Weigh 200 g of fresh pear, peeled and cored. Steam until soft, 8–10 min. Transfer to blender, add 80 ml water, blend 30–45 s until smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; press and discard any fibrous residue that will not pass — note this on the card, do not force it. Weigh the sieved purée; for L3 add thickener at the calibration starting point of 2.0 g per 100 ml purée, then stir 20–30 s and re-weigh. Add more thickener in 0.2 g increments per 100 ml only if the flow test fails; record the final grams used per 100 ml on the data sheet.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork 4–5 cm; liquid drips slowly in a continuous thin stream, not in drops.
Fork pressure: tines leave clear, persistent lines on the surface when pressed lightly.
Spoon tilt: spoon held at 30°; specimen flows off in a steady ribbon, not a lump, not a pour.
Syringe flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe, cut the tip as specified; after 10 s, 8–10 ml remains. Pass = 8–10 ml retained.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: thin even coating, drips in a slow continuous thread. On the spoon: smooth, glossy, no lumps or fibres, holds a soft mound that slumps when tilted. No free liquid pooling on the plate.

FAIL MODES
Too thin: drips in drops from fork, <8 ml retained in syringe. Re-make, add 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, retest. Too thick: holds shape on spoon tilt, >10 ml retained. Re-make, thin with 5–10 ml water, retest. Fibrous residue not sieved: catches in fork drip or syringe tip. Re-blend finer, re-sieve fully, retest.
