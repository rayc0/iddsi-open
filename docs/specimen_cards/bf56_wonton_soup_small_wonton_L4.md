**BF56 · wonton soup, small wontons 上湯雲吞 · target L4 (pureed: lump-free, thicker, holds a soft shape)**
**Bought form:** takeaway or frozen, cooked. **Allergens:** gluten (wheat wrapper), egg (wrapper and/or filling) — confirm against the pack label at portioning and log extras in `prep.notes`.
**Tests that apply at L4:** fork pressure (5.1), spoon tilt (5.2), fork drip (5.3).

1. PREP
- Cook: bring **500 ml** water to a brisk boil, add **200 g** frozen small wontons, simmer **4–5 min** until they float and the wrapper is fully tender. Lift out, drain **30 s**, and keep **100 ml** of the cooking broth. (With takeaway, weigh **200 g** cooked wontons and keep **100 ml** of their broth.)
- Blend: the **200 g** cooked wontons and **100 ml** reserved broth into the blender. Blend on high **45 s**, scrape the walls down once, blend **20 s** more (**65 s** total). Start on low and vent the lid if the broth is still hot.
- Sieve: press the blend through the **4 mm sieve** with the back of a spoon and discard what stays on the mesh (wonton-skin fragments and filling fibre). At L4 the sieve is the fibre/skin remover (equipment list item 3) and L4 is a lump-free level, so **no un-sieved specimen is tested**. Record the retained mass.
- Weigh the sieved purée: target **260–300 g** (record actual). Let it stand until it is **20–40 °C** before testing.
- Thicken — required, not optional: broth-thinned wonton purée will not hold a shape on its own. Whisk in thickener at the calibration starting point of **1.5 g gum-based per 100 ml** of purée (if only a starch-based thickener is stocked, start at **6.0 g per 100 ml**). Whisk **20 s**, rest **60 s**, stir **10 s**. Record the actual grams used in `prep.thickener_g`.
- Titrate against section 2 in **0.5 g (gum) or 1.0 g (starch) per 100 ml** steps, up to a ceiling of **4.0 g gum / 12.0 g starch per 100 ml**.

2. IDDSI TEST
- Fork pressure (5.1): press the fork tines flat into the purée on the tray until the thumbnail blanches white, hold **5 s**, lift. **Pass** = a clear impression remains, the surface does not rebound, and the purée does not squeeze through the tine gaps.
- Spoon tilt (5.2, L4 criterion): scoop a full spoon, hold it level, then tilt to **90°**. **Pass** = the purée holds its shape on the spoon and slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue left behind, and no separate thin liquid separates out.
- Fork drip (5.3, L4 criterion): scoop with the fork and hold it over the tray. **Pass** = the sample sits in a mound above the tines and does **not** flow through them.
- Separation check (QC rule 4): no thin broth may pool on the spoon or the tray at any point before the clip ends; any pooling fails the specimen at L4.
- Syringe flow: **skip** — 5.4 is required on every L3 only. Running the 10 ml / 10 s flow test here would invite thickening toward the L3 window and drop the specimen below L4, so it is not run and is not recorded.

3. VISUAL QC
- Smooth, glossy, pale-beige purée with no visible wonton-skin fragments, no filling flecks and no lumps.
- Sits in a defined peak above the fork tines and above the spoon rim; does not spread across the tray.
- No free broth at the tray edge or in the spoon well.

5. FAIL MODES
- Purée flows off the tilted spoon, runs through the fork tines, or thin broth separates and pools (**too thin**): whisk in **0.5 g gum (or 1.0 g starch) per 100 ml** of purée, rest 60 s, re-stir 10 s, re-test. This moves the specimen toward L4.
- Purée cracks or tears under the fork and will not spread (**too stiff** for a level that must hold a soft shape): thin with **10 ml** reserved broth per 100 ml of purée, re-blend **20 s**, re-test.
- Visible skin fragments or filling flecks after sieving: re-blend **20 s** and re-sieve through the 4 mm sieve; record the retained mass. If lumps persist, this batch cannot be made lump-free — log the attempt, mark the row FAIL, and re-cook a fresh 200 g portion.
- Free broth pooling under the mound: re-blend **20 s** to re-incorporate, then thicken in the steps above. Do not add water to a separated specimen — that moves it further from L4.
- Achievability, stated plainly: the reliable route to L4 is to blend the **whole** wonton (wrapper and filling) with its broth and then sieve out the skins. Blending the filling alone leaves a crumbly mass that will not hold a shape, and the broth on its own will never reach L4 — the thickener is required, not optional.
