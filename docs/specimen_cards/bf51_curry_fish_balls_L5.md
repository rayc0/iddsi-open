1. PREP
Weigh 200 g cooked curry fish balls with sauce. Chop with knife until largest piece is ≤ 4 mm; reject any piece that will not reduce. Mash thoroughly with a fork so sauce coats every fragment. Add sauce only if dry; do not add water. Do not blend — blending destroys the required particle texture. Do not sieve — sauce carries solids and sieving would strip them. No thickener — L5 must show visible particles and must not be a paste. Record actual chop time, final mash consistency, and sauce volume on the capture sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press fork tines into a 15 mm portion on a flat plate until thumbnail blanches; sample must squash and not recover its shape when fork is removed. Spoon tilt test: tilt a spoon holding ~5 mm of sample; sample must sit in a cohesive mound and must not slide off or drip. Fork drip test: lift sample on fork; no thin liquid should run off the bulk. No syringe flow test — that is an L3 calibration tool only.

3. VISUAL QC
Coarse minced texture with clearly visible flecks of fish and sauce specks, no whole balls, no pooling liquid at the plate edge, soft sheen indicating moist but not runny.

5. FAIL MODES
Visible thin liquid pooling — remake by draining excess sauce, no water added. Particles > 4 mm — recook batch, chop finer, recheck. Dry, crumbly mash that will not hold on spoon — add 5 ml sauce at a time, remash.
