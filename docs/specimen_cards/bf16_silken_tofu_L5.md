PREP
Weigh 250 g silken tofu onto the scale. Tip onto a clean plate; mash thoroughly with a fork until uniform — no whole curds remain. Press the mash through the 4 mm sieve using the back of the fork or a spatula, discarding anything that will not pass; re-mash any retained lumps and sieve again. The target is soft particles ≤ 4 mm with surface moisture, no pooling liquid. No blender is required and none should be used — the food list notes spoon-break to curds ≤ 4 mm, which the fork-and-sieve sequence delivers. No thickener is added; no water is added.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines flat into the specimen for one second. The specimen must squash, and the tine marks must remain — no elastic recovery. Fork-drip test: lift a forkful 5 cm above the plate; nothing should drip or run through the slots. Spoon-tilt test: tilt a spoonful slowly past vertical; the full portion must slide off together, not pour, drip, or fall as separate liquid. All three must pass to confirm L5.

VISUAL QC
On the fork, the specimen should hold its shape as a coherent lump with a glossy, moist sheen — not wet, not dry, not crumbly. On the spoon, it should mound as one piece that slides off together. No free liquid should pool on the plate.

FAIL MODES
Particles exceed 4 mm after sieving — re-mash and re-sieve. Free thin liquid pools on the plate — drain briefly on absorbent paper, do not add thickener. Specimen recovers shape after fork press — specimen is too firm, discard and remake from a fresh block using gentler mashing.
