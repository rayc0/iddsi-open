1. PREP
Steam 250 g whole eggplant (pierced twice) at 100 °C for 12–15 min, until a fork pressed into the flesh with the thumbnail blanching white sinks in and the flesh does not push back against the tines — the section 5.1 criterion. A knife sliding in is a kitchen doneness cue, not a level test: "no resistance" to a knife over-softens the flesh toward L5 and drives weep water up, so do not use it to decide readiness. Longer steaming increases weep water. Halve, cool 5 min, and scoop the flesh away from the peel; discard the peel. Remove seeds and pull out strings by hand. Weigh the usable flesh — expect 200–240 g — and use all of it; record the actual starting weight. If usable flesh falls below 180 g, do not top the pack up from a second eggplant: two fruits steamed in the same load can differ in firmness, and one specimen of two textures cannot be certified at one level — record the smaller mass and shoot it, or reject the pack. Cut on a clean board with a spoon or blunt knife into 8–15 mm pieces: every piece must measure 8–15 mm along its longest edge on the 100 mm ruler and must not be smaller than 8 mm. The 100 mm ruler is the L6 sizing instrument (protocol 5.1 and docs/SHOT_LIST.md: "the 100 mm ruler is the sizing reference for L6 pieces"); the 15 mm checkerboard in frame is the scale reference for the photograph and is not a substitute for the ruler read-out. Weigh the cut pieces and record the actual yield; the loss at this step is fines and crumbs from cutting, because seed and string were already removed before the weigh — record it, do not top it up, and do not plate or photograph any of it: sub-8 mm fines in frame are L5 material carrying an L6 label, and the photograph is the training input. Re-weigh the plated portion and confirm every piece on the plate reads 8–15 mm before any shot is taken. No thickener, no added liquid. Do not use a blender and do not pass the specimen through the 4 mm sieve: a 4 mm sieve cannot enforce a 15 mm limit, it would reduce the batch to ≤4 mm (L5), and it is not a gauge for an 8–15 mm specimen. If free water collects, drain in a colander 30 s — do not squeeze the pieces. Hold at ≥60 °C for no more than 20 min; capture within 10 min of draining.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test (5.1) — the only mapped test that applies at L6: press the fork tines into one piece until your thumbnail blanches white. PASS = the piece squashes, does not return to its shape, expresses no free water, AND stays in one piece that can be lifted clear on the tines. "It squashed and did not recover" is not on its own an L6 pass: a piece that breaks apart or smears into purée has failed in the L5 direction, and a piece that keeps a clean 8–15 mm edge and only dents under full thumbnail pressure has failed in the L7 direction. Size criterion (level definition, not a 5.x flow test): every piece 8–15 mm along its longest edge, read on the 100 mm ruler. No fork-drip (5.3 is L3/L4) and no spoon-tilt (5.2 is L3/L4/L5) at L6. No syringe test at L6.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces hold a soft, moist but matte surface — no free liquid pooling on the plate or spoon and no water squeezing out under the fork. Colour stays uniform purple-grey flesh with no stringy fibres pulling away and no seed-coat fragments. Every piece reads 8–15 mm along its longest edge on the 100 mm ruler, and no piece is collapsed or smeared at the cut faces.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended or over-worked to a purée or paste: a purée cannot be brought back to 8–15 mm pieces by any bench adjustment — discard the batch and re-make by hand-cutting only; the blender is not to be used on this specimen. Pieces left larger than 15 mm after cutting: re-cut, do not re-blend. Pieces under 8 mm, or a batch that collapses to 4 mm or less under its own weight: the flesh is too soft to hold an L6 piece, and this pack cannot be brought to L6 by any reliable bench method — the closest achievable level for this pack is L5 (lumps ~4 mm, moist, fork-mashable). Re-make once from a firmer pack, or mark the event FAIL with tests.pass = false and the reason in prep.notes. Do NOT relabel the specimen L5: protocol section 7 QC rule 1 forbids renaming a specimen's media into another level, and an L6 image re-labelled L5 is a corrupted ground-truth row in both directions. Free water pooling on the plate: drain in a colander 30 s, do not squeeze, and capture within 10 min; if water re-accumulates within 2 min of draining, the pack is too wet to hold L6 as served — re-make with a 12 min steam and record. If the 12 min remake still weeps within 2 min, this pack cannot hold L6 as served: mark the event FAIL rather than thinning the specimen or labelling it L5.
