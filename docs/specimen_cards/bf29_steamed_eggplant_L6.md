1. PREP
Start with 200 g steamed eggplant flesh, peel discarded. Place in blender; pulse 5-8 s only — just enough to break large chunks into rough pieces, do not purée. Pass through the 4 mm sieve once; discard any coarse strings or seeds that do not pass. Spoon-cut on a clean board into pieces no larger than 15 mm in any dimension. Measure 250 g of cut pieces. No thickener, no added liquid — L6 is the calibration starting point; record actual moisture if visible pooling. Hold at serving temperature.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test: press fork tines into a piece until thumbnail blanches white. Pass = piece squashes and does not return to shape. Fork-drip test: lift fork with a loaded piece; no liquid should drip through the tines within 10 s. Spoon-tilt test: place 3-4 pieces on a spoon and tilt to 45°; pieces must sit in a pile and not flow, run, or drip off. No syringe test at L6.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces hold a soft, moist but matte surface — no free liquid pooling on the plate or spoon. Color stays uniform purple-grey flesh with no stringy fibers pulling away. Pieces compress visibly under light fork pressure without springing back.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended to a purée or paste — fails fork-pressure and spoon-tilt, re-make with shorter pulse. Pieces left larger than 15 mm after spoon-cut — re-cut, do not re-blend. Excess free water pooling on the plate — drain on sieve 30 s, do not squeeze.
