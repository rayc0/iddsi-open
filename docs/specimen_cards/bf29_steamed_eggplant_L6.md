1. PREP
Steam 250 g whole eggplant (pierced twice) at 100 °C for 12–15 min, until a knife slides in with no resistance — longer steaming increases weep water. Halve, cool 5 min, and scoop the flesh away from the peel; discard the peel. Remove seeds and pull out strings by hand. Weigh the usable flesh — expect 200–240 g — and use all of it; record the actual starting weight. Cut on a clean board with a spoon or blunt knife into 8–15 mm pieces: every piece must fit inside one 15 mm checkerboard square in every dimension and must not be smaller than 8 mm. Weigh the cut pieces and record the actual yield; a loss beyond 5 g is discarded seed and string — record it, do not top it up. No thickener, no added liquid. Do not use a blender and do not pass the specimen through the 4 mm sieve: a 4 mm sieve cannot enforce a 15 mm limit, it would reduce the batch to ≤4 mm (L5), and it is not a gauge for an 8–15 mm specimen. If free water collects, drain in a colander 30 s — do not squeeze the pieces. Hold at ≥60 °C for no more than 20 min; capture within 10 min of draining.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test (5.1) — the only mapped test that applies at L6: press the fork tines into one piece until your thumbnail blanches white. PASS = the piece squashes, does not return to its shape, and expresses no free water. Size criterion (level definition, not a 5.x flow test): every piece 8–15 mm on the 15 mm checkerboard. No fork-drip (5.3 is L3/L4) and no spoon-tilt (5.2 is L3/L4/L5) at L6. No syringe test at L6.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces hold a soft, moist but matte surface — no free liquid pooling on the plate or spoon and no water squeezing out under the fork. Colour stays uniform purple-grey flesh with no stringy fibres pulling away and no seed-coat fragments. Every piece reads between the 8 mm and 15 mm marks of the checkerboard in all three dimensions, and no piece is collapsed or smeared at the cut faces.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended or over-worked to a purée or paste: a purée cannot be brought back to 8–15 mm pieces by any bench adjustment — discard the batch and re-make by hand-cutting only; the blender is not to be used on this specimen. Pieces left larger than 15 mm after cutting: re-cut, do not re-blend. Pieces under 8 mm, or a batch that collapses to 4 mm or less under its own weight: the flesh is too soft to hold an L6 piece, and this pack cannot be brought to L6 by any reliable bench method — the closest achievable level for this pack is L5 (lumps ~4 mm, moist, fork-mashable); either re-make from a firmer pack or relabel the specimen L5 and record why. Free water pooling on the plate: drain in a colander 30 s, do not squeeze, and capture within 10 min; if water re-accumulates within 2 min of draining, the pack is too wet to hold L6 as served — re-make with a 12 min steam and record.
