PREP
Weigh 150 g hor fun, fresh pack, blanched. Chop into 2-3 cm pieces. Add 80 ml water. Blend on high 45 s; stop, scrape down, blend 30 s more. Press through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Stir in thickener at 1.5 g per 100 ml of sieved liquid — calibration starting point only, record the actual g used and final ml. Stir 30 s, rest 60 s, stir 10 s. Re-weigh and record total specimen mass.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift fork from specimen at 5 cm/s; liquid drips in a continuous slow stream through the tines, drops form but do not pour. Fork pressure: fork prongs leave a clear, persistent score on the surface under light thumb pressure; surface does not flow back. Spoon tilt: small mound on a tilted spoon barely shifts; thick, controlled slide. IDDSI flow test: fill 10 ml syringe, cut at 10 ml mark, release for 10 s; 8-10 ml remains. All four must pass.

VISUAL QC
On spoon: smooth, glossy, no lumps, no noodle strands visible, thick coat that holds the spoon shape. On fork: a clean coat between tines, drips threadlike not watery. Color uniform off-white.

FAIL MODES
Too runny: drips pour off fork, syringe reads under 8 ml — add 0.25 g thickener per 100 ml, rest, retest. Too thick: holds shape on tilted spoon, syringe reads over 10 ml — add 5 ml water, stir, retest. Lumpy or grainy: sieve left residue — re-blend 20 s and re-sieve, then re-thicken to spec.
