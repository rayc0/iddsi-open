1. PREP
Weigh 120 g steamed bitter melon rings straight from the steamer; do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener or water. Keep rings intact as served. Portion the whole-food reference specimen onto the capture plate in a single layer with one ring standing on its edge for the side view.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press a dinner fork onto a ring with normal thumb pressure; the ring must flatten, break apart, or fully lose its shape under the fork tines — it must not return to its original shape when the fork is lifted. Spoon tilt: tilt a spoon holding a ring; the ring must slide off or fall off easily with minimal coaxing, not stay stuck to the spoon. Fork drip: lift a pierced ring on the fork; no continuous drip or flow through the tines. All three must pass to confirm L7.

3. VISUAL QC
Ring holds its hollow cross-section and pale-green outer skin. Edges look tender and moist, not mushy or collapsed. Wall thickness reads roughly even around the ring with no translucent breakdown zones.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-steamed ring collapses to a paste and passes L6 only — re-make with shorter steam. Under-cooked ring resists the fork and reads L5 or above — re-steam and re-test. Wet surface that drips through the fork tines fails the drip test — drain briefly on paper and re-shoot.
