1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed eggplant (skin removed, all visible seeds removed). Place in consumer blender with 40 ml water. Blend on high for 40 s, scrape the jar walls, blend 20 s more until uniform. Press through 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard residue. Weigh sieved purée. The per-level note is blend-then-sieve-hold: L4 is lump-free, so the sieve is mandatory and no specimen is judged before it has been pressed through — the blend step never replaces it. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 3 g per 100 g sieved purée (7.0 g for a 235 g yield); record the actual grams used. Stir 20 s, rest 60 s. If the fork-drip test fails, add 0.5 g per 100 g per increment, re-rest 60 s and re-test, up to a maximum of 5 g per 100 g.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch; puree leaves a clear imprint, no spring-back. Pass = clean impression, holds shape.
Fork drip: lift fork with a loaded mound; puree sits in a mound above the tines for 10 s with zero drip. Pass = zero drip in 10 s.
Spoon tilt: scoop, level the edge, tilt spoon to 45 degrees; specimen must not slide, pour, or drip. Pass = sticks to spoon, no flow.
Syringe test is NOT required at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is matte and even, no free liquid pooling around the mound. Color is uniform grey-violet, no dark skin flecks or seed specks. Mound on the fork keeps its peaked shape; the spoon-tilted sample stays put.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: drips through fork tines or runs off spoon — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g, rest 60 s, retest (maximum 5 g per 100 g).
Too thick / crumbly: cracks or breaks on fork press, loses sheen — add 5 ml water per 100 g, stir, retest.
Visible skin or seed specks after sieving: re-blend 15 s and re-sieve; the sieve is never skipped at L4.
