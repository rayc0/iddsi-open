1. PREP
Weigh 200 g steamed eggplant (skin removed, seeded if needed). Place in consumer blender with 40 ml water. Blend 30-45 s until uniform. Press through 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Weigh puree. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 3 g per 100 ml of puree; record the actual grams used. Stir 20 s, rest 60 s. If fork-drip test fails, add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-rest. Per-level note is blend-hold, so no sieving or shaping step is required beyond pressing out fibers.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch; puree leaves a clear imprint, no spring-back. Pass = clean impression, holds shape.
Fork drip: lift fork with a loaded mound; puree sits in a mound above the tines for 10 s with zero drip. Pass = zero drip in 10 s.
Spoon tilt: scoop, level the edge, tilt spoon to 45 degrees; specimen must not slide, pour, or drip. Pass = sticks to spoon, no flow.
Syringe test is NOT required at L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is matte and even, no free liquid pooling around the mound. Color is uniform grey-violet, no dark skin flecks or seed specks. Mound on the fork keeps its peaked shape; the spoon-tilted sample stays put.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: drips through fork tines or runs off spoon — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 60 s, retest.
Too thick / crumbly: cracks or breaks on fork press, loses sheen — add 5 ml water per 100 ml, stir, retest.
Visible skin or seed specks after sieving: re-blend 15 s and re-sieve; do not skip the sieve.
