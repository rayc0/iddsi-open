PREP
Weigh 200 g sweetcorn kernels (drained canned or thawed frozen, heated). Add 100 ml hot water. Blend 60 s. Pass through 4 mm sieve; press to express liquid; discard fibrous residue. Measure sieved liquid: top up with hot water to 200 ml total. Add 2.0 g thickener per 100 ml (calibration starting point; record actual mass). Stir 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 15 s. Per-level note "blend-thin" applies: thin to pour, not to chew.

IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: fork lifted 2 cm; liquid drips in slow, continuous threads through the tines within 10 s — pass. Fork pressure: pressing fork tines into 5 mm of specimen on a flat surface leaves a clear imprint without crumb splitting — pass. Spoon tilt: specimen on spoon holds its shape when tilted 45°, does not run off — pass. IDDSI flow test: fill 10 ml syringe, release; 8–10 ml remains in the syringe after 10 s — pass. Reject if < 6 ml (too thin) or > 11 ml (too thick).

VISUAL QC
On the fork: a smooth, glossy coat clings between tines with no kernels or yellow flecks visible. On the spoon: surface is flat and level, no pooling of free liquid. Colour uniform pale yellow, no graininess or streaks.

FAIL MODES
(1) Visible specks or yellow flecks after sieving: re-blend 15 s and re-sieve. (2) Syringe residual < 6 ml: thickener too low; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, stir, rest, retest. (3) Residual > 11 ml: over-thickened; discard and remake with reduced thickener.
