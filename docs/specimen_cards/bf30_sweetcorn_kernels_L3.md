1. PREP

Weigh 200 g sweetcorn kernels (drained canned or thawed frozen, heated). Add 100 ml hot water. Blend 60 s. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; press to express liquid; discard fibrous residue. Measure the sieved liquid and top up with hot water to 200 ml total. Add thickener at 2.0 g per 100 ml (4.0 g for the 200 ml batch), weighed to 0.1 g, as the calibration starting point; record the actual mass. Stir 30 s, rest 2 min, stir 15 s. Titrate to the syringe window in 0.5 g per 100 ml steps, each weighed. The BF30 per-level note "blend-thin" applies: thin the specimen to pour. Do not chase a chewable, shape-holding or imprint-leaving texture — those are higher levels, and an L3 specimen that holds an imprint is an L4 specimen.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork drip: fork lifted 2 cm clear of the surface; the specimen drips slowly through the tines in a continuous flow and leaves a coating on the fork. Pass = steady slow drip within 10 s; a free stream is too thin, a mound sitting above the tines is too thick. Spoon tilt: a full spoon tilted to 45° pours off in a slow continuous stream over 2-4 s, not instantly and not as one lump. Pass = slow pour, with no separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon or tray. Syringe flow test (L3, mandatory): fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release with the nozzle open, start the 10 s timer; pass = 8.0-10.0 ml remaining at t = 10 s with a measurable discharge. Any value outside 8.0-10.0 ml is corrected and re-tested; it is never logged as a pass. Fork pressure is not an L3 test and is not performed on this specimen.

3. VISUAL QC

On the fork: a smooth, glossy coat clings between the tines with no kernels, skins or yellow flecks visible. On the spoon: the surface is flat and level while held and pours as one slow ribbon when tilted; no pooling of free liquid. Colour uniform pale yellow, no graininess or streaks.

5. FAIL MODES

(1) Visible specks, skins or yellow flecks after sieving: re-blend 15 s and re-sieve; if flecks persist, discard and remake. (2) Too thin — residual <8.0 ml, or a stream off the fork: add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml (weighed to 0.1 g), stir, rest 2 min, retest. (3) Too thick — 10.0 ml remains with no discharge, or the specimen holds a mound on the spoon: thin with 10 ml hot water, re-blend 10 s, retest; if it still holds a mound, discard and remake at 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml. The two directions are not interchangeable: too thin is corrected by thickening, too thick by thinning, and no imprint criterion is ever used to judge this specimen.
