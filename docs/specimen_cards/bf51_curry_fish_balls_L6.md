1. PREP
Weigh 150 g cooked curry fish balls with sauce. Quarter each ball with a knife to pieces ≤15 mm; discard any piece >15 mm. Keep all sauce. Pulse in consumer blender 4 s, two pulses, just to coat pieces in sauce — do not purée. No sieve, no thickener, no added water. Note: the L3 thickener calibration is not applicable at L6; if any piece exceeds 15 mm after quartering, the batch fails — re-cut or do not submit.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines against a piece on a plate until thumbnail blanches white. Pass if the piece squashes, does not spring back, and breaks apart easily. Fork drip test does not apply at L6. Spoon tilt: scoop a piece with sauce onto a spoon and tilt; the piece must sit on the spoon without sliding off or dripping liquid freely. Also confirm piece dimensions with the 100 mm ruler — every piece must measure ≤15 mm in any direction.

3. VISUAL QC
On the spoon: irregular chunks no bigger than the 15 mm checkerboard square, glossy from sauce, no whole or half balls present. On the fork: pieces hold shape but yield visibly under thumb pressure. Sauce should coat, not pool, around the pieces.

5. FAIL MODES
Whole or half balls present (>15 mm) → re-quarter and re-shoot. Pieces spring back under fork pressure → piece too firm, re-cut or discard. Sauce so thin it pools and pours off spoon → drain excess sauce on a plate for 10 s, then re-shoot; do not add thickener.
