1. PREP — Place the whole de-seeded wedge on the board. Cut a 50 mm × 50 mm × 25 mm specimen from the flesh with a clean knife; do not crush or trim beyond removing rind and any white pith. Record mass on the scale (target ~80 g). Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add water, do not add thickener. The wedge-as-cut note is respected: the specimen is taken from a freshly cut surface, not reconstructed. L3 calibration does not apply at L7; record zero added thickener and zero added liquid.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat onto the specimen with normal thumb force; the flesh must crush, flake, or split cleanly and not return to its original shape when the fork is lifted. Fork drip test: lift the fork; nothing drips or runs through the tines. Spoon tilt test: a spoonful must fall off the tilted spoon with no residual film; this is a confirmation only and must not be relied on alone. No syringe flow test at L7.

3. VISUAL QC — On the fork: bright red flesh with visible juice on the surface but no free liquid pooling; tines leave a clean impression that does not spring back. On the spoon: a single soft mound that holds its shape briefly then releases. Specimen edges are sharp-cut, not ragged or pulped.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Specimen weeps free juice that pools on the plate or drips from the fork: re-cut from a drier interior face, do not blot. (b) Flesh is pulped or shredded by knife work, giving a puree appearance: re-cut a fresh specimen with one clean stroke. (c) Rind or white pith left attached, altering bite behaviour: trim and re-cut.
