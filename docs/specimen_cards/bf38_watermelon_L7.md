Applicable tests: fork pressure only. Fork drip and spoon tilt are L3/L4/L5 tests and must not be recorded as pass/fail at L7. No syringe flow test at L7.

1. PREP — Place the whole de-seeded wedge on the board. Cut a 50 mm × 50 mm × 25 mm specimen from the flesh with a clean knife; do not crush or trim beyond removing rind and any white pith. That cube is 62.5 cm³ and, at watermelon density (~0.95 g/cm³), weighs 55-68 g. Record mass on the scale, target 60 g. The size spec and the mass spec describe the same specimen: if the mass reads outside 55-68 g, the cube is not 50 × 50 × 25 mm and must be re-cut — do not reconcile the two by trimming to mass. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add water, do not add thickener. The wedge-as-cut note is respected: the specimen is taken from a freshly cut surface, not reconstructed. L3 calibration does not apply at L7; record zero added thickener and zero added liquid.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork pressure test: press the fork tines flat onto the specimen with normal thumb force; the flesh must crush, flake, or split cleanly and not return to its original shape when the fork is lifted. Sizing is part of the pass: measured against the 100 mm ruler, the cube reads 50 × 50 × 25 mm ± 2 mm and every dimension is ≥15 mm. No fork-drip test at L7, no spoon-tilt test at L7, and no syringe flow test at L7.

3. VISUAL QC — On the fork: bright red flesh with visible juice on the cut surface, which is expected and acceptable at L7; tines leave a clean impression that does not spring back. Specimen edges are sharp-cut, not ragged or pulped. Measured on the 100 mm ruler: 50 × 50 × 25 mm ± 2 mm.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Free juice on the cut faces, or juice pooling on the plate: this is normal watermelon behaviour and is NOT a fail condition at L7 — fork drip is not an L7 test. Do not blot, which wets the surface and destroys the sharp-cut edges, and do not drain on paper. If pooling makes the ruler unreadable in the hero frame, work on a chilled board, shoot within 60 s of cutting, or re-cut from a colder interior face; record the pooling on the capture sheet and keep the specimen. (b) Flesh is pulped or shredded by knife work, giving a puree appearance: re-cut a fresh specimen with one clean stroke. (c) Rind or white pith left attached, altering bite behaviour: trim and re-cut. (d) Cube measures under 15 mm in any dimension: re-cut to 50 × 50 × 25 mm; do not submit a smaller piece as L7 evidence.
