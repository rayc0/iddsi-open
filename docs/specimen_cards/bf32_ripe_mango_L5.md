1. PREP — Weigh 200 g ripe mango flesh on the digital scale; reserve natural juice. Pulse in the consumer blender 6-8 s only — do not purée to smooth. Transfer to bowl, mash firmly with fork 30 s to break fibres; keep dice <=4 mm. Pass mash through the 4 mm sieve using the fork back; discard any coarse strands that will not pass. Weigh back; record retained mass. Re-incorporate the reserved juice; stir to uniform moistness. No thickener added; no water added. Target final 180-200 g. This step is impossible if the sieve yields <120 g — then the mango is too fibrous for L5 and must be reported as off-spec.

2. IDDSI TESTS — Fork pressure: place fork tines down on 1.5 cm mound; press thumb on bowl until thumbnail blanches; the specimen must squash, leave clear tine marks, and not spring back. Spoon tilt: load 5 ml onto spoon; tilt 45°; specimen must sit in a cohesive pile, not slide or run. Fork drip: lift fork with sample; no thin liquid must drip through tines within 10 s. No separate thin liquid visible pooling on plate. All three must pass.

3. VISUAL QC — On fork: moist bright orange-yellow particles clearly visible, all <=4 mm, tines print into the sample without resistance. On spoon: holds a peaked mound at tilt, glossy surface, no free juice pooling at the rim.

5. FAIL MODES — (a) Blender run too long creates a smooth purée — fails fork pressure (springs back) and shows thin liquid; re-make by re-dicing fresh flesh, do not re-blend. (b) Sieve step skipped leaves >4 mm strands — fails particle size and pressure test; re-mash and re-sieve. (c) Juice drained off leaves dry crumb — fails spoon tilt (slides off); re-incorporate reserved juice or discard batch and start over.
