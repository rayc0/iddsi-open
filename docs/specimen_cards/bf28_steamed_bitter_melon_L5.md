PREP
Trim 200 g steamed bitter melon, discard seeds. Chop on board, then pulse in blender 5 s to break down. Gauge the mince against the 4 mm sieve instead of screening it (protocol sections 2 and 6 step 4): scoop a sample onto the mesh and confirm the particles fall through the 4 mm openings under their own weight or one light tap. Do not press the batch through — that screens it to a smooth purée, which is L4, not L5. Re-chop any coarse residue held back on the mesh. Mash the mince with a fork until uniformly minced. Add 15 ml water per 100 g minced portion to moisten; stir. Target particle size ≤ 4 mm throughout. L3 thickener calibration: not applicable at L5.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into specimen on plate; flesh must squash and not recover shape when fork is removed. Spoon tilt: tilt spoon 45 degrees; specimen must hold together without sliding or dripping. Fork drip: not applicable at L5 (5.3 covers L3/L4 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L5 evidence. L3 10 ml/10 s syringe flow test: not applicable.

VISUAL QC
On fork: discrete moist particles all clearly ≤ 4 mm, no visible fiber strands longer than 4 mm. On spoon: cohesive mound that holds its shape when spoon is tilted, surface glistening but no pooled liquid at edges. Color uniform green-cream, no translucent liquid separating in the plate well. By-eye check only (not a section 5.3 test at L5): lift fork with specimen; no thin liquid should run through tines separately.

FAIL MODES
Particles exceed 4 mm after sieving: re-blend 2 s, re-sieve. Specimen appears dry or crumbly on spoon tilt: add 5 ml water per 100 g, stir, retest. Thin liquid pools on plate beside mound: drain on sieve 30 s, retest fork drip. Re-make from fresh portion if any failure persists after two corrections.
