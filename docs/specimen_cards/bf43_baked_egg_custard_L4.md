1. PREP
Weigh 200 g of chilled baked egg custard into the blender cup. Blend 25-30 s on high until completely smooth with no curds or lumps visible. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard any residue retained on the mesh. Return sieved puree to the cup. If the puree flows through fork tines or loses mound shape on the spoon, add thickener 1.0 g per 100 ml of puree, stir 15 s, re-test. Record the actual grams added. Do not add water or milk; the custard's own moisture is the calibration starting point. Re-chill 5 min before testing.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip test: scoop puree, turn fork tines down for 10 s — puree must mound and sit above the tines, not drip or flow through. Fork pressure test: press fork tines into the surface until the thumb blanches; surface must retain a clear indentation with no surrounding liquid pooling. Spoon tilt test: place a heaped spoonful on a flat plate, tilt plate to 45 degrees — puree must hold shape and not slide, run, or drip. All three must pass to confirm L4.

3. VISUAL QC
Uniform pale yellow, glossy, no visible curds or browned skin fragments. Coats the spoon thickly without dripping off the edge. Mound on fork tines holds a clean peak for 10 s without slumping.

5. FAIL MODES
Puree flows through fork tines or drips in 10 s tilt — add thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml, stir, re-test. Surface weeps thin yellow liquid under fork pressure — blend a further 15 s, sieve again, re-chill. Lumpy or grainy texture after sieving — discard and start with a fresh 200 g cup; do not re-blend a failed batch.
