1. PREP — Weigh 200 g cooked white rice into the blender. Add 100 ml water. Blend 60 s on high, scraping once at 30 s. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Reweigh the sieved base and record the mass in g. For the IDDSI Level 3 liquidised target, dose the thickener from that weighed mass: calibration starting point 3.0 g thickener per 100 g of sieved base — operator records the actual grams used. Whisk into the sieved base, rest 2 min, re-whisk 5 s. Re-weigh the final specimen and record.

2. IDDSI TEST —
- Fork drip: lift a loaded fork and hold it level over the bowl. Pass = liquid drips through the tine gaps in a thin continuous stream, not a solid lump falling.
- Spoon tilt: load a spoon and tilt to 45°. Pass = cohesive but spreadable — the mass moves and spreads on the tilted spoon, leaving a slow-travelling trail; it must not sit as a fixed lump that will not move.
- 10 ml / 10 s syringe flow test (deciding test at L3): fill to the 10 ml mark, release, time 10 s. Pass = 8–10 ml remaining in the syringe (i.e., 0–2 ml flowed out), with the flow continuous through the full 10 s.
- Fork pressure: skip — not applicable at L3. Fork pressure is the L4/L6/L7 test; an L3 specimen is lump-free and flowing and must not be required to hold a pressure imprint.

3. VISUAL QC — On fork: smooth glossy coating, single colour, no whole grains or white flecks. On spoon: mound holds a peak briefly then levels; surface matte-sheen, no free liquid pooling. When the spoon tilts, the mass slides as one and leaves a slow-travelling trail.

5. FAIL MODES —
- Too thin (runs off the spoon; less than 8 ml remaining in the syringe at t=10 s, i.e. more than 2 ml flowed out): add 0.5 g thickener per 100 g of current specimen mass, whisk 30 s, rest 2 min, re-test.
- Too thick (mound stands and will not move on the tilted spoon; 10 ml still in the syringe at t=10 s with no measurable flow, or the slow drip stalls before 10 s): stir in 5–10 ml water, re-mix, re-test.
- Visible grains or flecks after sieving: re-blend 30 s and re-sieve. Re-make if any test still fails after two corrections.
