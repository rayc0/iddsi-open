1. PREP — Weigh 200 g cooked white rice into blender. Add 100 ml water. Blend 60 s on high, scraping once at 30 s. Pass through 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Reweigh blended base. For the IDDSI Level 3 liquidised target, mix thickener at the calibration starting point of 3.0 g per 100 ml — operator records the actual grams used. Whisk into the sieved base, rest 2 min, re-whisk 5 s. Re-weigh final specimen and record.

2. IDDSI TEST —
- Fork drip: liquid pours/drips slowly in a thin continuous stream through tine gaps. Pass = slow drip, not a solid lump falling.
- Fork pressure: tines leave clear indentation with no separate particles. Pass = single smooth mass.
- Spoon tilt: spoon holds its shape when tilted; does not run off the edge. Pass = cohesive but spreadable.
- 10 ml / 10 s syringe flow test: fill to 10 ml, release 10 s. Pass = 8–10 ml remaining in syringe (i.e., 0–2 ml flowed out).

3. VISUAL QC — On fork: smooth glossy coating, single colour, no whole grains or white flecks. On spoon: mound holds peak briefly then levels; surface matte-sheen, no free liquid pooling. When spoon tilts, mass slides as one and leaves a slow-travelling trail.

5. FAIL MODES —
- Too thin (runs off spoon, <8 ml in syringe): add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, re-mix, re-test.
- Too thick (mound stands, <2 ml in syringe, slow drip stalls): stir in 5–10 ml water, re-test.
- Visible grains or flecks after sieve: re-blend 30 s and re-sieve. Re-make if any test still fails after two corrections.
