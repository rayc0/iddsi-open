PREP
Cook 80 g thin wheat noodles in boiling water until fully tender, drain, and cool to room temperature. Weigh 100 g cooked noodles into the blender, add 30 ml water, and blend 45–60 s, scraping the lid and jar once. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard any residue and weigh what passed. In a small bowl, stir thickener into the blended mass at 2 g per 100 ml (calibration starting point — record the actual grams used and final texture). Let rest 2 min, then stir. Reject if any separate thin liquid pools or the mass flows through fork tines. If it flows, add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, stir, rest 2 min, retest. If it crumbles, add 5 ml water and re-blend 10 s.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-drip: lift a fork tines-down through the specimen; the mass must sit in a mound above the tines with no drip or flow for 10 s. Spoon-tilt: scoop, then tilt the spoon 45°; the specimen must hold its shape and not slide off. Fork-pressure: press the fork tines flat into the specimen; the surface should show a clear thumbprint imprint with no separate liquid squeezing out. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
Specimen mounds cleanly on the spoon with a smooth, glossy surface and no visible noodle strands or lumps. No thin liquid pools around the base on the plate. Fork tines come out coated but the mass stays heaped above, not dripping.

FAIL MODES
Too runny: flows through tines or forms a puddle — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 2 min, retest. Too stiff: cracks or crumbles on the spoon — add 5 ml water, re-blend 10 s, retest. Visible strands or grit: re-blend 30 s and re-sieve; if still present, re-make from a new cooked batch. Two failed re-makes: discard and start over with a fresh 80 g cook.
