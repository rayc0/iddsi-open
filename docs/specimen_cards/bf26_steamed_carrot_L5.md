(level L5 — lumps ~4 mm, moist, fork-mashable; applicable tests: 4 mm sieve as gauge, spoon tilt, fork pressure supporting)

1. PREP

Weigh 200 g steamed carrot. Mash with a fork on a plate until no lumps >4 mm remain. Do NOT press the mash through the 4 mm sieve — pressing with the back of a spoon grinds the mash finer than 4 mm and drifts the specimen toward L4. Use the 4 mm sieve as a GAUGE only: tip 20 g of the dry mash onto the sieve and tilt it 3 times without pressing or rubbing; return the material that falls through to the plate, and re-mash by hand anything retained on the mesh until the 20 g aliquot passes. Discard only woody core fibre that will not break down. Weigh mash. Stir in 10 ml water per 100 g mash to moisten; do not add so much that liquid pools. Do not blend — a blender reduces carrot to a smooth puree and loses the L5 particle character. No thickener required for this level; if the mash looks dry and crumbly, dust in up to 0.5 g thickener per 100 g and re-test.

2. IDDSI TEST

Spoon tilt (pass/fail): tilt a spoonful 45°; the mass must sit in a pile, cohesive, sliding slowly as a single lump rather than flowing off. Fork pressure (supporting check only — record it, do not gate on it): press fork tines into the specimen on a plate until the tines blanch; the mash must squash, leave a clear dent, and NOT spring back. Spoon tilt must pass. Fork drip and the syringe flow test are NOT in the L5 set — do not apply them.

3. VISUAL QC

On the fork: discrete, soft orange flecks, none longer than a tine-width (~4 mm), surface glistens but no shine of free liquid. On the spoon: holds the scoop shape, mound stays together when plate is tapped.

5. FAIL MODES

Free liquid pools on the plate within 60 s → too much water added; drain on the 4 mm sieve for 30 s without pressing, or re-make with 5 ml less water per 100 g.
Mash is dry and crumbly and will not hold a pile → too dry; add 5 ml water per 100 g, or dust in 0.5 g thickener per 100 g, and re-test.
Mash is smooth/pureed with no visible flecks → over-blended or pressed through the sieve; re-mash a fresh portion by hand and stop at 4 mm — do not blend, do not press the mash through the sieve.
Particles retained on the 4 mm gauge exceed 4 mm, or the mass springs back under the fork → insufficient mashing; re-mash by hand and re-gauge. Do not re-pass the whole mash through the sieve to correct this.
