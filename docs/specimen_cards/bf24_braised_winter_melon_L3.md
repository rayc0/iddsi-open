1. PREP
Weigh 200 g braised winter melon onto the scale. Add 60 ml water. Blend on high for 45 s until fully broken down. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard residue. Return puree to the blender cup. Add thickener at 1.5 g per 100 ml of puree as the calibration starting point; blend 10 s to incorporate. Pour into the capture bowl. Record the actual thickener mass used per 100 ml before adding. Reject the batch if total added thickener drifts outside 1.2-1.8 g per 100 ml without re-sieving.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: hold fork vertically; liquid should not flow in a continuous stream, drop slowly in 2-4 s drips. Pass: drips every 2 s or slower, no steady stream.
Fork pressure: press fork tines flat into surface; if tines leave a clear imprint and surface does not return to level, reject as too thick for L3; accept only if surface barely retains a faint mark.
Spoon tilt: spoon should pour readily off the tilted edge but not run; pass if it slides in a thick ribbon, not a thin stream.
Syringe flow: fill 10 ml IDDSI syringe, release for 10 s. Pass: 8-10 ml remaining in barrel. Outside that range, re-adjust and re-record.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface sheen even, no visible fibres or melon skin flecks. Coats back of spoon with thin film. Fork tines show pale, translucent coating when lifted.

5. FAIL MODES
Too thin: drips in a steady stream, syringe drops below 8 ml. Re-make by sieving again and adding 0.2 g thickener per 100 ml, re-test.
Too thick: spoon piles, fork holds shape. Re-make by diluting with 10 ml water and re-sieving, never add more thickener without re-sieving.
Fibrous: visible strings on fork lift or sieve residue. Reject; re-blend 30 s and re-sieve before adjusting thickness.
