1. PREP
Weigh 150 g canned or frozen sweetcorn, drained. Pulse in the consumer blender for 5-8 s — do not purée; aim for chopped kernels under 4 mm. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard overs. Return fines to a bowl, add 15 ml water per 100 g solids, and mash firmly with a fork for 30 s until cohesive and visibly moist with no pooled liquid. If it sits wet, stir in thickener 1 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point, then record the actual grams used. Fork must pass pressure test before filming.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the fork tines into the specimen until the tines blanch fully white; on release the flesh stays compressed and does not spring back — pass. Spoon tilt: a loaded spoon held at 90 degrees holds the specimen for 10 s without sliding off — pass. No fork drip test applies (L5, not L3); no thin liquid should separate when the specimen sits on a plate for 1 minute.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: granular but cohesive mass, no whole kernel slipping off, surface sheen from added water. On the spoon: holds a rounded heap without slumping. On the plate: leaves a wet trail, no puddle around the mound.

5. FAIL MODES
Whole kernels survive blending above 4 mm — re-pulse and re-sieve. Specimen slides off the spoon at 90° — add thickener in 1 g per 100 ml steps and re-mash. Pooling thin liquid appears on the plate — blend in extra thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml until cohesive; re-shoot from step 2.
