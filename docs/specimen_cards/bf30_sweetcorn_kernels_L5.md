1. PREP
Weigh 150 g canned or frozen sweetcorn, drained and blotted dry (record the drained weight). Pulse in the consumer blender in 1 s bursts for 5–8 s total — do not purée; target pieces of 2–4 mm. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; the sieve is a GAUGE — every piece must pass a 4 mm opening without applied pressure. Discard overs; if more than ~15 g is retained, weigh in an equal mass of extra drained kernels so the batch stays at 150 g. Do not force material through and do not blend below ~2 mm, which gives L4. Return the fines to a bowl, add 22 ml water (15 ml per 100 g drained solids), and mash firmly with a fork for 30 s until cohesive and visibly moist with no pooled liquid. If it sits wet, stir in thickener at 1 g per 100 g of drained kernels — weigh the solids, this specimen has no 100 ml — as the calibration starting point, then record the actual grams used. The fork pressure supporting check must pass before filming.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure (supporting check only at L5 — not a primary L5 test): press the tines into the mound on a flat white plate with normal thumb pressure. The specimen must be mashable with the side of the fork, the mound must hold its shape after release, and the tines must NOT blanch white. Blanching tines means the specimen has been taken past L5 toward L4 — re-make.
Spoon tilt (applies at L3/L4/L5): a loaded spoon holds a rounded heap at rest without slumping; tilted to 90° the specimen slides off as one mass rather than slumping into a paste, pouring, or crumbling.
No fork drip test applies (L5, not L3/L4). Free liquid: the specimen must be moist, but no thin liquid may pool in a ring wider than 10 mm around the mound after it sits on a plate for 1 min.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: granular but cohesive mass, no whole kernel slipping off, surface sheen from added water. On the spoon: holds a rounded heap without slumping at rest, and slides off as one mass when tilted. On the plate: leaves a thin wet film or trail, no pool of free liquid around the mound — a ring wider than 10 mm fails.

5. FAIL MODES
Whole kernels survive above 4 mm — re-pulse 2–3 s and re-sieve, then top up with extra drained kernels to restore 150 g.
Slumps into a paste or pours off the spoon when tilted (too wet / over-mashed) — drain and blot the surface 10 s with paper towel, or stir in 25 g additional drained kernels, and re-mash 15 s. If it still will not cohere, bind with 1 g thickener per 100 g drained solids and re-mash 30 s; re-shoot from step 2.
Crumbles and will not hold a heap (too dry) — add 5 ml water per 100 g of drained kernels, re-mash 15 s, re-test.
Free liquid pools in a ring wider than 10 mm after 1 min (too wet) — drain, blot 10 s, stir in 25 g drained kernels and re-mash 15 s; thickener only as a last resort at 0.5 g per 100 g of drained solids, never per 100 ml.
