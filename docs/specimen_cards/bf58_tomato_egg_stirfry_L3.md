**Bought form & allergens:** bought as a ready-cooked chilled/refrigerated tomato-and-egg stir-fry (deli or takeaway), or home-made from fresh tomato, egg, oil, salt and a little sugar. Allergens: egg (declarable); soy and wheat if soy sauce is used; sesame if sesame oil is used — check the bought pack's label.

1. PREP
- Weigh 250 g of the stir-fry into the blender jug; reject any burnt or bitter portion.
- Add 80 ml warm water (40–50 °C) and blend 45–60 s until smooth — no egg grains or skin flecks visible when a spoonful is held to the light.
- Press through the 4 mm sieve with the back of a ladle; discard the retained skin and curd. At L3 the 4 mm sieve is the lump-free gauge (the restriction on sieving applies at L5, not L3).
- Measure the sieved volume V in ml and record it. Using one thickener type throughout the study (e.g. a xanthan-based dysphagia thickener), add 2.5 g per 100 ml of V while stirring briskly; let stand 60 s; stir again 15 s.
- Calibrate by test, not by feel:
  - If it runs off the fork in a continuous stream (too THIN): add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml steps, up to a ceiling of 4.0 g per 100 ml.
  - If it will not leave the fork at all and just mounds there (too THICK): whisk in 10 ml warm water per 100 ml, then retest.
- Record the actual grams of thickener per 100 ml used.

2. IDDSI TEST
- ACTIVE — 5.2 spoon tilt. Take a level spoonful; tilt the spoon smoothly to 30–45° over the bowl. PASS: the specimen slides off as one mass within 3 s and leaves a thin, complete film on the back of the spoon.
- ACTIVE — 5.3 fork drip. Lift a loaded fork and hold it level over the bowl. PASS: the specimen drips through the tines slowly in separate dollops over 5 s — not a continuous stream, and not sitting there refusing to move.
- ACTIVE — 5.4 syringe flow. Fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release the plunger, time 10 s. PASS: 8–10 ml remain in the syringe at t = 10 s. Below 8 ml remaining = too thin (reads L2); above 10 ml remaining = too thick (reads L4). All three active tests must pass on the same specimen.
- SKIP — 5.1 fork pressure. Applies at L4/L6/L7 (supporting check only at L5); an L3 specimen is a flowing, lump-free liquid with no shape to hold, so a fork press cannot separate L3 from L4 — the syringe and fork tests do that.
- L3 verdict = all three active tests pass together.

3. VISUAL QC
- A spoonful coats the back of the spoon completely, no gaps, no lumps catching the edge.
- A ribbon laid on a flat plate settles to a smooth mound within 2–3 s and does not run out.
- Uniform pale orange-yellow; no tomato skin flecks, no egg curds, no oil film on the surface.
- No blending bubbles on the surface — rest 60 s before shooting.

5. FAIL MODES
- Syringe leaves <8 ml at 10 s (too thin, reads L2): re-blend 15 s with the thickener, then add 0.5 g per 100 ml thickener and retest — thickening moves it toward L3. Do not add water.
- Syringe leaves >10 ml at 10 s (too thick, reads L4): whisk in 10 ml warm water per 100 ml and retest — thinning moves it toward L3.
- Fork drip runs as a continuous stream (too thin): add thickener 0.5 g per 100 ml, retest.
- Fork drip will not move off the fork (too thick): whisk in 10 ml warm water per 100 ml, retest.
- Lumpy — tomato skin flecks or egg curds block the sieve: add 20 ml warm water, re-blend 60 s, re-sieve through the same 4 mm sieve. If a second pass still blocks, discard the batch and remake — a lumpy specimen is neither L3 nor L4, so do not relabel it at a coarser level.
