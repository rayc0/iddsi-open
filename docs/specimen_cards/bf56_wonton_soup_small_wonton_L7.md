1. PREP

Cook the wonton soup per pack instructions — boil 500 ml water, add 200 g frozen wontons, simmer 4-5 min until floating, add seasoning/soup sachet. Serve one portion: ladle 250-300 g total (broth + wontons) into the capture bowl. Do NOT blend, sieve, or thicken. The per-level note says "wontons in soup as served" — this IS the whole-food reference specimen. Place 4-5 wontons in broth, keeping skin intact. Record actual served mass on the scale log.

2. IDDSI TEST

- Fork pressure test: press fork tines into one wonton with normal thumb pressure. Wonton skin should resist, compress slightly, then spring back. Pass = no full squish-through, shape recovers when fork lifts. Soup itself is liquid — that is expected at L7; the L7 claim applies to the wonton solids.
- Spoon tilt test: scoop one wonton plus 5 ml broth, tilt spoon at 90 degrees. Wonton stays on spoon; broth pours. Pass = solid component does not slide off.
- Fork drip test: lift fork with broth only. Broth drips continuously in a thin stream. Pass = continuous drip confirms liquid soup component, consistent with L7 food-in-liquid.

3. VISUAL QC

- Wonton skin smooth and intact, no tears or starch bloom.
- Broth clear to lightly cloudy, golden, no lumps.
- Wontons hold a rounded dumpling shape, not collapsed or split.

5. FAIL MODES

- Wontons split during cooking → solids no longer hold shape; re-make with shorter simmer.
- Skin ruptures under fork thumb pressure → overcooked; re-make and reduce cook time by 1 min.
- Broth over-reduced and syrupy instead of fluid → re-make with full water volume; L7 requires the liquid component to actually drip.
