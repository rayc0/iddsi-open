PREP
Dice 200 g ripe mango flesh on a clean board into 8–12 mm cubes. Confirm no piece exceeds 15 mm by visual check against the 100 mm ruler; reject any oversized cube. Do not blend, sieve, or thicken. Ripe mango at L6 needs no liquid, no gum, and no mechanical breakdown. State in notes: per-level note caps pieces at 15 mm — dicing only.

IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure: press the tines flat onto a piece with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass if the piece squashes, does not spring back, and the thumbnail clearly whitens. Fork drip: lift a loaded fork; pieces must not drip through or fall. Spoon tilt: tilt a spoon holding 4–5 pieces; pieces stay without sliding off.

VISUAL QC
On the fork: cubes hold shape but glisten with fibrous moisture; surface looks soft, not rubbery. On the spoon: pieces sit in a shallow pool of their own juice, edges rounded, no intact skin strips. Color is uniform mango yellow-orange with no green.

FAIL MODES
Pieces over 15 mm: re-dice smaller. Under-ripe fruit springs back under thumb: re-make with riper mango or discard. Excess juice pools and drips through fork tines: drain 30 s on the sieve, re-shoot.
