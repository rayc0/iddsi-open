Steamed carrot — L7 Regular — desk-specimen capture

PREP
Use 250 g fresh peeled carrot, steamed soft in a steamer basket 18–20 min until a fork pierces it with no resistance. Cut into batons ≈50 mm long × 15 mm cross-section, matching how the food is normally served. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. This is the L7 reference specimen. Plate three batons for the test, one baton for each still, one baton for each clip.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the baton until the blanched nail whitens. The carrot must compress, mash, and break apart under the fork — it must not return to its original shape. Spoon tilt: lift a broken piece on a standard teaspoon and tilt; the piece stays on the spoon, it does not flow or drip. Fork drip: lift a baton on fork tines and hold over the plate; no liquid runs through or drips off. All three must pass to confirm L7.

VISUAL QC
Muted orange surface, slight sheen from steaming, no pooled liquid on the plate. Edges of batons are rounded from cooking, not sharp. Surface breaks into coarse granules, not paste, under fork press.

FAIL MODES
Baton still firm or springs back under fork — under-cooked, re-steam 4 min and re-shoot. Water pools on plate — drain batons on paper towel 30 s before plating. Carrot collapses to puree on fork — over-cooked, discard batch and start with a fresh carrot and 2 min less steam.
