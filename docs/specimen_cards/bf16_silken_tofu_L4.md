1. PREP
Weigh 200 g silken tofu directly into the blender jar. Add 30 ml water. Blend 10 s on high; stop, scrape the walls with a fork, blend a further 10 s. Hold the lid for the full cycle. Sieve the blend once through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a fork — discard any retained solids or note them. The per-level note says "brief blend, blend-hold", so do not over-process: target is smooth but cohesive, not aerated or glossy-runny. No thickener is specified by the food list for L4; this is the calibration starting point. Record the actual gram weight on the capture sheet. Do not add ice, salt, oil, or acid. Keep the specimen covered until shot 1 to prevent surface skin.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork drip: lift a loaded fork (tines loaded across the full width) 5 cm above the plate. Specimen must sit as a mound above the tines and fall off in a single clump within 10 s — no thin tail, no drip through gaps. Fork pressure: press the fork tines flat into the mound; the specimen must yield to clear thumb pressure (1-2 mm) and not return to shape, and must not run between tines. Spoon tilt: a level 5 ml spoon heaped and tilted 45° must hold its shape with no flow, no drip from the spoon lip, no separate thin liquid pooling on the plate.

3. VISUAL QC
Surface is matte-smooth, no visible tofu curds, no weeping liquid ring on the plate after the spoon tilt. Mound on the fork retains defined edges and a flat top. One uniform off-white colour throughout.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-blended: glossy, aerated, flows through fork tines — discard, re-make with 5 s less total blend. Under-blended: visible curds caught on the 4 mm sieve — discard, re-make with 10 s more total blend after scraping. Added water too high: thin tail on fork drip or liquid pools on plate — discard, re-make with 10 ml less water. Any fail = full re-make, do not patch.
