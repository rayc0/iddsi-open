**Steamed shrimp, peeled — L7 (whole/regular) — desk-specimen capture**

1. PREP
Bought form (BASE_FOOD_LIST BF19): fresh or frozen peeled shrimp, steamed. Allergen: **crustacean** — confirm against the pack label; wash down tray, fork and scale before the next food.
Weigh 200 g of raw peeled shrimp on the 0.1 g digital scale and record the actual mass. Steam in a basket over boiling water for 5–7 min (start at 6 min), stopping when the flesh is opaque pink-white and just firm; do not overcook. Drain 30 s on paper towel, cool 2 min on the plate. Plate 3 whole shrimp beside the capture kit and record the plated mass. Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve — no 4 mm sieve is used at L7 (it is the L5 particle gauge and cannot size a ≥15 mm piece). Do NOT add thickener, water or any liquid. Per-level note "whole as steamed" applies: the specimen is the intact cooked shrimp exactly as served.

2. IDDSI TEST
ACTIVE at L7 — **5.1 fork pressure**: place a whole shrimp on the tray and press the bowl of the standard metal fork down with the thumb until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass = the shrimp squashes/compresses under that pressure, changes shape, and does not recover its shape when the fork is lifted (clear deformation, no spring-back). Camera must show food, utensil action and result in one unbroken take.
Ruler sizing check in the same take (a size check, not an IDDSI flow test): hold one shrimp against the 100 mm ruler — each specimen piece is a single whole shrimp ≥15 mm.
NOT APPLICABLE AT L7 — named only so the test set is unambiguous:
- Spoon tilt (5.2): **skip** — 5.2 applies at L3, L4, L5 only; log `tests.spoon_tilt = null`. Not performed, no pass/fail recorded.
- Fork drip (5.3): **skip** — 5.3 applies at L3, L4 only; log `tests.fork_drip = null`. Not performed, no pass/fail recorded.
- Syringe flow (5.4): **skip** — 5.4 is required for every L3 only (8–10 ml remaining at t=10 s); log `tests.flow_ml_remaining = null`. Not performed at L7.
L7 is decided on fork pressure plus the ≥15 mm whole-piece check; flow behaviour is not an L7 criterion.

3. VISUAL QC
Whole peeled shrimp, intact C-shape, no breakage at the bend. Surface opaque pink-white, matte-dry, light surface moisture only, no pooled liquid on the plate. Each shrimp sits across the fork tines without falling through and measures ≥15 mm against the 100 mm ruler. 15 mm checkerboard and 100 mm ruler fully in frame, specimen beside them, never covering them.

5. FAIL MODES
(a) Shrimp springs back fully under fork pressure (rubbery) — under-steamed, too firm for L7; steam a fresh 200 g batch 2–3 min longer and re-test (softer → toward L7).
(b) Shrimp turns mushy and pasty and no longer holds as a whole ≥15 mm piece — over-steamed, reads toward L6/L5; discard and steam a fresh 200 g batch for 5–7 min only, stopping at just opaque (firmer → toward L7).
(c) Shrimp measures under 15 mm — reads L6, not L7; re-portion using a larger shrimp size (e.g. 26/30 count) so every piece is ≥15 mm. Never chop, mash or blend smaller to "fix" it — that moves toward L6/L5.
(d) Liquid pools on the plate — pat the shrimp dry with paper towel before shooting; do not re-cook and do not add liquid.
