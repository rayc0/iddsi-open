1. PREP
Steam peeled shrimp per usual household method until just opaque and firm; do not overcook. Cool briefly on the plate. Weigh 200 g of steamed shrimp as the starting specimen. Do NOT blend. Do NOT sieve. Do NOT add thickener or liquid. Specimen is the whole cooked shrimp as served. Per-level note "whole as steamed" applies: the food is presented intact, identical to the reference.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into a shrimp on the plate until thumbnail blanches white. The shrimp must compress, change shape, and not return to original form. Pass = clear deformation, no spring-back.
Fork drip / spoon tilt: lift shrimp on fork and tilt over the plate; tilt a spoonful. Pass = no drip, no flow; shrimp holds on the utensil.
L7 requires the fork-pressure test only; flow and syringe tests do not apply. If any sample spreads, drips, or flows off the utensil, specimen is not L7.

3. VISUAL QC
Whole peeled shrimp body, intact C-shape, no breakage at the bend. Surface matte-dry, no pooled liquid on plate. Colour opaque pink-white with light surface moisture only. Check the fork: shrimp sits across the tine gap without falling through.

5. FAIL MODES
Over-steaming causes mushy texture that smears and drips on the spoon tilt — fails L7; re-steam a fresh batch to just opaque.
Under-steaming leaves rubbery shrimp that springs back fully under fork pressure — fails L7; re-steam briefly.
Pooled cooking liquid on plate makes the specimen drip from the fork — pat shrimp dry with paper towel before shooting; do not re-cook.
