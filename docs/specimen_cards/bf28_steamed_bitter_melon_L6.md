1. PREP
Start with 200 g bitter melon flesh, seeds and pith removed. Steam 20–25 min until a thumbnail press leaves a clean, persistent white mark (maximum achievable softness); cool 60 s. Slice into pieces 8–15 mm in every dimension, measuring each against the 100 mm ruler: no piece may be under 8 mm or over 15 mm. Re-cut any piece over 15 mm; set aside fines under 8 mm. Do not blend and do not sieve. No thickener, no added water or liquid. The pieces must hold their own shape on the fork.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork-pressure test (the only L6 test): press the fork tines onto a representative 8–15 mm piece on a flat plate until the thumbnail blanches white. Pass criterion: the piece squashes and does not recover its shape when the fork is lifted. Repeat on three pieces from different parts of the batch. Confirm with the ruler that each tested piece measures 8–15 mm (not under 8 mm, not over 15 mm).
Spoon tilt: not applicable for L6.
Fork drip: not applicable for L6.
Syringe flow: not applicable for L6.
L6 is confirmed by fork-pressure alone plus the 8–15 mm ruler check.

3. VISUAL QC
Pieces on fork should look moist but not wet, with visible ridges from the melon interior intact. Edges are soft and rounded, not torn or fibrous. Colour is translucent olive-green; no free liquid pools on the plate. A thumbnail press leaves a clean, persistent white mark.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces larger than 15 mm: re-cut smaller, do not re-steam. Pieces under 8 mm: re-cut from a larger piece; do not certify sub-8 mm fines as L6.
Pieces rubbery or springing back under the thumbnail (too firm, L7-like): re-steam 2–3 min longer and re-test — a firmer piece needs more softening. If the flesh is still rubbery and springs back after a total of 25 min steaming, it cannot reach L6 softness by this bench method: the specimen behaves as L7 (firm, needs chewing) and must NOT be certified L6.
Pieces breaking to puree or releasing free water (too soft, L5-like): discard batch, reduce steam time on the next batch.
Reliability: bitter melon flesh steamed on its own tends to stay rubbery/fibrous and most often lands at L7 rather than L6. L6 is reached only if the flesh softens enough to squash under the fork while holding 8–15 mm pieces; if it will not, certify honestly as L7 or choose a softer food — do not certify as L6.
