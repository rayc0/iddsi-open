1. PREP

Weigh 200 g tofu fa into the blender cup. Blend 30 s until smooth. Press through the 4 mm sieve into a bowl with the back of a spoon; discard coarse residue. Measure the sieved puree (expect about 180 ml). If the puree is stiffer than a slow pour, add measured warm water in 10 ml steps and record the total added — L3 is reached by thinning first, and water is the only thing that thins. Add thickener at the calibration starting point of 2.5 g per 100 ml (4.5 g for a 180 ml batch), weighed to 0.1 g; sprinkle, whisk, rest 90 s, whisk again. Titrate to the syringe window in 0.25 g per 100 ml steps. Record the final grams of thickener per 100 ml and the total water on the capture sheet. Thickener only ever thickens: it is never added to correct a too-thick reading.

2. IDDSI TEST

Fork drip: lift a loaded fork clear of the bowl; the specimen drips slowly through the tines in a continuous flow and leaves a coating on the fork. Pass = steady slow drip; a free stream is too thin, a mound sitting on the tines is too thick. Spoon tilt: a full spoon tilted to 45° pours off in a slow continuous stream over 2-4 s, with no separate thin liquid pooling on the spoon or tray. Pass = slow pour. Syringe flow test (L3, mandatory): fill the 10 ml syringe to the 10 ml mark, release with the nozzle open, start the 10 s timer; pass = 8.0-10.0 ml retained at 10 s with a measurable discharge. <8.0 ml retained is too thin (L2 territory); 10.0 ml retained with no discharge is too thick (behaves as L4). Fork pressure is not an L3 test and is not performed on this specimen.

3. VISUAL QC

Even glossy surface, no lumps or bean curds visible after the sieve. On the spoon the specimen coats the bowl in an even layer and pours off as one slow ribbon; no bare streaks, and no whey or syrup running ahead of the body. On the fork it clings as a smooth continuous film that releases as a slow drip when tilted.

5. FAIL MODES

Too thin: flows freely off the fork as a stream, or <8.0 ml retained; re-add 0.25 g thickener per 100 ml (weighed to 0.1 g), rest 60 s, re-test. Too thick: holds a mound on the spoon, or 10.0 ml is retained with no measurable discharge at 10 s; re-blend 5 s with 5 ml warm water per 100 ml (9 ml at a 180 ml batch) and re-test — thinning, never more thickener; if it still holds a mound, discard and remake at 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml. Lumpy or grainy surface: the sieve step was skipped or the blend ran under 30 s; discard, re-blend the full 30 s, re-sieve. Tofu fa does reach L3 reliably by this route — blend, sieve, thin with measured water, then thicken to the window; if the window cannot be reached after two corrections, discard the batch and mark FAIL rather than logging it at L4.
