1. PREP — Weigh 250 g peeled ripe mango flesh. Add 60 ml water. Blend 45 s on high until smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve; discard fibrous residue; you should obtain roughly 280 ml of purée. Transfer to a cup on the digital scale and tare. Add thickener at 3 g per 100 ml of purée (≈8.4 g for 280 ml) as the calibration starting point. Whisk 20 s, rest 2 min, re-whisk 10 s. If the flow test shows less than 8 ml retained, add thickener in 0.5 g increments and retest. The per-level note "blend-thin" applies: sieve first, then thicken; do not skip the sieve.

2. IDDSI TEST — Fork drip: spoon 5 ml, tip a fork; the drop falls in a continuous thin stream over 4-6 s, not a single drop. Fork pressure: press fork tines flat into the surface for 5 s; the surface blanches but the fork does not sink through, and the specimen does not run between tines. Spoon tilt: tilt a heaped spoon 45°; the specimen slides slowly and pools rather than pouring. Syringe flow test (the L3 gate): draw 10 ml into the IDDSI syringe, start the timer, release; after 10 s, 8-10 ml remains in the syringe barrel. Record actual grams used and actual retained volume on the data sheet.

3. VISUAL QC — Glossy, uniform yellow with no visible fibre or seed flecks. Coats a spoon in a thin even layer that holds its shape briefly then settles. Holds a fork-drip pattern for a second before merging back into the bulk.

5. FAIL MODES — Runny: retained volume below 8 ml; add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, rest 2 min, retest. Too thick: retained above 10 ml or fork holds its print without blanching; whisk in 5 ml water and retest. Lumpy / flecked: sieve was skipped or blender under-run; discard and re-blend 60 s then sieve. Any fail triggers a full remake; partial fixes are not logged.
