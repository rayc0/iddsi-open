1. PREP
Weigh 200 g blanched bok choy (drained). Add 60 ml warm water. Blend 45 s on high; scrape down; blend 30 s more. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Return purée to blender with 2.0 g thickener per 100 ml as the calibration starting point; record the actual mass used. Blend 20 s, rest 60 s, blend 10 s. Adjust: +0.5 g thickener per 100 ml if it flows through fork tines; -0.3 g per 100 ml if it cracks on the spoon. Re-blend 15 s after any adjustment.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: a 4 mm-wide mound holds its shape when the fork presses to 1 cm above the plate without squashing flat. Pass criterion: clean edges, no liquid bleed.
Spoon tilt: a 4 ml scoop on a 1 cm spoon sits and slowly slides only 1-2 cm before stopping. Pass criterion: mounded, not liquid, not stiff.
Fork drip: mound rests above tines with no drip in 10 s. Pass criterion: no drop falls; gloss only on tine surface.

3. VISUAL QC
Matte sheen, uniform pale-green, no leaf flecks or fibres. Mound holds sharp ridges from fork tines. No puddle forming around the base on the white plate.

5. FAIL MODES
Visible green fibres or seed specks → re-sieve and re-blend. Mound slumps below tine tops within 5 s → add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml, re-blend, re-test. Liquid ring forms at base → too wet, re-blend with extra 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml. Re-make from raw if any two tests still fail after two adjustments.
