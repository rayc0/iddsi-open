PREP
Weigh 150 g peeled ripe banana on the digital scale. Mash thoroughly with a fork against the bowl wall for 60-90 seconds until uniform. Press the mash through the 4 mm sieve using the back of the fork; discard any fibrous strands retained. Return the sieved mash to the bowl and fold briefly to redistribute moisture. Do not blend, do not add liquid, do not add thickener. Confirm final mass on the scale; record.

IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure test: press the tines of the fork into a heaped spoonful of mash until the tines blanch, then withdraw. Pass = the slot fully collapses, the mash squashes easily, and does not recover its shape when the fork is lifted. Spoon tilt test: scoop a teaspoon, tilt to 90 degrees over the bowl. Pass = the specimen sits in a clump and slides off slowly as one mass; no thin liquid drips. Fork drip test: lift a loaded fork and hold level. Pass = no drip runs off within 5 seconds. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC
Specimen holds a soft mound on the spoon with rounded edges, shows a slight sheen from natural moisture, and contains no visible lumps above 4 mm. The colour is uniform pale yellow with no white fibrous threads.

FAIL MODES
(1) Lumps or fibrous strands exceed 4 mm — re-sieve the entire batch; if strands persist, re-mash then re-sieve. (2) Specimen recovers shape after fork press (too firm) — fold in 5 g water per 100 g, re-test. (3) Thin liquid pools or drips — drain on a paper towel 30 seconds, re-test; if still wet, the banana is overripe and a fresh fruit must be used.
