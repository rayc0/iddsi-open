1. PREP
Dice steamed carrot into 15 mm cubes (measure with 100 mm ruler; reject any cube that exceeds 15 mm on any edge). Transfer 200 g of diced carrot to the blender. Add 40 ml water. Pulse 6 seconds total in 2-second bursts; stop and inspect between bursts. No sieve at any point: L6 is sized against the 100 mm ruler, never through a 4 mm mesh. A 4 mm screen caps every particle at 4 mm, which is the L5 ceiling, and this card's target is 15 mm cubes (protocol sections 2 and 3). Keep the 15 mm dice whole; if the pulse broke any piece below 8 mm, re-dice from a firmer batch rather than rescuing it by sieving. Weigh the mash. Record starting weight, blended weight, and any material discarded. Do NOT add thickener; L6 requires visible pieces, not a flow-controlled bolus. Dice retention is acceptable only if pieces meet the fork-pressure test below.

2. IDDSI TESTFork pressure: press fork tines into a representative piece on a flat plate until thumbnail blanches white. Pass: piece squashes and does not return to original shape. Size check: 10 of 10 random pieces must measure ≤15 mm with the ruler. Spoon tilt: not applicable at L6 (5.2 covers L3/L4/L5 only) and is not run, filmed or recorded as L6 evidence. Fork drip: lift fork; nothing should drip through tines. Reject if any piece remains firm or springs back, or if purée is sticky enough to require a 10 ml/10 s syringe flow test.

3. VISUAL QC
Soft 15 mm dice visible, not puréed. Pieces hold shape on fork tines without sliding off. Light orange, moist surface, no separated liquid pooling on the plate. By-eye check only (not a section 5.2 test at L6): sample must sit in a stable mound on the spoon; minor spread acceptable, no liquid pool.

5. FAIL MODES
Pieces exceed 15 mm after dicing: re-dice and re-weigh. Pieces spring back under fork pressure: re-steam 3 minutes, re-test. Excess water pools on plate: drain 30 seconds on paper, re-shoot, do not blot pieces.
