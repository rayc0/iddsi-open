1. PREP
Measure 40 g rolled oats. Add 100 ml hot water (85–95 °C). Stir once, lid on, rest 3 min. Fork-mash 60 s: break the flakes down until every piece is ≤4 mm and no whole flake remains, then stop — further mashing makes paste. Do not blend. Do not sieve the batch: at L5 the flake pieces are the texture, and sieving removes them. Add no thickener at any point on this specimen: none is used and `prep.thickener_g` stays null for it (the schema carries a thickener mass only at L3). If the batch is too wet, correct it by absorbing or draining free water (see FAIL MODES) — thickener binds the free water into a gel and destroys the visibly distinct flakes that make the specimen L5, so the specimen would leave L5 and read as L4. The 4 mm sieve is a GAUGE here, not a step: press a 1 tbsp (15 g) aliquot through it and confirm the whole aliquot passes. Anything held back on the mesh is a flake piece larger than 4 mm and fails the gauge — mash longer and re-gauge on a fresh aliquot, then return the whole aliquot to the batch. Never read a partly-retained gauge as a pass and never discard the retained flakes as waste: L5 requires every piece <= 4 mm. Target yield ≈135 g, with no free water standing on a tilted plate.

2. IDDSI TEST
Applicable at L5: spoon tilt (primary); fork pressure (supporting check only). No fork drip at L5 (5.3 is scoped to L3/L4); no syringe flow at L5 (5.4 is L3 only).
Spoon tilt (5.2, primary): scoop 5 ml onto the spoon, tilt to 45°, hold 10 s. Pass = soft moist flakes hold a loose mound that slumps and slides only slowly, with at most a small amount of free liquid at the spoon edge, and the spoon is not swimming in separate liquid (5.2, L5). A continuous stream off the edge is L4 — fail. A puree that slides off as one cohesive mass with little residue and no visible flakes is L4, not L5 — fail; the fork-pressure check below is what separates the two.
Fork pressure (supporting only): press the fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate until the thumbnail blanches. Pass = the specimen squashes completely with no spring-back, and visibly distinct soft flake pieces remain on the fork and in the plate. A smooth paste with no visible flake pieces fails.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a cohesive clump of visibly distinct soft flake pieces, none larger than 4 mm. On the spoon: no loose water in the well. On the plate: surface matte and damp, flakes evenly dispersed, no lump over 4 mm on the gauge, no pooling liquid at the edges. A surface with no visible flake pieces at all is a smooth paste — L4 — and fails L5; stop and correct per FAIL MODES before shooting.

5. FAIL MODES
Too wet / too thin: free water pools on the plate and the spoon-tilt specimen runs continuously off the edge. Correct by ABSORBING or DRAINING the free water — do NOT add thickener, which gels the free water and hides the flakes that define L5, leaving a smooth gel that reads as L4. Drain the free water off the plate, or fold 10 g dry rolled oats through the batch and rest 2 min. On a re-make, drop the water to 80 ml. Re-test spoon tilt.
Too dry / crumbly: the specimen will not hold a mound on the spoon. Correct by thinning in small steps — 10 ml hot water at a time, maximum 120 ml total. No cold water.
Over-mashed to a smooth paste with no visible flake pieces: fails L5. Discard and restart from oats with fork-mash only, no blender; mash only to ≤4 mm pieces.
Flakes >4 mm remain: mash longer or break with the fork edge, then re-run the 4 mm gauge on a fresh 15 g aliquot and return the pass-through to the batch. Never discard the sieved flakes.
