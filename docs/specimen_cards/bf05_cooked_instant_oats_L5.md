1. PREP
Measure 40 g rolled oats. Add 150 ml hot water. Stir, lid on, rest 2 min. Fork-mash 30 s until flakes are broken and no whole flake remains. Do not blend — blender will destroy the L5 flake texture. Do not sieve — sieving removes the flake pieces that define L5 for oats. Add 0 g thickener at this calibration point; record the actual grams added per 100 ml on the data sheet.

2. IDDSI TEST
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a flat plate. Specimen must squash completely and not spring back when fork is removed — no shape recovery, no flake rebound.
Spoon tilt: scoop 5 ml, tilt spoon 45°. Specimen must hold together and slide off in one mass; zero flow, zero drip.
Fork drip: lift fork with a loaded scoop. Nothing drips through or off the tines.
All three must pass simultaneously.

3. VISUAL QC
On the fork: a cohesive clump of visibly distinct soft flakes, no loose water in the well of the spoon. On the plate: surface matte and damp, flakes evenly dispersed and all under 4 mm, no pooling liquid at the edges.

5. FAIL MODES
Too wet: water pools on the plate, spoon tilt drips — add 0.5 g thickener per 100 ml and re-test.
Too dry or blended: specimen is a smooth paste, fork-pressure leaves a clean dent with no flake texture visible — discard, restart from oats with fork-mash only, no blender.
Whole flakes >4 mm remain: mash longer or break with fork edge, then re-sieve check against the 4 mm grid.
