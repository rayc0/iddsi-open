# Base Food List — 60 Foods, 6 Categories (IDDSI-Open Desk Specimens)

> Status: research preview. Skews to Chinese/Cantonese foods eaten by HK
> elderly, balanced across 6 categories x 10 foods. Each row gives the bought
> form, the per-level prep note, and the allergen tag. Cells marked N/A name
> the reason: those levels are skipped for that food, never forced.
>
> Labels from these foods may be called "physically tested" and MUST be
> described as "single-operator, protocol-following, test-filmed" — never
> implying clinical or multi-rater validation. Research and culinary-education
> use only. Not IDDSI-endorsed.

## How to read a row

- **ID**: BF01–BF60, used in filenames (`bf01-rice_l7_take1_45.jpg`) and in
  the specimen log (`base_food_id`).
- **L7 → L3**: the prep for that level, or N/A with the honest reason.
  Shorthand used across rows: `as-is` = serve the aliquot unchanged;
  `<=15 mm` = cut/break to soft bite-size pieces checked against the 100 mm
  ruler; `<=4 mm` = chop/mash to particles checked with the 4 mm sieve;
  `blend-hold` = blend to a smooth puree that holds shape on a spoon;
  `blend-thin` = blend with liquid (and weighed thickener where noted) to the
  L3 flow window (8–10 ml remaining after 10 s).
- **Allergens**: peanut, sesame, crustacean, fish, egg, milk, soy, gluten, or
  none. Tags reflect the typical bought form; the operator confirms against
  the pack label at portioning time and records extras in the log.

## Thickener prep-parameter table (brand-agnostic)

Two generic thickener types cover the commercial range. Quantities below are
STARTING points for calibration, not fixed recipes: starch-based thickeners
keep thickening on standing, gum-based ones hold steady, and every base food
absorbs liquid differently. The operator fixes working values on the first 5
foods and records the actual weighed grams for every later specimen in
`prep.thickener_g`.

| Thickener type | Starting range per 100 ml liquid, to reach L3 (8–10 ml remaining) | Handling notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starch-based (grain-derived) | 4–8 g per 100 ml | Disperse in a little cold liquid first; stir in; rest 60 s, re-stir, then run the flow test; re-test after 5 min standing — if it has moved out of the 8–10 ml window, adjust and log the final weighed amount |
| Gum-based (xanthan/gum blend) | 1–2 g per 100 ml | Disperses in hot or cold liquid; steadier over time; sift in slowly while stirring to avoid lumps; sieve out any lump before the flow test and log it |

Calibration procedure (first 5 foods, BF01–BF05):

1. Start at the middle of the range (6 g starch-based or 1.5 g gum-based per
   100 ml). Weigh every addition on the digital scale.
2. Run the filmed 10 ml / 10 s flow test. Adjust in 0.5 g (gum) or 1 g
   (starch) steps until two consecutive tests land inside 8–10 ml remaining.
3. Record the winning grams-per-100-ml per thickener type in the session notes
   and use it as the starting point for foods BF06 onward — still verifying
   with the filmed flow test every time, since each food absorbs liquid
   differently.

## Coverage summary — the 267-of-300 accounting

The plan is 60 base foods x 5 levels (L3–L7) = **300 cells**. The capture run
parsed **267 specimen cards** (`docs/specimen_cards/`, 267 `bf*_L*.md` files plus
`INDEX.md`). So the yield is **267 of 300 cells**, and the 33-cell difference is
**not a shortfall and not a loss**: it is the 33 cells this list itself marks
N/A.

**Disposition of the 33: dropped by design — none deferred, none duplicated.**
Every one of the 33 absent cells is a cell the row above already marks N/A with
a stated reason. An N/A cell is skipped, never forced, never substituted and
never re-filed under another level, so nothing was carried forward to a later
session (deferred) and nothing was re-used from another food or level
(duplicated). Every claim below is measured, not assumed.

Reproduce with `python3 tools/audit/audit_cell_accounting.py --markdown`
(report: `tools/audit/cell_accounting.json`; pinned by
`tools/tests/test_cell_accounting.py`).

### What 267 means for the rest of the pack

- **Expected specimen yield is 267, not 300** (N/A cells are skipped, FAIL rows
  are kept as fails). The release notes state the achieved count per level
  against this denominator; the re-test subsample (>= 30 specimens) is drawn
  from achieved passes. 30 of 267 is 11.2%, so the >= 10% coverage rule in
  `docs/CAPTURE_PROTOCOL.md` section 9 is still met with the same floor of 30 —
  the floor is unchanged, only its denominator is now stated correctly.
- `docs/CAPTURE_PROTOCOL.md` section 1 quotes "60 base foods x 5 levels = 300
  specimens" as the *planned cell count*. 267 is the achievable yield once the
  33 N/A cells above are dropped. Both numbers are correct for what they count;
  neither should be quoted without its other half.
- Allergen tags present on every row; the operator confirms against the pack
  label and logs any extra in `prep.notes`.
