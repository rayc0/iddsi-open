## English

### Snap-to-IDDSI: a guided research demo for learning food-texture tests

Snap-to-IDDSI is an open research and culinary-education demo from LinguaLeap Tech Limited. It uses a guided 45° food photo and a short video of the relevant fork or spoon test to estimate how the visible result compares with IDDSI descriptors.

It does **not** determine whether food or drink is safe for any person. A photograph cannot measure properties such as hardness, stickiness, cohesiveness or flow. When the evidence is incomplete or close to a boundary, the demo says **“unclear — perform the physical test.”**

### How to use the demo

1. **Prepare:** Use the texture level prescribed by the person's clinician or care team. Do not use this demo to choose a level.
2. **Capture:** Follow the on-screen guide to take a 45° photo with the stated reference and an 8–12-second clip of the relevant fork-press, fork-drip or spoon-tilt test.
3. **Review:** Read the estimated visual similarity and the cues the demo could or could not observe.
4. **Physically test:** Follow the applicable IDDSI testing method before serving. Repeat the test if the demo reports an unclear or incomplete capture.

For drinks, the separate research flow-test demo measures the visible residue in a correctly prepared 10 mL/10-second syringe test. It does not replace the complete physical test procedure or professional judgment.

### What the result means

The result is an educational comparison with IDDSI descriptors—not a diagnosis, clinical recommendation, certification, compliance record, or **safe/unsafe** verdict. It does not assess swallowing, prescribe a texture, or determine suitability for a resident, patient or other individual.

**Never replace a clinician-prescribed texture or physical IDDSI testing with this demo.** If a result conflicts with the prescribed texture, the physical test, or care-team instructions, stop and follow the clinician/care team's instructions.

### Open research

The project is being developed as an openly documented dataset, model and test-assistant research project. Methods, limitations and failure cases will be published with the research release. No accuracy or validation claim should be inferred before the stated external evaluation is complete.

**Independent-status notice:** This is not the official IDDSI website. IDDSI has not endorsed this demo or its outputs. For the framework and official testing resources, visit [IDDSI](https://www.iddsi.org/).

## 繁體中文

### Snap-to-IDDSI：學習食物質地測試的引導式研究示範

Snap-to-IDDSI 是 LinguaLeap Tech Limited 開發的開放研究及烹調教育示範。使用者按指引拍攝 45° 食物照片，以及相關叉壓、叉滴或匙傾測試的短片；系統會估算畫面所見與 IDDSI 描述的相似程度。

本示範**不能**判斷任何食物或飲品對任何人是否安全。單憑照片不能量度硬度、黏性、凝聚性或流動性。如資料不足或結果接近分界，系統會顯示：**「未能判定——請進行實體測試」**。

### 使用方法

1. **準備：** 只採用臨床人員或照護團隊已為該人士指定的質地級別；不要用本示範選擇級別。
2. **拍攝：** 按畫面指引，以指定參考物拍攝 45° 照片，再拍攝 8–12 秒相關叉壓、叉滴或匙傾測試。
3. **查看：** 閱讀系統估算的視覺相似程度，以及它能夠和未能觀察的線索。
4. **實體測試：** 上餐前依照適用的 IDDSI 測試方法操作。如畫面不完整或系統未能判定，請重新測試。

飲品方面，另一項研究示範可量度正確進行 10 mL／10 秒針筒流動測試後，畫面中可見的剩餘容量；它不能取代完整實體測試程序或專業判斷。

### 結果代表甚麼

結果只是與 IDDSI 描述作教育用途的比較，並非診斷、臨床建議、認證、合規紀錄或**安全／不安全**判定。系統不會評估吞嚥能力、處方食物質地，亦不會判斷食物是否適合任何院友、病人或其他人士。

**本示範絕不可取代臨床人員處方的食物質地或 IDDSI 實體測試。** 如結果與處方質地、實體測試或照護團隊指示不符，應停止使用該結果，並遵從臨床人員／照護團隊的指示。

### 開放研究

本項目正以開放文件形式開發資料集、模型及測試輔助研究工具。研究發佈時將同時公開方法、限制及失敗案例。在完成指定的外部評估前，不應推斷本項目已有任何準確度或驗證結論。
