**Interpretation (regen, vs the published v0 ceiling finding):** the new exact-agreement figure
of 31.0% is still only marginally above the ~20% chance level for 5 classes, and the chance-corrected
kappas are very low (Landis-Koch: κ_unw 0.11 = "slight agreement"; κ_quad 0.28 = "fair agreement"
at best). The cross-tab reveals a NEW systematic pattern the published card did not state:
tier-1 (Qwen3-VL-2B) **systematically under-predicts** difficulty. L3 calls are mostly correct
(11/12), but the interior classes are dragged toward the regular-food end:
- L4 calls (84 total) miss to L6 in 28 cases and to L7 in 69 cases — the dominant confusion
- L5 calls (131 total) miss to L6 in 42 and to L7 in 54
- L6 calls (155 total) collapse into L7 in 111 cases
- L7 calls (126 total) over-pull to L5 (6) and L6 (47)

shape of the noise is now disclosed: judges do not just disagree randomly — they share a
directional bias toward the SAFER texture (the dangerous direction, see H-L7SINK).
