## Verdict

**Strong milestone receipt; moderate KOL launch.** I rate it **9/10 for PR2 evidence, 6/10 for cold-start reach**. It proves Raymond can connect AI, robotics and an operating eldercare-food business, but it is a specialist safety tool—not a mass-audience breakthrough.

Do **not** claim “first IDDSI texture AI” or simply “beats EdUHK 61.7%.” EdUHK already demonstrated single-photo classification at 61.74% and explicitly proposes open-benchmark expansion, video rheology and small-model fine-tuning next. Without access to its CEIV-115 set, a higher score on Raymond’s own data is not comparable. Use: **“open, physically ground-truthed Hong Kong IDDSI image/video benchmark and reproducible baseline.”** [EdUHK paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1829703/full)

Prior art makes photo-only safety claims especially vulnerable: OptiTexture reached 91.96% on L3–L6 only by adding optical sensing, beating vision alone by 22.32 points; ViscoCam already used smartphones for liquid-viscosity assistance. [OptiTexture](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/optitexture-a-low-cost-optical-deep-learning-solution-for-objecti/), [ViscoCam](https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/publications/viscocam-smartphone-based-drink-viscosity-control-assistant-for-d/)

**Who cares:** caregivers want rapid triage; SLPs/dietitians want low dangerous-miss rates and abstention; food makers want batch QC; robotics researchers want a perception module. Photo classification alone cannot reveal hardness, cohesiveness, adhesiveness or temperature. Labels therefore require contemporaneous physical tests, and train/test splits must be by recipe/batch—not correlated frames.

For an unknown founder, expect roughly **500–3,000 HF downloads and 300–2,000 Space users in 90 days**, plus 0–2 niche HK stories; meaningful mainland pickup is unlikely without an institutional co-launch. Independent validation could lift those ranges several-fold.

**The single highest-value change:** secure a **pre-registered, blind, externally held-out HKU/EdUHK benchmark**, reporting exact match *and* liquid/solid false-negative rates. That converts a self-scored demo into a credible scientific receipt.
