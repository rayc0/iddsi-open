  STRENGTH: strong (the two frames are one liquid at two solids contents; nothing the camera can see was varied)

pair 11: food=bf01 level=L3

  STRENGTH: strong — the most valuable pair in the batch, because it shows the applicable tests are not even internally consistent at the L3/L4 boundary before photography enters; it attacks the definition, not the image

REPEATS:
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L3 x 3 — batch-to-batch water:rice ratio and cook-time drift; the dominant source of L3/L4 boundary noise, read as retained ml at t = 10 s. Three independent preps give an SD for the syringe read.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L3 x 2 — same-batch re-measurement, no re-preparation: pure operator and timing repeatability of the 10 s syringe window and plunger release, separating measurement noise from preparation noise.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L4 x 3 — thickener dose and blend-time drift; how far the L4 "holds a soft shape" spoon-tilt reading moves batch to batch.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L4 x 2 — same-batch re-measurement of spoon tilt and fork drip: operator-judgement noise on the two qualitative reads, which are the only L4 evidence.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L5 x 3 — hand-chopping consistency; the real SD of the 4 mm lump-size criterion, the single most operator-dependent measurement in the set.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L5 x 2 — re-photography of one prepared specimen under the standard capture protocol: isolates the camera channel's own noise (lighting, angle, distance, focus) with preparation held at zero. This is the crux — it sets the resolution at which any photo-based level call can be trusted.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L6 x 2 — piece-size spread across the 8–15 mm band; how much of the L5/L6 boundary is preparation drift rather than a real difference.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L6 x 2 — re-photography of one specimen: capture noise at a level where piece count per frame is small, so a single framing decision changes what the model sees.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L7 x 2 — piece-size and firmness spread at ≥15 mm; the noisiest boundary, per pair 9.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L7 x 2 — re-photography of one specimen: capture noise where only 1–3 pieces are in frame, the worst case for a photo-based size call.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L3 x 2 — cross-session re-preparation on a different day: day-to-day drift (rice lot, water hardness, room temperature) beyond within-session batch noise.
  bf01_steamed_white_rice_L5 x 2 — cross-session re-preparation: the between-day component of the noise floor at the level where operator chopping varies most.

WEAKEST_CONTRAST: pair 9 (14 mm vs 16 mm at L6). It fails on both axes at once — the 2 mm size difference is invisible to any camera at the standard capture distance, and the fork-pressure read barely separates 14 mm from 16 mm pieces of the same soft rice, so there is no dependable measurement difference either. A contrast with neither a visible nor a measurable difference measures nothing; if its noise is read as signal it will manufacture a false "photograph lacks the information" result. Run it only as a negative control.

VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT — three independent reasons. (1) This is a design; no measurements exist yet, so no hypothesis has been tested.
