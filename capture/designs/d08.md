WEAKEST_CONTRAST: pair 12 (L3 vs L7) — the two sides are separated by five levels and are obvious to any viewer, so it cannot reveal anything about whether the defining information is visible; it earns its place only as a constant-output check. Among the informative pairs, pair 11 is next weakest because its 8 vs 15 mm difference is the one size gap a photograph can genuinely resolve.

VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT — this design is powered to settle the two boundaries the team named (L3/L4 viscosity and L4/L5 particle size) but the claim as stated spans all four, and L5/L6 and L6/L7 are size-defined and therefore partly photographable, so no single verdict can hold before the measurements are taken; the boundary-level asymmetry is the finding the contrasts are built to expose.
