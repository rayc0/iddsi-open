WEAKEST_CONTRAST: pair 6 (bf11 L5, round vs flat 4 mm dice). Both sides are L5 by every defining test, and lump aspect ratio is one of the few attributes a plan-view photograph partly carries, so the pair is both visible and label-neutral — no model outcome is interpretable in either direction. It earns its place only as a control that the set is not trivially separable.

VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT. The set is well aimed at the two boundaries the team named — pairs 1-4 isolate viscosity, temperature, mesh retention and gloss, none of which can be recovered from a 2-D image, and pairs 5, 7, 10 and 12 do the same for yield stress — but three gaps stop it settling the claim. (a) sealed.txt exposes only bf11, so nothing here generalises beyond steamed fish; a photo-information result on one base food is a single data point, not a finding. (b) There is no arm that varies the photograph while holding the physics constant (lighting, angle, sauce pooling, plate colour), so a model failure cannot be attributed to information absent from the scene rather than information absent from the image. (c) One repeat per specimen gives a noise floor whose interval is wide enough to swallow the L3/L4 margin it is meant to protect. To flip to SUPPORTED: add the photographic-control arm, at least three repeats per specimen at L3 and L5, and at least three further base foods spanning colour and gloss extremes.
