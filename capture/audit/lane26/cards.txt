### bf26_steamed_carrot_L3  (level L3)
PREP:
Weigh 200 g steamed carrot, soft enough to crush with a fork. Add 120 ml cool water. Blend 45-60 s until completely smooth, no visible flecks. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard anything that will not push through and note the loss. Measure the sieved volume in ml. Stir in thickener at the calibration starting point of 1.5 g per 100 ml of puree, then rest 2 min and stir again. The operator records the actual grams used and the final ml. The flow should be a thick pour that drops in a continuous ribbon from the spoon. Adjust with small extra thickener (0.1-0.2 g) or water (5 ml) only if the IDDSI test fails, and re-record.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_drip, flow_ml_remaining):
Fork drip test: lift the fork; the puree drips slowly in a thick continuous ribbon, not in separate drops. Spoon tilt: the specimen holds its shape on a tilted spoon, then slides off as one lump when the spoon is nearly vertical. Fork pressure: pressing the tines leaves a clear imprint but no liquid pools around it. Syringe flow: fill to the 10 ml mark, start the timer for 10 s; pass if 8-10 ml remains in the syringe.

VISUAL QC:
Surface looks glossy and even, no orange specks, no bubbles. Coats the back of the spoon in a uniform layer about 2-3 mm thick. Holds a soft peak that slowly flattens.

FAIL MODES:
Runny like a drink: too little thickener, add 0.2 g per 100 ml and re-test. Holds shape too firmly on spoon tilt and blocks the syringe: over-thickened, whisk in 5 ml water and re-test. Visible fibre or specks catching on the sieve: blend a further 20 s and re-sieve. Any fail means remix from the recorded quantities; do not patch the existing specimen.

### bf58_tomato_egg_stirfry_L3  (level L3)
PREP:
Weigh 250 g tomato egg stir-fry on the digital scale. Add 80 ml warm water, blend 45-60 s until smooth. Press through the 4 mm sieve; discard any residue. Measure volume of sieved liquid. Add thickener at 2.5 g per 100 ml as the calibration starting point while stirring briskly; let stand 60 s, stir again. If it does not drip slowly through fork tines, add thickener in 0.5 g per 100 ml increments up to a ceiling of 4.0 g per 100 ml. Record the actual grams per 100 ml used. Target: pours readily, drips slowly in dollops from the fork.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_drip, flow_ml_remaining):
Fork drip: holds its shape on the fork, then drips slowly through the tines in dollops, not a continuous stream. Spoon tilt: a level spoon tilts 30-45 degrees before the specimen flows off. Fork press: thumbnail blanches white briefly under light pressure on the spoon mound. Flow test: fill the 10 ml syringe, release; 8-10 ml remain in the syringe after 10 s. All four must pass.

VISUAL QC:
Coating completely covers the back of the spoon with no gaps. A ribbon laid on a flat plate settles to a smooth mound within 2-3 s without running. Color is uniform pale orange-yellow with no visible tomato skin flecks or egg curds.

FAIL MODES:
Too runny, drops through sieve fast or flow test ends below 8 ml: re-blend and add 0.5 g per 100 ml thickener, retest. Too thick, mounds on the fork or flow test ends above 10 ml: whisk in 10 ml warm water, retest. Lumpy from egg curds blocking the sieve: re-blend a further 30 s and re-sieve; if a second pass still blocks, note sieve-impossible and switch to a coarser capture.

### bf26_steamed_carrot_L4  (level L4)
PREP:
Weigh 200 g steamed carrot. Add 60 ml hot water, blend 45 s on high until smooth. Pass through the 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon; discard fibrous residue. Return puree to blender, add 1.5 g thickener per 100 ml puree, blend 15 s. Rest 60 s, stir, retest. If too soft to mound on the spoon, add 0.5 g/100 ml increments, re-blend 10 s, rest 30 s, until the specimen sits above fork tines. Record actual final thickener mass g/100 ml on the data card.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_pressure, spoon_tilt, fork_drip):
Fork drip: turn fork tines-down; puree must sit as a cohesive mound above the tines, not drip through or fall. Fork pressure: press fork tines into the puree on a plate; puree should yield to pressure, flatten and smear under the fork, not return to original shape, and not flow through the gaps. Spoon tilt: scoop puree and tilt spoon; specimen must hold shape, slide off as a single mass, not run. L4 does not require the syringe flow test.

VISUAL QC:
Smooth, uniform orange surface with no fibre specks, no separate liquid pooling at the edges of the mound. The mound keeps clean edges where the fork or spoon lifted it.

FAIL MODES:
(a) Puree drips through fork tines or runs on spoon tilt — puree is too thin, too much water or not enough thickener, or insufficient rest time; remake and re-thicken in 0.5 g/100 ml steps. (b) Puree cracks or splits when pressed with fork — too thick, over-thickened or sieved too dry, re-blend with 5-10 ml hot water and retest. (c) Visible fibre specks or pale streaks — sieve pass incomplete, re-sieve and discard residue before thickener step.

### bf60_lor_mai_gai_glutinous_ri_L4  (level L4)
PREP:
Weigh 300 g cooked lor mai gai (debone, strip chicken). Add to blender with 120 ml warm stock (chicken or rice cooking water). Blend 45–60 s on high until homogeneous. Sieve once through 4 mm sieve; discard retained solids, weigh back to confirm ≥ 250 g of puree. Return puree to blender; thicken by adding 1.0 g thickener per 100 ml puree, blend 15 s, rest 60 s. Record actual grams used. If after rest the puree flows through fork tines, add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml and re-blend; record. Target final mass 250–300 g, no standing liquid.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_pressure, spoon_tilt, fork_drip):
Fork pressure test: mound puree on fork; press thumbnail until blanching. Surface should flatten and stay compressed — no crumbs, no run-through. Fork drip test: lift fork 15 cm; puree must sit as a mound above tines, zero drip in 10 s. Spoon tilt test: scoop puree, tilt spoon 90°; specimen holds shape, slides off as one lump or not at all. No separate thin liquid may pool on plate after 60 s.

VISUAL QC:
Fork: glossy mound capping all tines, clean spaces between prongs blocked by puree. Spoon: drops cleanly as a soft lump leaving spoon near-empty. Surface: matte-sheen, even colour, no rice grains or chicken fibres visible, no watery halo at plate edge.

FAIL MODES:
Puree drips through tines or runs on tilt: too thin — add 0.3 g thickener per 100 ml, re-blend, retest. Visible rice grains, chicken fibre, or grainy paste after sieving: too coarse — re-blend 20 s, re-sieve, retest. Watery halo pools around mound on plate: phase separation — blend 10 s longer and rest 90 s before re-test. If still failing, re-make from 300 g with stock reduced to 100 ml.

### bf26_steamed_carrot_L5  (level L5)
PREP:
Weigh 200 g steamed carrot. Mash with a fork on a plate until no lumps >4 mm remain. Press through a 4 mm sieve using the back of a spoon to catch any oversize; discard residue. Weigh mash. Stir in 10 ml water per 100 g mash to moisten; do not add so much that liquid pools. Do not blend — a blender reduces carrot to a smooth puree and loses the L5 particle character. No thickener required for this level; if the mash looks dry and crumbly, dust in up to 0.5 g thickener per 100 g and re-test.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_pressure, spoon_tilt):
Fork pressure: press fork tines into the specimen on a plate until the tines blanch; the mash must squash, leave a clear dent, and NOT spring back. Spoon tilt: tilt a spoonful 45°; the mass must sit in a pile, cohesive, sliding slowly as a single lump rather than flowing off. Fork drip: hold a forkful above the plate for 5 s; nothing should drip or run between the tines. All three must pass.

VISUAL QC:
On the fork: discrete, soft orange flecks, none longer than a tine-width (~4 mm), surface glistens but no shine of free liquid. On the spoon: holds the scoop shape, mound stays together when plate is tapped.

FAIL MODES:
Mash is wet and pools liquid on the plate → too much water added; remix drier. Mash is smooth/pureed with no visible flecks → over-blended or over-sieved; re-mash and stop at 4 mm. Particles exceed 4 mm or spring back under fork → insufficient mashing/sieving; re-pass through sieve and re-test.

### bf28_steamed_bitter_melon_L6  (level L6)
PREP:
Start with 200 g steamed bitter melon flesh, seeds and pith removed. Slice into pieces no larger than 15 mm in any dimension; measure against the 100 mm ruler. Do not blend and do not sieve. No thickener, no added water or liquid. Mild steam-softening only; the pieces must hold their own shape on the fork.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_pressure, spoon_tilt):
Fork-pressure test: press the fork tines onto one 15 mm piece on a flat plate until thumbnail blanches white. Pass criterion: the piece squashes and does not recover its shape when the fork is lifted. Repeat on three pieces from different parts of the batch. Spoon-tilt test: heap 5-6 pieces on a spoon and tilt to a steep angle. Pass criterion: pieces stay on the spoon without sliding or dripping liquid. Fork-drip test: lift a forkful and hold level for 5 s; pass if no liquid runs or drips. Confirm with ruler that each tested piece measures ≤15 mm.

VISUAL QC:
Pieces on fork should look moist but not wet, with visible ridges from the melon interior intact. Edges are soft and rounded, not torn or fibrous. Colour is translucent olive-green; no free liquid pools on the plate. A thumbnail press leaves a clean, persistent white mark.

FAIL MODES:
Pieces larger than 15 mm: re-cut smaller, do not re-steam. Pieces rubbery or springing back under thumbnail: re-steam 2-3 minutes and re-test. Pieces breaking to puree or releasing free water: discard batch, reduce steam time on next batch.

### bf31_banana_L7  (level L7)
PREP:
Peel one ripe banana per the per-level note. Cut the peeled banana in half crosswise. Reserve one half as the reference specimen; set the other half aside. Do not blend, do not sieve, do not add thickener, do not add water. Place one half on a plate. Record the specimen mass in grams on the capture sheet; it is what it is, and that mass is the reference value. This specimen is a whole food as normally served, so there is no calibration to perform and no grams-per-100 ml to record.

IDDSI TEST (applicable: fork_pressure, spoon_tilt):
Fork pressure: press the tines fully into the banana half until the fork stops against the plate. The piece must break, cleave, or squish apart with little effort, leaving food clearly crushed, changed shape, and not returning to its original form when the fork is lifted. Pass = clear deformation with no recovery. Spoon tilt: not required at L7; skip. Fork drip: not required at L7; skip. Syringe flow test: not required at L7; skip.

VISUAL QC:
Whole peeled banana half on plate, pale cream to pale yellow flesh, no brown oxidation streaks. Skin-on is wrong; blended paste is wrong; sliced coins are wrong.

FAIL MODES:
Served unpeeled with skin on. Cut into coins or mash beyond the L7 piece. Left out long enough to brown and harden, then it resists the fork and reads as a different texture class. Any failure: re-make from a fresh banana, re-shoot the full set, do not patch a single frame.
