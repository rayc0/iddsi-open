You are auditing physical specimen cards for a dysphagia texture study.
An operator will cook these exactly as written, at a bench, for ~9 hours. A wrong
card therefore becomes a wrong GROUND-TRUTH label that silently trains a model
incorrectly. Your job is to catch errors BEFORE a human burns a day on them.

The file cards.txt contains several specimen cards. Each has a target IDDSI level
(L3-L7), a PREP procedure, the IDDSI TEST criteria, a VISUAL QC description, and
FAIL MODES.

IDDSI definitions you are checking against:
  L3 liquidised / smooth   -- no lumps at all, flows freely from the spoon.
  L4 thickly liquidised    -- no lumps, but markedly thicker, holds a soft shape.
  L5 minced and moist      -- lumps ~4 mm, mashable with a fork, holds together
                              loosely, moist.
  L6 soft bite-sized       -- lumps ~8-15 mm, fork needs real effort, cut pieces.
  L7 regular               -- pieces >=15 mm that require real chewing.

Applicable tests, by level:
  L3 : fork drip, plus the 10 ml syringe flow test (8-10 ml left at t=10 s)
  L4 : fork pressure, spoon tilt, fork drip
  L5 : fork pressure, spoon tilt
  L6 : fork pressure, spoon tilt
  L7 : fork pressure, spoon tilt

FOR EACH CARD report, using the specimen_id as the key:

  ACHIEVABLE: yes | no | uncertain -- would following this PREP actually produce
    the stated level, as written? Quote the specific step that decides it.
  TEST_SET_CORRECT: yes | no -- are the applicable tests the right ones for this
    level, and does the card avoid asserting tests that do not apply at this level
    (for example a 10 ml syringe flow test on an L5, or a "no lumps" criterion on
    an L5 or above)?
  PARAMETERS_PRESENT: yes | no -- list any measurement the card asks for but does
    not pin down (grams, ml, seconds, sieve mm, particle size) that would let two
    operators produce materially different results.
  CONTRADICTIONS: <any internal conflict: the prep implies a different level than
    the target, the QC describes a texture the target level should not have, the
    fail-mode remedy would push it into an adjacent level, or so on>
  SINGLE_WORST_RISK: <the one sentence an operator would most likely get wrong>

Be concrete and quote. A card that is fine must be reported as fine -- do not
manufacture findings. Do NOT rewrite the card; report the defect and the minimal
correction.

Output format, no preamble:
CARDS: one block per card, exactly:
  <specimen_id> | ACHIEVABLE=<yes|no|uncertain> | TEST_SET_CORRECT=<yes|no> |
    PARAMETERS_PRESENT=<yes|no>
    CONTRADICTIONS: <none, or the conflict quoted>
    SINGLE_WORST_RISK: <one sentence>
SUMMARY: <n cards>, <n not achievable>, <n wrong test set>, <n missing parameters>
NEEDS_HUMAN_JUDGEMENT: <cards where you are genuinely uncertain, and why>
VERDICT: SUPPORTED | REFUTED | INSUFFICIENT
