# IDDSI-Open — growth plan: genuine downloads and citations

**Lane:** `hf04_growth_plan` · **Date:** 2026-10-12 · **Status:** plan, not yet executed.
Every number cited here is measured by me and traced in
[`EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md`](EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md). Anything I could not measure is
labelled `UNVERIFIED-HERE` and says why.

---

## 0. The frame: what "downloads" can even mean for this release

The 2026-09-07 release shape is locked and it is the right call: **code + eval
results + the flow-test grader ship; model weights and the harvested dataset are
withheld.** That decision removes the two channels most repos use to grow:

- There are **no weights** to download, so HF model-repo download counts will
  never move.
- There is **no dataset** to download, for licence reasons
(587 unique events, exactly 2 clean; 592 rows with unverified
open-images/openverse licences).

But one asset in this repo is **not** covered by either withholding, and it is
the asset that works:

> `flowtest/` — a deterministic OpenCV grader for the 10 mL / 10 s IDDSI Flow
> Test. No learned model, no GPU, no network, two dependencies
> (`numpy`, `opencv-python-headless`).

And its benchmark is **synthetic** — generated by `flowtest/synthetic.py` from
a documented 108-combo grid with seed `1000 + i`. There is no third-party image
in it, therefore **no licence exposure**. I verified this: the bench contains no
harvested media, and `flowtest/flowtest/bench/results_n100/` is the only
artifact it produces.

So the growth strategy is not "promote the model". It is:

> **Make the one licence-clean, working, pip-installable component of this
> release trivially obtainable, and attach the negative finding to it as the
> citable payload.**

Downloads come from **PyPI** (counted, genuine, and completely unstarted — all
four candidate names returned 404 today). Citations come from the **negative
finding**, not from the model, because the model is explicitly dead at its
gates and nobody cites a dead capability. The one-line positioning that makes
both work at once:

> *"The first open-source smartphone grader for the 10 mL IDDSI Flow Test — plus
> the measurement that shows why you cannot get IDDSI texture levels from a
> photograph."*

The second half is the citation engine. The first half is the download engine.
Neither requires a weight file, and neither is a misrepresentation.

Ranked by (genuine downloads + citations expected) × (probability of execution)
÷ (effort), with dependencies respected. "Why now" is the reason it sits at
this rank rather than a lower one.

| # | Channel | Concrete artefact | Why this rank |
|---|---|---|---|

| **7** | HF model card SEO | Front-matter upgrade (`library_name`, `base_model`, `arxiv`, `pretty_name`) + a "what you can actually download" section | Makes the repo appear in the right task pages *and* converts the "no weights" disappointment into a PyPI/ Space click. |

## 2. Channel detail

### #1 — Paper integrity *(gates #8, #9, #10)*

**Artefact:** `paper/paper.md` rewritten to carry the v0.2→v0.7 arc, plus
`growth/artefacts/METRIC_DICTIONARY.md` fixing one name per quantity.

**One-line pitch:** *"A negative result with a working tool attached: photo
classification for IDDSI texture is dead at its own gates, and here is the
measurement that shows why — plus a grader that does work."*

**Why it is #1.** `paper/paper.md` (486 lines, dated 7 September 2026) reports
weighted kappa 0.42, macro-F1 0.358, 33% dangerous under-classification and
31.0% inter-judge agreement on n=575. `hf/model_README.md` (441 lines) has since
published v0.2, v0.3, v0.5, v0.6 and v0.7 — including the finding that the gate
outcome is a property of the yardstick rather than the model, and that
cross-family consensus (74.7%) beats within-family agreement (30.9%). **None of
that is in the paper.** arXiv postings cannot be withdrawn, so this ordering is
not negotiable.

**What must be reconciled first** — the same phrase resolves to three different
numbers today (see `EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md` §G):

| Source | "under-classification, all-N" |
|---|---|
| `paper/paper.md` §6/§7 | 33% |
| `hf/model_README.md` v0.7 table | 0.093 – 0.130 |
| `results/v07_release/*_metrics.json` | 0.0278 – 0.0463 |

The JSON column is `trainer/eval/metrics.py:77`,
`((raw_predictions < labels) & accepted).mean()` — the exact defect the card
names **H-ABSTBLIND** in its own v0.3 section. The card is right to publish the
corrected figure; the paper must say which quantity it uses and why, in one
sentence, once.

**Also required:** wire in the orphaned `paper/sections/related_work.md`
(131 lines) and `paper/sections/limitations.md` (157 lines), which `build.sh`
never includes, and correct the flow-grader sentence in §3.4/§6/§7 to the
reproducible form in `EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md` §E.

### #2 — PyPI *(the only real download channel)*

**Artefact:** `iddsi-flowtest` 0.1.0 published from `flowtest/pyproject.toml`.

**One-line pitch:** *"`pip install iddsi-flowtest` — measure the residual volume
in a filmed 10 mL / 10 s IDDSI Flow Test with deterministic OpenCV, no ML and no
GPU."*

**Evidence it is ready and untouched:** `pyproject.toml` already declares the
name, version, deps and `requires-python >=3.10`; `iddsi-flowtest`,
`iddsi-open`, `iddsi-open-flowtest` and `iddsi-flowtest-grader` all return 404
on PyPI today. The test suites pass (37/37 trainer, 22/22 ops, 3/4 tools,
58/60 flowtest). Nothing has been published.

**Do not** bump the version past 0.1.0 and do not add a `description` that
mentions swallowing safety. `flowtest/README.md` already carries the correct
framing; the PyPI long description should be that file verbatim.

### #3 — Fix the red test *(blocks #2)*

**Artefact:** a green `pytest` in `flowtest/`, plus a decision recorded in the
PR on which side is wrong.

**One-line pitch:** n/a — this is hygiene.

**The measurement:** 60 items → 58 passed, 1 skipped, 1 failed. The failure is
`tests/test_w9_fixes.py::test_fiducial_optional_mode_grades_uncalibrated`,
which asserts `status == "graded"` in fiducial-optional mode and gets
`status == "abstain"` with `abstention_reasons =
['bubbles_or_lumps_detected', 'uncalibrated_scale_mode']`,
`estimated_residual_ml = 9.723`, `boundary_distance_ml = 1.723`.

Two defensible resolutions, and the choice matters for the public claim:

- **(a) The bubble heuristic is over-firing on the synthetic fixture.** Fix the
  detector, keep the documented behaviour. Preferred if the fixture is meant to
  be a clean uncalibrated case.
- **(b) The documentation over-promises.** `flowtest/README.md` line 37 says
  fiducial-optional mode "lets the grader proceed without the card" and that
  "other quality checks still apply and can still abstain" — which is what the
  code does. If (b), fix the *test's* expectation, not the code, and do **not**
  soften the abstention.

Whichever is chosen: the abstention must not be removed, only made consistent
with the documentation. A first-time user who runs `pip install
iddsi-flowtest`, tries the documented flag and gets an unexplained abstention
is a user lost permanently.

### #4 — HF dataset: the synthetic flow-test bench *(licence-clean downloads)*

**Artefact:** a dataset repo, card at
`growth/artefacts/hf_dataset_card.md`, containing the generated bench media +
`bench_summary.json` + the exact generation command.

**One-line pitch:** *"A 100-case synthetic stress bench for IDDSI Flow-Test
video grading — regenerate it deterministically in one command, no licence
questions, no harvested media."*

**Why this and not the harvested dataset:** the harvested corpus cannot ship
(587 events, 2 clean). The bench can, because every frame is drawn by
`flowtest/synthetic.py` over a documented grid. That makes it the only
IDDSI-related *data* this project can legitimately offer — and a benchmark
nobody else has, since arXiv metadata search for "IDDSI" returns 0 results.

**Non-negotiable card content:** the scope string from
`bench_summary.json` — *"synthetic pipeline-development benchmark only; not
real-video validation and not a safety benchmark"* — must appear above the fold,
and the MAE must be quoted as **graded-only over 16 of 100 cases** with the
all-estimates figure (0.6251 mL in my repro) beside it. `UNVERIFIED-HERE`: the
exact HF dataset front-matter keys (`task_categories`, `task_ids`) could not be
checked because huggingface.co is unreachable from this sandbox.

### #5 — GitHub hygiene *(measured empty; cheapest stars in the plan)*

**Artefact:** `growth/artefacts/github_repo_settings.md` — copy-paste
description, homepage, 12 topics; plus `growth/artefacts/CITATION.cff`.

**One-line pitch:** n/a — discovery surface.

**Measured today:** `description = None`, `homepage = None`, `topics = []`,
0 stars / 0 forks, `license.spdx_id = NOASSERTION`, `has_pages = False`,
`README.md` has one external link and zero badges, no `.github/` directory at
all. A repo with an empty description and no topics is invisible to GitHub
search and to every topic-browse page. This is the highest
return-per-minute item in the plan.

The `NOASSERTION` licence is worth a look: the repo *does* contain a
10 374-byte Apache-2.0 `LICENSE`, so this is most likely stale detection rather
than a real problem — but "GitHub says Other" is what a prospective user sees
before they read anything, so re-check it after the next push and, if it
persists, add an SPDX identifier line at the top of `LICENSE`.

### #6 — Demo Space *(converts attention into HF likes)*

**Artefact:** `growth/artefacts/space/` — a Gradio app plus its Space README.

**One-line pitch:** *"Film a 10 mL / 10 s syringe Flow Test with your phone; get
the residual volume in mL, the L0–L4 band, and — more often — the exact reason
the grader refused."*

**Why a Space and not a web app:** HF Spaces have their own usage and like
counts, they appear in Spaces category browse pages, and a Space can be linked
from the model card, the dataset card and the paper. `UNVERIFIED-HERE`: HF
trending is generally understood to be driven by likes and downloads in a recent
window, but I could not verify the weighting because huggingface.co is
unreachable from this sandbox — treat the trending claim as unconfirmed and
measure the effect rather than assuming it.

**The demo's real selling point is the abstention.** On the shipped bench the
grader abstains on 84 of 100 synthetic stress cases and grades 16. A demo that
shows *"abstain — bubbles_or_lumps_detected, re-film without foam"* is more
persuasive to a dietitian than any accuracy number, because it is the behaviour
they need from a tool they cannot trust. Build the abstention reasons into the
primary output, not a debug panel.

**Also required:** a "bring your own clip" path, so a real dietitian can test
their own phone footage — with the honest caveat that no real-video validation
has been performed (`flowtest/README.md` §"Honest limitations").

### #7 — HF model-card SEO

**Artefact:** `growth/artefacts/hf_model_card_frontmatter.md`.

**One-line pitch:** n/a — discovery surface.

`hf/model_README.md`'s YAML currently carries `language`, `pipeline_tag:
image-classification`, four tags and `license: apache-2.0`. Missing:
`library_name`, `base_model`, `pretty_name`, `arxiv`, `datasets`. The `arxiv`
key is the single highest-value addition once #8 exists — it is what links the
repo into HF Papers.

**One judgement call to make deliberately.** `pipeline_tag:
image-classification` puts this repo on the image-classification task page,
where visitors expect weights. The card does say weights are withheld, and it
says so early. My recommendation: **keep the tag** (removing it costs discovery
and the repo genuinely is an image-classification research artefact) but add a
"What you can actually download today" block in the first screenful, pointing at
the PyPI package, the synthetic-bench dataset and the Space. That converts the
one thing a visitor will be disappointed by into three clicks that count.

### #8 — arXiv + HF Papers *(the citation surface)*

**Artefact:** a cs.CV + cs.LG preprint of the regenerated paper.

**One-line pitch:** *"Why IDDSI texture levels cannot be read off a photograph —
a pre-registered negative result, three label sets, and an AUGRC-based
selective-risk analysis that abstention cannot game."*

**Evidence for the niche:** `all:"IDDSI"` → 0 results,
`all:"International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative"` → 0,
`abs:"food texture" AND abs:"classification"` → 0, `abs:"dysphagia"` → 15
(measured 2026-10-12). There is no arXiv competition for this topic, which also
means arXiv visibility alone will not reach the citing audience — hence #10.

**Papers with Code is not a separate step.** `paperswithcode.com` 302-redirects
to `huggingface.co/papers/trending` (measured today). HF Papers ingests arXiv
submissions, so a single arXiv posting plus the `arxiv:` key in the model card
(#7) is the whole linkage. Do not spend effort on a PwC account.

**Category choice:** primary `cs.CV`, cross-list `cs.LG`; add `eess.IV` only if
the flow-grader photogrammetry is made a co-equal contribution rather than a
supporting one.

### #9 — DOI and citation plumbing

**Artefact:** `growth/artefacts/CITATION.cff` + `CITATION.bib`, and a Zenodo
release DOI wired into the README's citation block.

**One-line pitch:** n/a — plumbing.

A citation needs a resolvable, versioned identifier. `CITATION.cff` is what
makes GitHub render "Cite this repository", and a Zenodo–GitHub connected
release mints a DOI per tag. Both are ~30 minutes of work and both are
prerequisites for #10 being able to say "cite this" with a straight face.

### #10 — Communities *(the highest yield per reader, but last)*

**Artefact:** `growth/artefacts/community_outreach.md` — four audiences, four
one-line pitches, four specific venues, and an explicit list of sentences that
must never be used.

**One-line pitches:**

| Audience | Pitch | Venue |
|---|---|---|
| Dysphagia dietitians / SLPs | *"A free tool that reads the syringe Flow Test off your phone video — and tells you when the film is not good enough to read."* | IDDSI's own channels, ESSD, national SLP/dietetic associations, r/SLP |
| Food science / texture measurement | *"An open, deterministic reimplementation of the IDDSI Flow Test with a reproducible 100-case synthetic stress bench."* | Journal of Texture Studies / IFT circles, r/foodscience |
| Culinary education | *"Show students what 'Level 4 Minced & Moist' actually looks like under the fork — with the scale card in frame."* | culinary-school lecturers, hospitality-teaching networks |
| Eldercare / care-home QA | *"A phone-video check a kitchen can run in 20 seconds that refuses to answer when the clip is unclear."* | care-provider QA forums, catering standards bodies |

**Why last:** every one of these posts points at an install. If #2/#3 are not
done, the post's first reader hits a broken package and the community's first
impression of the project is permanent. Sequence matters more than volume here.

**The pitch discipline.** The model is dead at its gates and the weights are
withheld. Leading with the classifier would be leading with the thing the
project itself killed. Lead with the grader and the negative finding; mention
the withheld weights as evidence of rigour, not as an apology. Never write
"validated", "clinically tested", "safe", "IDDSI-approved", or any phrasing that
implies the tool decides whether food may be served.

## 3. What not to do

These are all cheap, all tempting, and all disqualifying for this project.

1. **No fake or inflated downloads.** No self-download loops, no bot traffic, no
   "download our model" campaigns for a repo with no weights. The download
   number that matters is a real `pip install`.
2. **No HF trending gaming.** Trending is driven by likes and downloads; there
   is nothing legitimate to download here and no reason to manufacture likes.
   `UNVERIFIED-HERE` on the exact weighting — which is itself the reason not to
   optimise against it.
3. **Do not re-type the flow MAE as an accuracy claim.** "MAE 0.065 ml on a
   100-video bench" is graded-only over 16 of 100 synthetic cases and does not
   reproduce across OpenCV builds (0.0651 → 0.0491, and n-with-estimate 97 →
   100, in my repro). The honest sentence is in `EVIDENCE_BASELINE.md` §E.
4. **Do not weaken a gate, hazard entry or disclaimer to make a better headline.**
   Specifically: the withheld weights stay withheld, the 84/100 abstention rate
   stays published, and the H-ABSTBLIND disclosure stays in the card. The v0.6
   finding — that the gate outcome depends on the yardstick — is the most
   citable thing in the project precisely *because* it was published against
   interest.
5. **Do not describe this as a swallowing-safety classifier.** It is not one,
   the release says so repeatedly, and the regulatory section of the paper
   (§5) explains exactly why that boundary is engineered rather than assumed.
6. **Do not post the arXiv preprint before #1 is done.** It cannot be withdrawn.
